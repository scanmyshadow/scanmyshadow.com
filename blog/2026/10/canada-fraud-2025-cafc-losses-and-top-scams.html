Canada Fraud in 2025: C$704 Million Reported Lost, Investment Fraud Leads at C$351 Million

Scan My Shadow · October 7, 2026 · Figures checked against the sources below in October 2026
Scam StatisticsDataCanadaCAFC

TL;DR: The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre received over 112,000 fraud reports in 2025 with more than C$704 million in reported losses, up from C$638 million in 2024 (2025 figures published February 2026). Investment fraud was the costliest at C$351 million, ahead of spear phishing (C$67.9 million), romance (C$63.3 million) and job scams (C$50.6 million). The CAFC says only 5 to 10 per cent of fraud is reported.

Reported losses by year

The CAFC collects reports from victims across Canada. Its figures are reports, not a measure of all fraud, and the CAFC and the Competition Bureau say only 5 to 10 per cent of fraud connected to Canadian victims is reported.

Canada's reported losses rose every year, on either 2023 figure cited above (RCMP: C$578 million; CAFC presentation: C$572 million). The Competition Bureau says they now exceed C$2.4 billion for 2022 to 2025 combined. Per resident, 2025 losses work out to about C$17 using Statistics Canada's preliminary April 2026 population of 41,417,056 (our calculation).

The top 10 frauds by money lost

Investment fraud accounted for about half of all reported losses (C$351 million of C$704 million, our calculation). Of the four largest categories by loss, all rose between 2024 and 2025:

How we compiled this data

Loss and report figures are from the CAFC's "Top 10 frauds in 2025" page (February 2026), its Fraud Prevention Month 2025 page for 2024 data, the CAFC 2021 and 2022 annual reports and Competition Bureau releases. Population is Statistics Canada's preliminary April 2026 estimate. Percentage changes and the per-resident figure are our arithmetic on those values.

The CAFC publishes 2025 reports only as "over 112,000" and the 2022 and 2023 report counts were not available to us in an official source we could confirm, so those cells are blank. The 2025 age, payment-method and contact-method tables were not published in a form we could verify, so we do not include them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much did Canadians lose to fraud in 2025?

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre recorded more than C$704 million in reported losses from over 112,000 reports in 2025, up from over C$638 million in 2024.

What is the most costly scam in Canada?

Investment fraud, at C$351 million in reported losses in 2025, followed by spear phishing (C$67.9 million), romance (C$63.3 million) and job scams (C$50.6 million).

Is the real figure higher than C$704 million?

Almost certainly. The CAFC and the Competition Bureau say only 5 to 10 per cent of fraud is reported.

Where do I report fraud in Canada?

Report to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre online or by phone and to your local police. Contact your bank and credit bureaus too if money or identity details were taken.
