The Largest Data Breaches Loaded Into Have I Been Pwned, 2024 to 2026

By Siddharth Shankar · October 12, 2026 · Figures checked against the sources below in October 2026
Data BreachesDataStatistics

TL;DR: Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) lists 20 breaches dated 2024 to 2026 with more than 31 million accounts each, ranging from 31.1 million (Internet Archive) to an aggregated credential-stuffing dataset of 2 billion. Several of the largest are stealer-log and credential-list collections rather than a single company hack. This list is a snapshot from HIBP’s public list and may not be exhaustive.

Breach counts are easy to misread: an “account” in HIBP is a unique email address in a dataset, the same person can appear in many datasets, and some entries are compilations of older leaks. This page lists the large ones with the figures HIBP publishes, and says plainly what it does not show.

Why the numbers are not “people affected”

HIBP counts accounts (email addresses) in each dataset. One person can appear in several breaches, and stealer-log and credential-list collections can repackage data from many sources. So the total of the rows in the table is not a count of affected people, and we do not sum it.

The data types differ by breach. For example, HIBP lists the 1win breach as including dates of birth, email addresses, geographic locations, IP addresses, passwords and phone numbers, and the Addi breach (March 2026) as including government-issued IDs, income levels, credit scores and phone numbers. Phone numbers and IDs matter because they are what impersonators use to sound credible on a call.

How we compiled this data

We read the HIBP public breach list (PwnedWebsites) for entries with a breach date in 2024, 2025 or 2026 and more than 30 million accounts, and cross-checked several entries against the HIBP API. Counts on the public list are rounded by HIBP.

We did not check for breaches that appear only after the cut-off in the list we read, and we did not verify the underlying incidents ourselves. New breaches are added to HIBP continually, so treat this page as a dated snapshot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Have I Been Pwned?

A free service that lists email addresses found in known data breaches, so people can check whether their address appears. This page uses its public breach list.

What was the largest breach on the list?

Synthient Credential Stuffing Threat Data, with about 2 billion accounts, added to HIBP on November 6, 2025. It is a credential-stuffing dataset rather than a single company breach.

Do these numbers show how many people were affected?

No. HIBP counts accounts (email addresses) in each dataset, one person can appear in many datasets, and some entries are compilations.

Is the list complete?

No. The list we read was cut off alphabetically after “Bookchor”, so some breaches may be missing, and new breaches are added to HIBP regularly.
