People search for "example of strong passwords" and "good password suggestions" hoping for something to copy. Do not copy an example: any published password is a bad password. Learn the pattern and generate your own.

What makes a strong password

  • Length: every added character multiplies the work of guessing it.
  • Uniqueness: one password per site, so one breach does not open the rest.
  • Unpredictability: random words or characters, not names, dates or keyboard patterns.

Strong password examples (patterns, not passwords)

  • A passphrase of 4 or 5 random words, such as "maple tiger quiet lantern", with separators added.
  • A password manager generated string of 16 or more characters.
  • A passkey, which needs no password to remember.

Weak examples: "P@ssw0rd", "Summer2026!", a pet name plus a birth year, or "qwerty123". Common substitutions are in every cracking list.

How passwords get cracked

When a company is breached, stolen password hashes can be tried offline against lists of common and previously leaked passwords. Short or predictable passwords fall quickly; long random ones effectively do not. See the biggest data breaches and how to check if your password was leaked.

What is a cracked password?

A cracked password is one an attacker has recovered from a stolen hash by trying guesses. Password cracking in cyber security tests how quickly weak or common passwords fall to lists and brute force.

For the best passwords examples, look at structure rather than content. Bitwarden passwords, like those from any manager, are strong when generated randomly and unique per site.

FAQs

What is the strongest password?

A long, random, unique password, ideally 16 or more characters from a password manager, or a passphrase of four to five random words. Use passkeys where offered.

What is an example of a strong password?

A random passphrase such as four unrelated words with separators. Any example published online is compromised the moment it is public, so generate your own.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 to 16 characters for most accounts, and longer for email, banking and your password manager. Length matters more than complexity.

Are passphrases better than complex passwords?

Usually yes: four or five random words are long, easy to remember and hard to guess.

Is P@ssw0rd a strong password?

No. It uses a predictable substitution pattern that appears in cracking lists.

How do passwords get cracked?

Attackers try leaked and common passwords against stolen hashes or login pages. Unique long passwords defeat this.

Should I use a password manager like Bitwarden?

Yes for most people. A manager creates unique passwords and fills them in, so you only remember one strong master passphrase.

What is a cracked password?

A password an attacker has recovered from a stolen hash by trying guesses. Short or common passwords are cracked quickly; long random ones effectively are not.

Where can I find a strong passwords list?

You do not need one. Use a password manager's generator or a random passphrase, since published lists are compromised.

What is a strong password for Snapchat or other social apps?

The same rules apply everywhere: long, random and unique, plus two-factor authentication on the account.

Are Bitwarden or 1Password secure?

Reputable password managers use strong encryption and zero-knowledge designs, but a strong master password and two-factor authentication still matter. Check each provider's security disclosures.

What are some strong passwords to use?

Use random passphrases or generated strings instead of copied examples. Four or five unrelated words with separators, or a 16-plus character generated password, works well.

Is 1Password secure?

Reputable managers use strong encryption, but a strong master password and two-factor authentication matter most. Review each provider's security disclosures.
