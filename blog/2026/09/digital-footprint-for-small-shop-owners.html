Digital Footprint for Small Shop Owners: Google My Business, Justdial and WhatsApp Business

Scan My ShadowSeptember 12, 20264 min read

TL;DR: Listing a small shop on Google My Business, Justdial, or WhatsApp Business usually means a personal phone number becomes permanently public — often the same number used for banking and personal messaging.

For a huge number of small shop owners in India, there's no separation between the business number and the personal one — it's the same SIM, used to take orders, talk to family, and receive banking OTPs, all at once. That's convenient, and it's also a permanent public listing of a number that touches everything else.

Where the number ends up being public

  • Google My Business listings display the phone number to anyone who searches the shop's name or location, indefinitely.
  • Justdial and similar directories often list numbers pulled from public sources, sometimes without the owner ever having actively submitted them.
  • WhatsApp Business profiles, when linked to a public catalog or shared widely, expose the number to anyone who has the link, not just confirmed customers.

The risk this creates beyond spam calls

  • A number confirmed as active and business-facing becomes a more convincing target for social engineering — fake supplier calls, fraudulent “order confirmation” scams, and similar attempts.
  • If the same number is used for banking OTPs, anyone attempting SIM-swap fraud has a confirmed active target with a public, searchable history.
  • Personal WhatsApp status updates and profile photos become visible to a much wider audience than intended, since business contacts see the same profile as personal contacts.

A more separated setup

  • Use a dedicated business number wherever possible — a second SIM or a virtual business number, kept entirely separate from personal banking and messaging.
  • On WhatsApp Business specifically, review what's visible in the public profile — status updates and “about” text can be more exposed than expected.
  • Periodically check what Justdial and similar directories have listed, since numbers can appear there without an active submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really necessary to have a separate business number?

It's the single most effective step — it keeps a number used for banking OTPs and personal messaging out of public business directories entirely.

Can my number appear on Justdial even if I never submitted it?

Yes — directories sometimes pull listings from other public sources without an active submission from the business owner.

Does WhatsApp Business expose more than a regular WhatsApp account?

If linked to a public catalog or widely shared business link, yes — it's visible to anyone with the link, not just confirmed contacts, unlike a personal account.

What's the quickest way to check what's currently public about my shop's number?

A general exposure check across public sources, including directory listings, gives a faster read than manually searching each platform individually.
