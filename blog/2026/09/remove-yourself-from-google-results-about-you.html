How to Remove Personal Information From Google Search (Results About You)

Scan My Shadow · September 20, 2026
GuideGooglePrivacy

TL;DR: Google's Results about you tool finds and requests removal of Search results that show your phone number, address, email and, since February 2026 in the US, government ID numbers. Removal hides the result from Google, not from the source site, so pair it with a takedown request there. Google announced the expansion. People search for "remove from Google", "delete personal information from Google" and "results about me" to take back control of what shows up under their name. Google offers a tool for this, and it works best combined with removing the data at its source.

Step 1: Use Google's Results about you tool

  1. Open the Google app, tap your profile photo and choose Results about you, or go to goo.gle/resultsaboutyou.
  2. Add the contact details you want monitored (phone, address, email).
  3. In the US, add government ID numbers such as a driver's licence, passport or Social Security number; Google says the tool then monitors Search and alerts you.
  4. Review the flagged results and submit a removal request for each.

Step 2: Request removal of specific URLs

If a page shows your personal information and the tool did not flag it, use Google's personal information removal request. Google may decline content that is newsworthy, public-record or freely available, so keep your request specific. This is the "Google removal request" route for individual pages.

Step 3: Remove it at the source

Google's own guidance says removing a result from Search does not remove it from the web. Contact the website, or the data broker behind the listing, and ask for deletion. Start with US data broker opt-outs, people-search site opt-outs and UK, Canada and Australia opt-outs.

Removing a website or page you own from Google

If it is your own site, delete the page or add a noindex tag, then use the Removals tool in Search Console for a faster temporary hide. This is different from removing personal information about you.

Ways to remove content from Google search results

Google's tool is called Results about you (often searched as "result about you"). Use it to remove Google search results that show your contact details, then request to remove personal information from Google for specific URLs. To remove content from Google search that is not about you, use the relevant policy request form. You can delete information from Google results, remove your name from Google search results and remove info from Google in the same way, but the source page remains. If you want to remove doxxing content on Google, use the doxxing removal request. To remove Google results permanently, remove the page at its source.

To remove from Google search a page you do not own, you need the owner's cooperation or a policy-based request. To remove a website from Google search that you do own, use Search Console. To delete name from Google search results or remove search results about you, use the Results about you tool first.

FAQs

What is Google's Results about you tool?

A Google tool that monitors Search for results containing your contact details and, since February 2026 in the US, government ID numbers, and lets you request removal of those results.

How do I remove personal information from Google?

Use Results about you to flag and request removal, submit a removal request for individual URLs, and ask the source website or data broker to delete the data.

Does removing a result from Google delete it from the internet?

No. It only reduces visibility in Google Search; the page stays on the source site until its owner removes it.

How do I delete my information from Google search results for free?

The Results about you tool and the personal information removal request are free. Paid services automate broker opt-outs but are not required.

How do I remove a website from Google search?

If you own the site, delete or noindex the page and use the Search Console Removals tool. If you do not own it, request removal only if it exposes your personal information or violates a policy.

Is Results about you available outside the US?

The government ID monitoring rolled out in the US first, with more regions planned. The contact-information tool was available earlier, so check the tool for your country.

How do I remove my name from Google search?

Use Results about you to request removal of results that display your personal contact information, and ask the source websites to delete the pages. Google will not remove results just because you dislike them.

How do I remove doxxing content from Google?

Google has a removal request for content that exposes contact information with intent to harass. Submit the URLs and explain the context.

How do I remove Google search results about me?

Start with the Results about you tool, then submit removal requests for specific URLs, then contact source sites and data brokers.

How do I remove my address from Google Search?

Use Results about you to monitor and request removal of results showing your home address, and submit a removal request for specific URLs.

Can I remove me from Google search entirely?

No. You can remove results that show personal information, but you cannot remove all mentions of your name.
