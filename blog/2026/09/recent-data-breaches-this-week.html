Latest data breaches (September 2026)

  • Revolut: customer data, including ID document copies, was handed to a third party using fraudulent government requests. Read the Revolut breach guide.
  • Telus: a breach notice told some customers to ignore it; names, billing addresses and phone numbers were named as exposed. Telus breach email explained.
  • CenterPoint Energy: an attacker claims millions of customer records from a US utility. What CenterPoint customers should do.
  • Florida DMV: the DAVID driver database was accessed using improperly stored credentials. Florida DMV breach explained.
  • City Relay (London): property key-code data may have been exposed. City Relay breach.
  • Auto-IT (Australia): car dealers were listed by a ransomware group. Auto-IT ransomware.
  • Spain: the first reported breach attributed to an AI agent. AI-agent breach in Spain.

What is the difference between a data breach and a leak?

A breach is unauthorized access to a system. A leak is data exposed without a break-in, such as an open database. Both can expose personal data.

Why do breach numbers keep growing?

More services hold more data, and criminal groups increasingly target third-party providers so one incident reaches many companies.
