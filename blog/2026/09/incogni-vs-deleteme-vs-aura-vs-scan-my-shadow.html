A data removal service, also called a personal data removal service, contacts data brokers on your behalf so your details disappear from people-search sites. The options differ mainly in three ways: subscription vs one-time, coverage, and what else is bundled.

The three approaches

  1. Subscription removal: Incogni and DeleteMe run recurring opt-outs.
  2. Bundle: Aura packages removal with identity theft protection, credit monitoring and security tools.
  3. Scan and DIY: Scan My Shadow shows where you appear first, then gives steps you follow yourself.

Incogni

Incogni is made by Surfshark and automates removal requests to data brokers. Reviewers in 2025 and 2026 report annual pricing of about $95.88 for the Standard plan and about $15.98 for a single month. Custom removal requests are in higher tiers. Limits: it cannot remove data from brokers that ignore opt-outs.

DeleteMe

DeleteMe is a subscription service from Abine that submits removals and reports on progress; reviewers report about $129 per year for one person. Limits: it focuses on data brokers, not on every public record or social account.

Aura

Aura offers data broker removal as one part of a bundle that reviewers say also includes VPN, antivirus and a password manager, with credit monitoring and identity theft protection. It is more attractive if you want the whole bundle. See identity theft protection: is it worth it. Identity Guard and similar products compete in the same identity-protection space; check each provider for login, cancellation and pricing details.

Scan My Shadow is not a removal subscription. It is a one-time scan of your phone number and email across 1,500+ sources for $9.99, delivered as a report with removal steps, after which your data is deleted. Start with the free digital footprint checker.

FAQs

What is a data removal service?

A service that finds your information on data broker and people-search sites and sends removal requests on your behalf, usually as a subscription.

Reviewers report about $129 per year for one person, with higher prices for multiple people. Check the DeleteMe site for current pricing.
