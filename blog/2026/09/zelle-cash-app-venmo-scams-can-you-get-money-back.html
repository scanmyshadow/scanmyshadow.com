Payment App Scams: Zelle, Cash App and Venmo, and Getting Money Back

Scan My Shadow · September 21, 2026
Scam GuideUSAPayment Apps

TL;DR: The FTC says sending money through a payment app is like sending cash: it is very hard to get back. Common scams include a fake bank fraud department telling you to move money through Zelle, a relative in an emergency, and a prize that needs a fee. If you paid a scammer, report it to the payment app and your bank straight away, then to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Payment apps are built for speed. That is great for splitting dinner and terrible when you have just paid the wrong person, because a completed transfer is hard to pull back. Here are the scams to know and what to do if one has already worked.

The scams to know

  • The "bank fraud department". The FTC warns that scammers use Zelle to pose as your bank, say there is a problem with your account, and give step-by-step instructions to move money into a "new account in your name" that actually belongs to them, in an FTC consumer alert. The FTC adds that your bank will never tell you to transfer money or ask for personal information or passcodes.
  • A loved one in trouble. A message from a supposed relative or friend who needs money urgently for an emergency.
  • A prize with a fee. You have "won" a sweepstakes but must pay to collect it.
  • The accidental payment. A stranger sends you money, then begs you to send it back because it was a mistake. Do not send it back yourself; contact your bank or the app so the original payment is returned through them.
  • The refund rumour. Videos on social media claim the CFPB pays refunds to Zelle or Cash App scam victims who fill in a form. Enlace Latino NC says this is false.

Why it is hard to get money back

The Identity Theft Resource Center says that once money is sent to a scammer it is often gone for good, and that recovery depends on how it was sent and how fast you act. In its 2025 guide, cash and gift cards are virtually impossible to recover, wire transfers may be stopped if caught quickly, and payment apps such as Venmo, Zelle, PayPal and Cash App are generally harder, with recovery not guaranteed even if you report. Ask your bank whether the payment counts as unauthorised (someone else got into your account) or authorised (you were tricked into sending it), because the rules can differ.

What to do right now

  1. Call your bank at once, especially for Zelle, which moves money bank to bank, and report the payment inside the app too.
  2. Change passwords and the app PIN if you shared any details.
  3. Report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, as the FTC advises after reporting to the app.
  4. Keep records: screenshots of chats, account names, amounts and times.
  5. Beware of "recovery" offers. People who promise to get your money back for a fee are usually the next scam; see the fake FTC refund recovery scam.

If you sent a wire, there is a short window: see wire fraud recovery. If you paid with gift cards, follow gift card scam recovery. The full checklist is in I just got scammed in the US.

How to pay more safely

  • Only send money to people you know and trust, and confirm requests using a phone number you already have.
  • Never move money because a "bank" told you to. Real banks do not.
  • Do not pay anyone who insists on a gift card, cryptocurrency, payment app or wire transfer.
  • Turn on alerts and set low transfer limits in your banking and payment apps.

Pressure scripts like these appear in the fake bank investigator scam and in grandparent scams.

FAQs

Can you get money back from a Zelle scam?

Sometimes, but it is difficult. Report the payment to your bank and to Zelle immediately. If you were tricked into sending the money yourself, recovery is not guaranteed, so speed matters.

Are Cash App and Venmo scams reversible?

Payments sent through Cash App and Venmo are generally hard to reverse. Report the payment to the app and your bank at once and to the FTC, but do not assume a refund is guaranteed.

Does the CFPB refund Zelle scam victims who fill out a form?

No. Social media videos claiming the CFPB pays refunds to Zelle or Cash App scam victims through a form are false. Do not enter personal details on those pages.

What is the fake bank fraud department Zelle scam?

A caller or text claims to be your bank's fraud team and tells you to move money to a safe account in your own name. The account belongs to the scammer. Your bank will never tell you to transfer money.

Someone sent me money by mistake and wants it back. Is that a scam?

It can be. Do not send the money back yourself. Contact your bank or the payment app so the original payment can be returned through them, and check for other unexpected activity.
