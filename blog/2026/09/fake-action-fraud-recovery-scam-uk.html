Fraudsters Are Impersonating Action Fraud Itself in the UK

Scan My Shadow · September 17, 2026
Scam AlertUKGovernment Impersonation

TL;DR: Fraudsters in the UK are impersonating Action Fraud — the UK's own national fraud and cybercrime reporting centre — calling and sending letters claiming they can recover money lost in a past scam, in exchange for an upfront fee. Action Fraud has confirmed it never charges for its services and never asks for upfront payment to 'recover' funds.

There's a particular cruelty to this scam: it impersonates the very organization someone would genuinely turn to after being defrauded. Action Fraud has issued direct warnings about criminals contacting past victims by phone, claiming to be from Action Fraud, and offering to recover lost funds — for a fee paid upfront, typically via wire transfer.

How the scam has appeared, in documented cases

  • Phone calls where the caller states a specific amount was fraudulently taken from the victim's account (sometimes with accurate-seeming detail, including the victim's bank), then demands an upfront fee to process the recovery.
  • Physical letters using Action Fraud's old branding and copied text from its real website, describing a fabricated international recovery network to appear more credible.
  • A related "card cleaning" variant — see our separate post on that specific scheme — where a courier is sent in person to collect bank cards under a different but related false pretext.

What real Action Fraud does — and never does

  • Action Fraud is the UK's national reporting centre for fraud and cybercrime — it is not responsible for investigating individual crimes or personally recovering funds, and it never charges any fee for its reporting service.
  • Action Fraud will never call and ask for bank account details or a PIN to verify identity.
  • A caller who keeps the phone line open and directs a victim to call their bank "to confirm" is using a known technique — some fraudsters don't actually hang up, so the victim's next call reconnects to the scammer instead of their real bank.

How to verify a genuine Action Fraud contact

If you receive a call or letter claiming to be from Action Fraud, hang up or set the letter aside, then call 0300 123 2040 directly using a number you look up independently — never one provided in the suspicious contact itself — to confirm whether it was genuine.

If you've already paid an upfront "recovery fee"

Contact your bank immediately to attempt to halt or trace the transfer. Report the incident to Action Fraud through its official channels (actionfraud.police.uk or 0300 123 2040) — despite the irony, this is still the correct and genuine place to report both the original fraud and this follow-up impersonation.

FAQs

Does Action Fraud ever charge a fee to recover money from a scam?

No — Action Fraud never charges for its services and does not personally recover funds. Any call or letter demanding an upfront fee for 'recovery' is fraudulent, regardless of how official it appears.

How do I verify if a call claiming to be from Action Fraud is genuine?

Hang up and call 0300 123 2040 directly, using a number you look up independently rather than one provided by the caller or in a suspicious letter.

Why do some of these scammers keep the phone line open?

It's a known technique where the fraudster doesn't fully hang up — so when the victim tries to call their bank 'to verify,' they're reconnected to the same scammer instead. Always use a different phone or wait several minutes before making a verification call.
