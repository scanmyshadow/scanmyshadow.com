How to Temporarily Lift a Credit Freeze at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion

By Siddharth Shankar · November 2, 2026
Credit FreezeIdentity ProtectionUnited StatesHow To

TL;DR: Lifting a credit freeze is free, and bureaus must do it within one hour when you ask online or by phone (mail takes up to three business days). Log in to each bureau, choose a temporary lift for set dates, and the freeze returns on its own afterwards. Ask the lender which bureau it checks so you only thaw what’s needed. TransUnion lets you schedule a lift up to 15 days ahead. Lost an old PIN? Experian says you no longer need one: manage the freeze through an online account. Smaller bureaus such as ChexSystems and NCTUE are separate, and NCTUE can take up to three business days.

Why "temporary lift" is the right choice

Freezing your credit is one of the best free protections against identity theft (here’s how to set one up). But you’ll need to open it for a mortgage, car loan, new card, apartment application or some phone contracts.

You have two choices:

  • Temporary lift (Experian calls it a thaw): opens your file for a date range, then refreezes automatically.
  • Permanent removal: stays open until you freeze again.

Pick the temporary lift. A permanent removal relies on you remembering to refreeze, and most of us won’t.

The rules, briefly

According to Credit Karma’s guide and the FTC:

  • freezing and unfreezing is free under federal law;
  • requests online or by phone must be honoured within one hour, and often take minutes;
  • mail requests have three business days from receipt;
  • each bureau is separate, so a lift at one doesn’t touch the others;
  • when a temporary lift ends, the freeze comes back automatically.

What you’ll be asked for

Credit Karma says online account holders are verified when they log in. Otherwise expect to give your name, address, date of birth and Social Security number. Mail requests need supporting documents such as a driver’s license, Social Security card or utility bill.

Only thaw what the lender checks

Ask the lender, landlord or carrier which bureau they pull. Many use just one or two. If they don’t know, lifting all three for a short window is fine, but a one-bureau lift keeps the other two locked. It’s like opening one door for a guest instead of taking the whole house off its hinges.

Give yourself a day of buffer before the application. Some lifts are instant; you don’t want to find out at the counter that yours isn’t.

Don’t forget the smaller bureaus

If you also froze specialty files, they lift separately and may be slower:

  • NCTUE (used by many phone, pay-TV and utility companies) warns a temporary lift can take up to three business days, and requests may not work outside business hours, according to Experian. You’ll need the PIN from your freeze confirmation page.
  • ChexSystems (used by banks for new accounts) needs your ChexSystems PIN. Log in, choose Security Freeze, then Manage Freeze.

"I lost my freeze PIN"

If you froze your credit years ago, you may have been given a PIN. The big bureaus have mostly moved to online accounts. Experian says you no longer need a PIN: create or log in to an account in its Security Freeze Center and manage the freeze there. For Equifax and TransUnion, set up an online account with the same personal details, or call the numbers above and verify your identity. Expect questions about past addresses and loans.

"The lender says my file is still frozen"

  1. Check you lifted the right bureau.
  2. Check the dates, including time zones; a lift scheduled for tomorrow isn’t active today.
  3. Ask whether the lender also checks a specialty bureau such as ChexSystems or NCTUE.
  4. If it’s been over an hour since an online or phone request, call the bureau.

Don’t confuse freezes with locks

Credit locks are bureau products, sometimes paid, with app toggles. Freezes are a legal right with guaranteed timelines. Our guide to lock vs freeze explains why we prefer the freeze. If you’re freezing because of a breach, see what else leaked with a free email exposure check.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to unfreeze my credit?

Within one hour for online or phone requests, often a few minutes. Mail requests can take up to three business days.

Does it cost money to lift a credit freeze?

No. Federal law makes freezing and unfreezing free.

Do I need to unfreeze all three bureaus?

Only the ones your lender checks. Ask which bureau they use.

Can I schedule a credit freeze lift in advance?

Yes. Experian lets you schedule a thaw for set dates, and TransUnion lets you schedule a lift up to 15 days ahead.

What if I lost my credit freeze PIN?

Experian no longer requires a PIN; manage the freeze through an online account. For Equifax and TransUnion, create an online account or call to verify your identity.

Does lifting my Equifax freeze also lift ChexSystems or NCTUE?

No. Specialty bureaus are separate, and an NCTUE lift can take up to three business days.
