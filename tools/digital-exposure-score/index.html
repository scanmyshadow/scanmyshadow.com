What Is a Digital Exposure Score?

Your Digital Exposure Score is a free online privacy check that estimates how visible your personal information may be across the internet. Based on your everyday digital habits — where you've shared your phone number, how you use email, whether you reuse usernames across different websites — this digital footprint check highlights areas where your identity could be easier to find, contact, or target.

This is a self-assessment tool, not a breach scanner. It does not search any databases or breach records — it uses your own answers to model the kind of personal information exposure that commonly leads to spam, scams, or worse. Think of it as a quick digital safety check you can run in under two minutes, entirely from your phone or browser.

Why Does Online Exposure Matter?

When personal information like phone numbers, email addresses, or professional details are publicly accessible, they can be used for spam calls, phishing attempts, impersonation, or targeted scams. Data brokers and people-search sites often aggregate scattered pieces of information into a single, easily searchable profile — sometimes without you ever finding out. A basic online privacy check like this one is a useful starting point for understanding where you stand before deciding what, if anything, to do about it.

How Is the Score Calculated?

Each of the 8 questions in this online exposure checker assesses a specific dimension of digital exposure — from public visibility of your phone number and email, to old unused accounts and repeated usernames. Your answers are weighted by severity, so higher-risk habits contribute more to the overall score. The result is a number between 0 and 100, mapped to four risk levels: Low, Moderate, High, and Very High Exposure. The scoring logic is transparent and deterministic — the same answers will always produce the same score.
