What Is the Digital Privacy Checklist?

This free checklist reviews your everyday digital privacy habits across contact details, old accounts, public information, and photos — the areas that most commonly contribute to online exposure. It's a self-assessment, not a scanner. It does not search any databases or the internet.

The checklist takes about two minutes and gives you a privacy risk score out of 100, along with your biggest exposure areas and practical next steps.

How Is the Score Calculated?

Each of the 10 questions targets a specific exposure dimension — contact information exposure, old accounts, public information, and how often you review your own privacy. Answers are weighted by how much they typically contribute to exposure, and combined into a score between 0 and 100, mapped to five risk levels from Low to Very High. The scoring logic is transparent and deterministic — the same answers always produce the same score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this checklist scan the internet for my information?
No. This checklist is a self-assessment based entirely on your answers. It does not search any databases, breach records, or the internet.
Does the checklist store my answers?
No. Your answers are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. If you refresh the page, your results will disappear.
How is my privacy score calculated?
Each answer is weighted based on how much it typically contributes to online exposure, covering contact details, old accounts, public information, and how often you review your own privacy. The weighted total is scaled to a score out of 100.
Is a high score proof that my data has been exposed?
No. A high score means your habits suggest a higher potential for exposure — it is not confirmation of an actual breach. An actual exposure check requires scanning real data sources.
What should I do after completing this checklist?
Start with the recommendations shown for your specific answers, such as closing unused accounts or removing public contact details. If you want to see what may actually be exposed, Scan My Shadow's paid scan checks your real phone number and email against public and breach sources.
