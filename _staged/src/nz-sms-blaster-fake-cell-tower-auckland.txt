=== TLDR
In **Operation Orca**, NZ Police arrested a **19-year-old** in central Auckland and seized an **"SMS Blaster"**: a device acting as a **fake cell tower** that forced nearby phones onto a bogus network and sent **bank phishing texts**, about **700 in a single night**. DIA flagged it in late July 2024; the warrant was executed **23 August**. Around **120 people** were affected; no losses were reported. Texts that arrive this way can bypass your carrier's spam filters.

=== BODY
## What happened

From [NZ Police](https://www.police.govt.nz/news/release/op-orca-%E2%80%94-smishing-scam-smashed) (3 October 2024):

- DIA first flagged an unusual smishing campaign in **late July 2024**.
- Police executed a search warrant in **central Auckland** on **23 August**.
- A **19-year-old** man was arrested and charged with **interfering with a computer system**.
- The device sent **thousands** of texts, about **700 in one night**, posing as **banks**, linking to sites imitating **bank domains**.
- About **120 people** were affected; **no financial losses** reported.

## How an SMS blaster works

Your phone connects to whichever nearby tower looks strongest. An SMS blaster pretends to be one, pulls nearby phones onto its fake network, and **sends texts directly**. Because the message never goes through your real mobile network, your carrier's **scam filters don't see it**.

Imagine a fake postie standing on your street handing out letters that look like they're from your bank. The real post office never saw them.

## Why it matters

- It shows scammers going **physical**, not just online.
- It means "my carrier blocks scam texts" isn't a full defence.
- The texts can show any sender name, including your bank's.

## How to protect yourself

- **Don't click links in bank texts.** Open your banking app instead. Many NZ banks say they never send login links ([[nz-anz-scam-alerts-2026|ANZ]], [[nz-kiwibank-latest-scams-2026|Kiwibank]]).
- If you suspect your account's compromised, **call your bank on its published number**.
- **Forward** scam texts to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]]).

Police advice: don't click links in unexpected texts, don't share personal or login details, and don't transfer money after a suspicious text or call.
