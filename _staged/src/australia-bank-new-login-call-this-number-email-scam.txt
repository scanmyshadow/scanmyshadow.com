=== TLDR
The newest bank scam emails don't have a link. They have a **phone number**. "**New login detected**", "**Automatic Payment Arrangement Now Active**", "**new direct debit set up**", "**unfamiliar charge on your card**": call us now. **Westpac**, **NAB** and **ANZ** have all warned about these in 2026. The number reaches a fake fraud team that talks you into moving money to a **"safe account"** or reading out codes. Never call a number from an email. Use the one on your card or app.

## Why phone numbers instead of links

People have been told for years "don't click links." Fair enough. So scammers switched to **"call us"**. Email filters don't flag a phone number the way they flag a dodgy link, and a human voice is more persuasive than a web page.

It's the scam equivalent of a shop that got banned from the mall opening a stall in the car park.

## What happens on the call

1. A calm "fraud specialist" confirms there's a problem.
2. They may know your name, address or partial account number (thanks, data breaches).
3. They ask you to **read out a code**, **install an app** for "security", or **move money to a safe account**.
4. Your money goes to the scammer.

## The rules that beat it

- **Never call a number from an unexpected email or text.** Use the number on your card, in your app or on the bank's website.
- **No bank uses "safe accounts."** Westpac and NAB both say they'll never ask you to move money to keep it safe.
- **Never read out a code.** See [[australia-bank-code-read-back-scam|read-back code scams]].
- **Never install remote access software** for a bank.

## If you called and acted

1. Hang up. Call your bank on the official number **immediately**.
2. Ask them to stop or recall any payment.
3. Change passwords from a different device.
4. Report to Scamwatch and ReportCyber; contact IDCARE (1800 595 160).

Forward the email to your bank's hoax address: [[australia-report-spam-sms-acma-0429-999-888|full list here]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if breach data is why they knew your details.

=== FAQ
Q: Is a "new login detected" email from my bank real?
A: Westpac and NAB have warned about fake "new login" emails that ask you to call a number. Call your bank on its official number instead.
Q: What is a safe account scam?
A: A scammer posing as your bank convinces you to move money to a "safe" account that they control. Banks don't do this.
Q: Why do scam emails ask me to call instead of click?
A: People are wary of links, and phone numbers are harder for filters to catch. A live caller can also pressure you more effectively.
Q: What should I do if I called the number?
A: Call your bank on its official number immediately, stop any payments, change passwords and report it.
