=== TLDR
Some websites now show a fake **"verify you're human"** check that tells you to **copy a command and paste it** into your computer, often by pressing a key combo and hitting Enter. **ANZ warned about this in June 2026**: running it **installs harmful software**. A real CAPTCHA asks you to click boxes or pick pictures. It **never** asks you to run anything. If you did it, disconnect and get the device checked.

=== BODY
## What it looks like

[ANZ's June 2026 alert](https://www.anz.com.au/content/anzcomau/en/personal/security/latest-scams-australia.html) describes fake verification pages that ask you to **copy and paste a command** to prove you're human. That command **installs harmful software**.

Typically the page says something like:

- "Verify you are human"
- "Press **[key] + R**", then "**Ctrl + V**", then "**Enter**"
- Sometimes it silently copies the command for you when you click a button

Security researchers call this style of attack **ClickFix**.

## Why it fools smart people

- CAPTCHAs are annoying and familiar, so we do what they say without thinking.
- The instructions are short and technical-looking. It feels like IT, not crime.
- The page may look like a site you trust, reached from a search ad or a link in an email.

Here's the tell: **no website needs you to open a system box and run code to prove you're a person.** A robot could do that faster than you anyway.

## What the malware can do

Pasted commands can download software that steals **saved passwords, browser cookies, banking sessions and crypto wallets**, or lets attackers control the device. That's how a "quick check" turns into an emptied account.

## If you already ran it

1. **Disconnect** from the internet (Wi-Fi off, cable out).
2. From a **different, clean device**, change passwords for email and banking, and sign out of all sessions.
3. **Call your bank.** Tell them your device may be compromised.
4. Get the computer **scanned or professionally cleaned**; consider a full reinstall.
5. Turn on **MFA** everywhere ([[how-to-set-up-two-factor-authentication|guide]]).
6. Report it to **ReportCyber** at cyber.gov.au.

## Avoid it next time

- Never paste anything a website tells you to into a Run box, Terminal or PowerShell.
- Be wary of sponsored search results for banks, software downloads and government services.
- Keep your OS and browser updated.

=== FAQ
Q: Will a real CAPTCHA ask me to paste a command?
A: No. Real human-verification checks ask you to click boxes or pick images, never to run a command.
Q: What happens if I paste the command from a fake verification page?
A: ANZ warns it installs harmful software, which can steal passwords and banking sessions.
Q: What should I do if I ran a command from a fake CAPTCHA?
A: Disconnect, change passwords from a clean device, call your bank and get the computer checked.
Q: What is ClickFix?
A: A name security researchers use for scams that trick people into running malicious commands themselves, often via fake verification pages.
