=== TLDR
New Zealand's **2026 general election** is in **November**, and same-day enrolment has been **scrapped**, so the Electoral Commission is sending reminders. Genuine ones in 2026: texts from **5101**, emails from **no-reply@easy.vote.nz**, sent **31 August–20 September**, and the email **doesn't include links**. The Commission **never** asks for a **password, PIN, login, money or bank details**, and you don't need any of those to enrol. Unsure? Call **0800 36 76 56**.

=== BODY
## What's genuine

From the [Electoral Commission](https://elections.nz/media-and-news/2026/31-aug-20-sep-emails-and-texts):

| Channel | Genuine sender |
|---|---|
| Text | **5101** |
| Email | **no-reply@easy.vote.nz** |
| Links in the email | **None** |
| When | **31 August–20 September 2026** |

You'll only get one if you gave an email or mobile number when enrolling or updating.

## What they never ask for

- A **password, PIN or login**
- **Money**
- **Bank account details**

You don't need any of those to enrol to vote. So any "pay $1 to confirm your enrolment" message is a scam, full stop.

## Why scammers love elections

- There's a **real deadline** in the news.
- Same-day enrolment is gone, so people are anxious to get it right. Lawmakers ended election-day enrolment in a December law change, and the Commission says voters "must be enrolled in advance" ([report](https://www.jonesborosun.com/news/world/new-zealanders-abroad-urged-to-enroll-for-the-election-as-new-law-locks-out-last/article_d86936cc-5f2b-5a6a-a548-3fce544a1efa.html)).
- Official-looking messages arrive anyway, so fakes blend in.

Real deadline, real anxiety, fake link. It's the scammer's holy trinity.

## How to check your enrolment safely

- Type **vote.nz** yourself.
- Or call **0800 36 76 56**.

## Privacy tip

If having your address on the public roll could put you at risk, apply for the [[nz-unpublished-electoral-roll-how-to|unpublished roll]].

## Got a fake?

- Forward texts to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]]).
- Clicked and paid? Call your bank, then Police (105).
