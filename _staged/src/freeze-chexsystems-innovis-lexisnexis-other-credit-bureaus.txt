=== TLDR
The big three bureaus get all the attention, but criminals can also exploit smaller files: **ChexSystems** (bank accounts), **NCTUE** (phone, pay-TV and utility accounts), **Innovis** (credit), **LexisNexis** (which now includes SageStream) and **Clarity Services** (personal and auto title loans). Each offers a **free security freeze** online, by phone or by mail. Freeze the big three first, then add these, especially if your **Social Security number has leaked**. Save every **PIN and confirmation page**: ChexSystems and NCTUE need a PIN to lift the freeze, and an NCTUE lift can take up to **three business days**.

=== BODY
## Why bother with the small ones?

A freeze at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion blocks most new credit. But not every company checks the big three:

- some **banks** check **ChexSystems** before opening a checking or savings account;
- many **mobile carriers and utilities** check **NCTUE**;
- some **lenders** use **Innovis**, **LexisNexis** or **Clarity** reports.

As security journalist [Brian Krebs reported](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2018/05/another-credit-freeze-target-nctue-com/), freezes aren't shared between agencies. Equifax told him that a consumer "would need to independently place and lift a freeze with NCTUE." A fraudster who can open a bank account or phone contract in your name can use it to launder money, take over your number or run up debts.

## Bureau by bureau: what to expect

### ChexSystems

According to [Experian's ChexSystems guide](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/how-to-freeze-chexsystems-report):

- you register for a **Consumer Portal** account with your name, date of birth, SSN, address and an ID number, then answer security questions;
- ChexSystems freezes your report **within 24 hours**;
- adding, removing or lifting the freeze is **free**, and it doesn't affect your credit score;
- you get a **PIN** (in the portal or by mail) that you need to lift the freeze, change details, request your report or dispute errors. Lost it? Request a replacement online or by mail.

A ChexSystems report can include **closed accounts, bounced checks, overdrawn accounts** and your check-ordering history, which is exactly what a bank reviews when you apply.

### NCTUE

NCTUE (the National Consumer Telecom and Utilities Exchange) collects account data from **telecom, pay-TV, home security and utility** companies. Krebs reported that Equifax runs the NCTUE database as a contractor, though Equifax says NCTUE is a separate entity.

From [Experian](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/how-to-freeze-and-unfreeze-nctue-report):

- the freeze is **free**;
- after freezing online, **download the confirmation page**, because it holds the PIN you'll need to lift the freeze;
- you can lift it temporarily, for **specific companies** (using one-time codes), or permanently;
- NCTUE warns a temporary lift can take **up to three business days**, and requests may not work outside business hours. Plan ahead before switching phone carriers or moving home.

### LexisNexis and SageStream

SageStream is now part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions. A LexisNexis freeze blocks release of your **LexisNexis Consumer Disclosure Report**, your **SageStream Consumer Report** and your score without your authorisation.

### Innovis and Clarity

Innovis is a smaller consumer credit bureau that some lenders check alongside the big three. Clarity Services, owned by Experian, is used for **personal and auto title loans**. Both offer free freezes online, by phone or by mail.

## The trade-off

Freezing ChexSystems means a bank may not be able to open an account for you until you lift it. Freezing NCTUE can slow down a new phone contract or utility connection. Both are small hassles when you switch providers, and big obstacles for someone pretending to be you.

Keep a simple record: **which bureaus, the date, the PIN or login, and how to lift each one.** Here's [[how-to-temporarily-lift-credit-freeze-thaw|how lifting works at the big three]].

## Who should do this

- anyone whose **full SSN** appeared in a breach (sadly, a lot of people after [[was-i-affected-by-the-national-public-data-breach|National Public Data]]);
- victims of [[what-is-synthetic-identity-fraud|synthetic or new-account fraud]];
- anyone whose **mobile number was hijacked** or ported out without permission;
- anyone who wants free, belt-and-braces protection.

=== FAQ
Q: Can I freeze ChexSystems?
A: Yes, free, through the ChexSystems Consumer Portal, by phone at 800-428-9623 or by mail. It may block new bank accounts until you lift it.
Q: What is NCTUE and should I freeze it?
A: NCTUE holds account data from phone, pay-TV, home security and utility companies. Many carriers check it, so a freeze helps block phone accounts opened in your name.
Q: How long does it take to lift an NCTUE freeze?
A: NCTUE warns a temporary lift can take up to three business days, so plan ahead.
Q: Is SageStream still a separate bureau?
A: SageStream is now part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions. A LexisNexis freeze covers the SageStream report.
Q: Do freezes at the big three cover these bureaus?
A: No. Each agency is separate, so you have to freeze and lift each one yourself.
Q: What happens if I lose my ChexSystems PIN?
A: You can request a replacement PIN online or by mail.
