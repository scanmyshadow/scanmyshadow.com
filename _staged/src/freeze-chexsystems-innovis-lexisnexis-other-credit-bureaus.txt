=== TLDR
The big three bureaus get all the attention, but criminals can also abuse smaller files: ChexSystems (bank accounts), Innovis (credit), LexisNexis (which now includes SageStream) and Clarity Services (personal and title loans). Each offers a security freeze online, by phone or by mail. Freeze the big three first, then add these for a sturdier fence, especially if your SSN has leaked.

=== BODY
## Why bother with the small ones?

A freeze at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion blocks most new credit. But some banks check **ChexSystems** before opening a checking account, and some lenders use **Innovis**, **LexisNexis** or **Clarity** reports. A fraudster who can open a bank account in your name can use it to launder money or cash fake checks. If your full Social Security number has been in a breach, these files are worth locking.

## How to freeze each one

Contact details from [Clark Howard's guide to smaller bureaus](https://clark.com/credit/smaller-credit-bureaus-credit-freeze/):

| Bureau | Used for | Freeze online | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChexSystems | Checking and savings account history | chexsystems.com (security freeze page) | 800-428-9623 |
| Innovis | Consumer credit | innovis.com (security freeze page) | 1-866-712-4546 |
| LexisNexis Risk Solutions (incl. SageStream) | Consumer disclosure and SageStream reports | LexisNexis consumer security freeze form | 1-800-456-1244 |
| Clarity Services (Experian) | Personal loans and auto title loans | Clarity consumer security freeze form | 866-390-3118 |

Mail options exist for all four. For LexisNexis: LexisNexis Risk Solutions Consumer Center, Attn: Security Freeze, P.O. Box 105108, Atlanta, GA 30348-5108. For ChexSystems: Chex Systems, Inc., Attn: Security Freeze Department, PO Box 583399, Minneapolis, MN 55458.

A LexisNexis freeze blocks release of your LexisNexis Consumer Disclosure Report, your SageStream Consumer Report and your score without your authorisation.

## The trade-off

Freezing ChexSystems means banks may not be able to open a new account for you until you lift it. That's a small hassle when you switch banks, and a big obstacle for someone pretending to be you. Write down which bureaus you froze and how to lift each one; [[how-to-temporarily-lift-credit-freeze-thaw|here's how lifting works]] at the big three.

## Who should do this

- anyone whose SSN appeared in a breach (sadly, a lot of people after [[was-i-affected-by-the-national-public-data-breach|National Public Data]]);
- people who've already been victims of [[what-is-synthetic-identity-fraud|synthetic or new-account fraud]];
- anyone who wants belt-and-braces protection for free.

=== FAQ
Q: Can I freeze ChexSystems?
A: Yes, online, by phone at 800-428-9623 or by mail. It may block new bank accounts until you lift it.
Q: Is SageStream still a separate bureau?
A: SageStream is now part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions. A LexisNexis freeze covers the SageStream report.
Q: Should I freeze Innovis?
A: It's an optional extra layer after the big three, worth doing if your SSN has been exposed.
Q: What is Clarity Services?
A: A specialty bureau owned by Experian, used for personal loans and auto title loans. It offers its own freeze.
