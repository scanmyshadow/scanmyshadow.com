=== TLDR
The OAIC received **1,205** notifiable data breach reports in **2025**, the most since the scheme started in 2018 and **up 8%** from 1,112 in 2024. **Malicious or criminal activity** caused **716**. **Health service providers** were the most affected sector (**225**, 19%), followed by finance (157) and the Australian Government (118). And **82%** of Australians now say they're concerned about breaches. Released **6 July 2026**.

=== BODY
## The headline numbers

From the [OAIC's July 2026 release](https://www.oaic.gov.au/news/media-centre/data-breach-notifications-increase-to-all-time-high-in-2025,-new-ndb-stats-show):

| Metric | 2025 |
|---|---|
| Total notifications | **1,205** (record) |
| Change vs 2024 | +8% (from 1,112) |
| Caused by malicious or criminal activity | **716** |
| Australians concerned about breaches | **82%** (up from 74% in 2023) |

That's about **23 breaches a week** serious enough to report, or roughly five every working day.

## Which sectors leak the most

| Sector | Notifications |
|---|---|
| Health service providers | **225** (19%) |
| Financial services | 157 |
| Australian Government | 118 |
| Business and professional associations | 103 |
| Education | 81 |
| Legal, accounting and management | 81 |

Health tops the list again. That fits the big health breaches of recent years: [[australia-medisecure-data-breach-12-9-million|MediSecure]], [[was-i-affected-by-the-medibank-data-breach|Medibank]] and [[australia-genea-ivf-data-breach|Genea]].

## What the Commissioner said

Privacy Commissioner **Carly Kind** described the threat as "substantial and rising year on year."

## What a "notifiable" breach means

Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, organisations covered by the Privacy Act must notify you and the OAIC when a breach is likely to cause **serious harm**. So these 1,205 are the serious ones, not every lost laptop.

## What it means for you

- **Assume your data is out there.** With this volume, it's a matter of how much, not whether.
- **Health and finance data is most at risk**, which is also the data scammers can use most convincingly.
- **Most breaches are deliberate attacks**, so the stolen data is usually going somewhere useful to criminals.
