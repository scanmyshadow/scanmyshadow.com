=== BODY
## Why TruePeopleSearch matters

TruePeopleSearch is free, fast and shows a lot without a paywall: current and past addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, relatives and associates. Because it's free, it's often the first site anyone uses to find someone, which makes it one of the most important to clear.

## Step by step

These steps follow [DuckDuckGo's TruePeopleSearch removal guide](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/truepeoplesearch-removal).

1. **Start the removal.** Go to [truepeoplesearch.com/removal](https://www.truepeoplesearch.com/removal), enter your email, confirm you're the subject of the record, complete the hCaptcha and click “Begin Removal.”
2. **Find your listing.** Search by name (recommended) plus city, state or ZIP, then click “View Details” on your listing. Copy that URL so you can check later.
3. **Remove it.** Scroll to the bottom of the listing and click “Remove This Record.”
4. **Confirm by email.** Open the email titled “Please complete your removal request.” and click the link. You should see: “Record removal request accepted. Your record should be removed everywhere from this site within 72 hours.”

## Timing and limits

- Typically within about an hour, and within 72 hours everywhere on the site.
- There's no follow-up email when it's done; revisit your saved URL to check.
- Up to **five requests** per email address. If you have more listings, use a second email.
- Google and Bing may keep a cached copy for days or weeks.

## It can come back

TruePeopleSearch refreshes its data regularly, so a new listing can appear later. A three-monthly self-search is a good habit. Our [[how-to-google-yourself-digital-footprint-audit|Google yourself audit]] shows how to do it in ten minutes.
