=== TLDR
Under **rule 6** of the **Health Information Privacy Code**, you can ask any health agency for the information it holds about you. They must respond within **20 working days** (or transfer your request within **10 working days** if another agency is more relevant). **Public hospitals can't charge.** Private providers like **GPs** can charge only if they gave you the same info in the **last 12 months**, or for **X-rays, videos and MRI/CT/PET images**. After recent health breaches, it's worth checking.

=== BODY
## Your rights

From the [Privacy Commissioner's HIPC fact sheet](https://www.privacy.org.nz/assets/New-order/Privacy-Act-2020/Codes-of-practice/Health-information-privacy-code-2020/HIPC-Factsheet-4_-Dealing-with-Access-Requests.pdf):

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Right** | Request health information about yourself (rule 6) |
| **Deadline** | **20 working days** to provide it or explain a refusal |
| **Transfer** | Within **10 working days** if another agency is more closely connected |
| **Public agencies** (e.g. hospitals) | **Cannot charge** |
| **Private providers** (e.g. GPs) | Can charge only if they supplied the same info in the **last 12 months**, or for **X-rays, video, MRI/CT/PET images** |

## When they can refuse

- If disclosure would **harm your physical or mental health**
- If it would **unwarrantedly reveal someone else's** affairs
- Other grounds under sections 49–53 of the Privacy Act

## How to ask

1. Contact the provider's **privacy officer** (health agencies should have one).
2. Write a short request: "Under rule 6 of the Health Information Privacy Code, please provide a copy of my health records from [date] to [date]."
3. Include **ID details** they can check.
4. Diary **20 working days**.

## Why check now

New Zealand health data has had a rough couple of years: the [[nz-manage-my-health-data-breach-privacy-commissioner-findings|Manage My Health breach]] and the [[nz-medimap-attack-patients-marked-deceased|MediMap attack]], where records were **altered**. Checking your records for errors is reasonable, especially medications and personal details.

Finding a mistake in your own medical file is the one time "being nosy" is officially encouraged.

## If something's wrong or they don't respond

- Ask for a **correction**.
- Contact the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner** on **0800 803 909**, or complain ([[nz-privacy-commissioner-complaint-how-to|how]]).

For non-health organisations, see [[nz-privacy-act-access-request-how-to|general access requests]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows which breaches your email is in.

=== FAQ
Q: Can I get a copy of my medical records in NZ?
A: Yes. Rule 6 of the Health Information Privacy Code gives you the right to request your health information.
Q: How long does a health provider have to respond?
A: 20 working days.
Q: Do I have to pay for my health records?
A: Public agencies like hospitals can't charge. Private providers may charge only in limited cases, like repeat requests within 12 months or imaging.
Q: Can a provider refuse my request?
A: Only on specific grounds, such as serious harm to your health or unwarranted disclosure of someone else's affairs.
