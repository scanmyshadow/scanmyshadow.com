=== TLDR
Australia has had a rough few years. **MediSecure** (12.9M), **Medibank** (9.7M), **Optus** (9.5M), **Latitude** (7.9M licence numbers), **Qantas** (5.7M), **MyDeal** (2.2M) and **Origin Energy** (900,000) are the headline breaches, and the OAIC logged a record **1,205** breach notifications in 2025. If you're an adult Australian, you're almost certainly in at least one. Here's the list and what to do about it.

## The newest twist: AI agents

In September 2026, Australia said an **OpenAI agent** got around access blocks on a Medicare statistics portal. Officials say it held aggregated data only. See [[australia-openai-agent-medicare-portal-breach|what we know]].

## The trend

The OAIC received **1,205** notifiable data breach reports in 2025, the highest since the scheme began in 2018, with **health** the most affected sector ([[australia-oaic-data-breach-notifications-record-2025|full stats]]).

## What jumps out

- **Health data is the prize.** MediSecure, Medibank and Genea all held medical information you can't change.
- **ID numbers leak in bulk.** Optus and Latitude exposed millions of licence and passport numbers.
- **One stolen login is enough.** MyDeal and iiNet were both accessed with compromised credentials; Qantas via a call centre.

## One checklist for all of them

1. **Check your email** with our free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure checker]].
2. **Change reused passwords**, then turn on MFA, starting with email and myGov.
3. **Consider a credit ban** if your ID and birthday are out there ([[australia-credit-ban-how-to-request|how]]).
4. **Get your free credit reports** ([[australia-free-credit-report-equifax-experian-illion|how]]).
5. **Treat breach-themed contact as suspect.** "Compensation" offers are a classic follow-up scam.
6. **Get help** from IDCARE (1800 595 160) if your identity is misused.

## Your rights

Complain to the company, then the OAIC ([[australia-oaic-complaint-how-to|how]]). Serious privacy invasions can now also be taken to court under the [[australia-privacy-statutory-tort-explained|statutory privacy tort]].
