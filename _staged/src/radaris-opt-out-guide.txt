=== TLDR
Search Radaris for your profile and copy its URL, then submit it with your name and email at radaris.com/control-privacy (also linked as “Start removing” in the profile footer). Solve the CAPTCHA, click the confirmation email link and complete a second CAPTCHA if asked. Radaris takes about 7 to 14 business days.

=== BODY
## What Radaris pulls together

Radaris builds profiles that can include addresses, phone numbers, relatives, property records, employment and court-related details. It's known for detailed, sometimes oddly specific pages, and for having more than one profile per person.

## Step by step

From [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually):

1. **Find your profile.** Search radaris.com for your name and location. Open your profile and copy its URL.
2. **Open the privacy control page.** Go to radaris.com/control-privacy, or click “Start removing” in your profile's footer.
3. **Submit.** Paste the profile URL, add your name and email, and complete the CAPTCHA.
4. **Confirm.** Click the link in the confirmation email, and complete a second CAPTCHA if prompted.

## Timeline

Radaris takes **7 to 14 business days**, per Cloaked. That's slower than most free people-search sites, so set a reminder to check after two weeks.

## Tips

- **Check for duplicates.** Search for maiden names, middle initials and past cities. Each profile needs its own request.
- **Use an email you can access but don't mind sharing.** An [[email-alias-temporary-phone-number-privacy|email alias]] is ideal.
- **Don't buy anything** to “manage” your profile. Removal is free.
- **Recheck every few months.** Listings can reappear.
