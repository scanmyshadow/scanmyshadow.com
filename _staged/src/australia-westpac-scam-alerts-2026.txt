=== TLDR
Westpac's 2026 alerts: **"New login detected"** emails asking you to **call a number**, which leads to a **"safe account"** transfer (October); fake **Online Banking verification** emails with a deadline (July); a business called **"Westpac-Group Australia"** that has nothing to do with Westpac (July); fake **ID Secure** "stay compliant" emails (May); and **Rewards points expiring** texts (March). Westpac will **never** ask you to move money to a safe account or verify ID via a link. Forward emails to **hoax@westpac.com.au**, texts to **0497 132 032**.

=== BODY
## Westpac's 2026 scam list

From [Westpac's latest scams page](https://www.westpac.com.au/security/latest-scams/):

| When | Scam | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 2026 | **"New login detected"** | Email says someone logged in and asks you to call a number; a fake banker then pushes you to move money to a "safe" account |
| Jul 2026 | **Online Banking verification** | Email says access will be restricted unless you verify by a deadline; link goes to a fake site |
| Jul 2026 | **"Westpac-Group Australia"** | A business unaffiliated with Westpac using a similar name; verify payment requests independently |
| May 2026 | **ID Secure** | Email says update your details to stay "compliant"; "Verify your ID" goes to a fake site |
| Mar 2026 | **Rewards SMS** | Points about to expire; "redeem" link goes to a fake site |

## The "new login" trick, step by step

1. You get a scary email: someone just logged in from a new device.
2. It gives a **phone number**, not a link, so it feels safer.
3. The "fraud team" answers, confirms the "attack", and says your money must be moved to a **safe account** right now.
4. The safe account belongs to the scammer.

There's no such thing as a safe account. If a bank ever needed to protect your money, it would lock the account, not ask you to move it. See also [[australia-bank-new-login-call-this-number-email-scam|the same trick at NAB and ANZ]].

## What Westpac will never ask

- Your Online Banking **password, card PIN or CVC**
- An Online Banking **Security Code** (SMS, token or app)
- To **install software** so Westpac can connect to your device
- To sign in while using **remote access software**
- To **verify your identity via a link** in an email or SMS
- To **move money to a "safe" account**
- To **leave your card** in the letterbox for a courier

## How to report

- **Emails:** forward to **hoax@westpac.com.au**, then delete.
- **Texts:** forward to **0497 132 032**.
- **Clicked, shared details or called a number?** Contact Westpac immediately via westpac.com.au/contact-us, change your password from another device, and watch your accounts.
