=== TLDR
If you live in Canada and shop, fly, pay a power bill or have ever left a Crime Stoppers tip, odds are good you're in at least one recent breach. The big ones: **Canadian Tire** (38.3 million emails), **Loblaw** (about 2 million people), **WestJet** (1.2 million, passports included), **Canada Goose** (about 582,000 emails), **Nova Scotia Power** (about 280,000, some SINs and bank accounts) and **Crime Stoppers** (1.5 million tips). Here's what each exposed and the one checklist that covers them all.

## What jumps out

**Retail is the biggest target by volume.** Canadian Tire alone produced roughly one record for every person in the country. Most of it is contact data, which fuels phishing rather than instant fraud.

**Identity documents are the scariest.** WestJet's passports and Nova Scotia Power's SINs and bank numbers can't be "reset" like a password. Those breaches deserve long-term attention.

**Notifications lag.** Loblaw detected its breach in March 2026 and started notifying people in October. Months can pass between theft and your inbox hearing about it.

**Your data adds up.** One breach gives a scammer your email. Another gives your birthday. A third, your address. Combined, they're enough to impersonate you convincingly.

## One checklist for all of them

1. **Check your email** against all known breaches with our free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure checker]] or [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/).
2. **Change reused passwords**, starting with email and banking.
3. **Turn on 2FA**, preferably app-based ([[how-to-set-up-two-factor-authentication|guide]]).
4. **Claim any free monitoring** offered in notification letters, through official links only.
5. **Place a fraud alert** if ID documents, SIN or DOB leaked ([[canada-equifax-transunion-fraud-alert-guide|how]]).
6. **Pull your credit reports** for free ([[canada-free-credit-report-equifax-transunion|how]]).
7. **Treat breach-themed messages as suspicious.** Fake "compensation" and "verify your account" emails always follow big breaches.

## Know your rights

Companies must notify you when a breach creates a real risk of significant harm.
