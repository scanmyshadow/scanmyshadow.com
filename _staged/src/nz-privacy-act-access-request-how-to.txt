=== TLDR
Under the **Privacy Act 2020**, you can ask any organisation for the **personal information it holds about you**. It must respond **as soon as reasonably practicable** and **no later than 20 working days** after receiving your request. It can extend only with reasons, told within the original timeframe. Refusals are allowed only on specific grounds, and you can **complain to the Privacy Commissioner** if you're unhappy. Here's how to do it.

=== BODY
## Your right

From [Sprintlaw's guide](https://sprintlaw.co.nz/articles/privacy-act-2020-responding-to-personal-information-access-requests-in-nz/) to access requests:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Deadline** | No later than **20 working days** (excluding weekends and public holidays) |
| **Extensions** | Must be reasonable, given within the original 20 days, with reasons and your right to complain |
| **Identity checks** | Must be proportionate; stronger for sensitive info |
| **Refusals** | Only on set grounds, e.g. revealing someone else's info, prejudicing the law, trade secrets, legal privilege, serious threat to safety. Redaction is an option |
| **Complaints** | You must be told you can complain to the Privacy Commissioner |

## How to make the request

1. **Find the privacy officer** (usually in the privacy policy).
2. **Send it in writing** so you have a timestamp.
3. **Keep it simple.** Something like:

> "Under the Privacy Act 2020, I request access to all personal information you hold about me, including where you got it from and who you've shared it with. My details: [name, email, phone, account number]. Please respond within 20 working days."

4. **Provide ID** if they ask (proportionate checks only).
5. **Diary the 20-working-day deadline.**

## What to do with the results

- **Wrong information?** Ask for it to be corrected.
- **Surprised by who they shared it with?** Ask why.
- **Got nothing back?** Complain to the Privacy Commissioner ([[nz-privacy-commissioner-complaint-how-to|how]]). Access complaints don't need proof of harm.

## Good places to start

- **Credit agencies** (Centrix, Equifax, Experian): see also [[nz-free-credit-report-how-to|free credit reports]].
- **Your bank and telco.**
- **Health providers** have specific rules: see [[nz-health-records-access-request|accessing health records]].
- **Companies that told you about a breach**: ask exactly what of yours was taken.

## Not sure who holds your data?

A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] lists the breaches your email appears in, which is a handy list of who to ask. The Privacy Commissioner also wants a **right to erasure** added to the Act ([[nz-privacy-breach-notifications-rise-43-percent|more]]).

=== FAQ
Q: How long does a NZ organisation have to answer a privacy access request?
A: As soon as reasonably practicable and no later than 20 working days.
Q: Can an organisation extend the deadline?
A: Yes, if the extension is reasonable and communicated within the original timeframe with reasons and information about your right to complain.
Q: Can they refuse my request?
A: Only on specific grounds, such as revealing someone else's information or a serious safety threat. They may redact instead.
Q: Where do I complain if they don't respond?
A: To the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
