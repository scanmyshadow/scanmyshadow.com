=== TLDR
In **March 2024**, Police warned that fraudsters posing as **landlords or agents** were targeting people, especially **new arrivals** to Auckland, through rental ads on a **Chinese-language forum** and chats on **WeChat**. Victims got **fake ID documents**, signed **fake tenancy agreements**, paid to **"reserve"** the property, then paid again to **"furnish"** it, and arrived to find it **already rented**. Each payment was typically **thousands of dollars**. Never pay for a place you can't verify.

=== BODY
## How it worked

From [1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2024/03/04/scam-alert-rent-fraudsters-target-chinese-community-police):

1. Listings appear on a popular NZ-based **Chinese web forum**.
2. Contact moves to **WeChat**.
3. The "landlord" sends **fake identity documents**.
4. You sign a **false tenancy agreement** and pay into a **NZ bank account**.
5. More payments to **"reserve"** it because "another tenant is interested."
6. A final payment to **"furnish"** the property.
7. You arrive in New Zealand. The property is **already rented** to someone else.

The scam targets people who can't inspect a property in person. Distance is the scammer's best friend.

## Police advice

- **Don't transfer money** to anyone you can't verify.
- Real landlords or agents have a **website** and **physical address**, and you can speak to **more than one person** there.
- Have a **NZ-based friend or relative view** the property for you.
- Suspect a scam? **Stop contact** and contact **Police**.
- Trust your instincts.
- Report and learn more at **Netsafe**.

## More checks before you pay

- Search the **address** online; if the same photos show up in another listing, walk away.
- Ask to **video call** inside the property, live, and ask them to show something specific.
- **Never pay** extra to "beat another tenant."
- Pay bonds properly: in NZ, landlords lodge bonds with **Tenancy Services**, so ask how the bond will be lodged.

## If you've paid

1. **Call your bank** immediately.
2. Report to **Police** (105) and **Netsafe**.
3. Keep every message, document and receipt.
