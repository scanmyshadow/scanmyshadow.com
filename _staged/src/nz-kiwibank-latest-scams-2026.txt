Kiwibank's scam page (updated **13 October 2026**) warns about **fake Kiwibank calls** from 0800, local, private or overseas numbers, **WhatsApp** messages using its logo, **IRD refund** emails (September 2026), **NZ Post texts** (August 2026), **fake apps** that install malware, **investment scams** impersonating CEO **Steve Jurkovich**, and **stolen cards added to mobile wallets**. === BODY
## Kiwibank's scam list

From [Kiwibank's latest scams](https://www.kiwibank.co.nz/help/fraud-scams/latest-scams):

1. **Impersonation calls and messages**: from 0800, local, private or overseas numbers, or WhatsApp; asking for logins, passwords or codes.
2. **WhatsApp and messaging app scams**: Kiwibank logo, "suspicious activity", urgent pressure. Increasing.
3. **Phishing and smishing**: posing as **NZ Post, NZTA, IRD** or Kiwibank. Kiwibank notes **AI** is making these harder to spot.
4. **IRD refund scam (Sep 2026)**: emails linking to **fake myIR** logins ([[nz-ird-tax-refund-scam-surge|IRD scams]]).
5. **NZ Post text scam (Aug 2026)** ([[nz-post-scam-texts-whatsapp|NZ Post guide]]).
6. **Fake apps and software**: via ads, social media, texts; gives scammers device and banking access ([[nz-fake-job-ads-app-malware-scam|example]]).
7. **Investment scams**: fake news, celebrity and political endorsements, deepfakes, fake advisers, including impersonating the CEO ([[nz-kiwibank-ceo-fake-investment-ads|details]]).
8. **Card fraud in mobile wallets**: stolen card details added to **Apple Pay or Google Pay**.

## The mobile wallet one

Scammers who get your card details (from a fake site or a breach) add the card to a mobile wallet on **their** phone and spend with it. If you get a mobile wallet notification you didn't expect, Kiwibank says to **contact it immediately**.

## What Kiwibank never asks

- Your **PIN, passwords, verification codes, KeepSafe answers** or **card details** on a call or email
- To **read out or repeat** a one-time code, **including "to block a payment"**
- To **download** software or an app
- To **move money** to a "safe account"
- To **buy gift cards** to protect your money

"Read me the code to block the payment" is the most convincing line in the scammer handbook. It's also always a lie.

=== FAQ
Q: Will Kiwibank ask me to read out a code to block a payment?
A: No. Kiwibank says it will never ask you to read out or repeat a one-time code, including to block a payment.
Q: How do I report a Kiwibank scam email?
A: Forward it to suspicious.email@kiwibank.co.nz, then delete it.
Q: What is Kiwibank's fraud phone number?
A: 0800 113 355, or +64 4 473 1133 from overseas, 24/7.
Q: What is mobile wallet card fraud?
A: Scammers add stolen card details to a mobile wallet like Apple Pay or Google Pay. Contact Kiwibank about any unexpected wallet notification.
