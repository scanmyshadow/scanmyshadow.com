=== TLDR
On **8 April 2026**, Kiwibank warned that scammers were using **CEO Steve Jurkovich**'s name and image in **fake social media ads and news stories** to promote a **fraudulent investment platform**. His message: **"If you see my name promoting an investment, it's a scam."** It's part of a wider wave using bank bosses, celebrities and politicians, sometimes with **deepfakes**, to sell fake investments.

=== BODY
## What Kiwibank said

From [Kiwibank's media release](https://www.kiwibank.co.nz/about-us/news-and-updates/media-releases/if-you-see-my-name-promoting-an-investment-its-a-scam-kiwibank-chief-executive/):

- Fake ads and news stories impersonated **Steve Jurkovich**.
- They promoted a **fraudulent investment platform**.
- Jurkovich: "I am not associated with any investment platform using my name or image."
- On why it works: "the appearance of legitimacy can be enough to push people into decisions they might otherwise question."

## Why bank bosses make good bait

A bank CEO sounds like the safest possible endorsement. That's the whole trick: borrow trust, then sell a platform that shows fake profits until you try to withdraw. Kiwibank lists these among its current scams, noting **deepfakes**, **fake advisers**, and victims being asked for **extra fees** to withdraw ([[nz-kiwibank-latest-scams-2026|Kiwibank's list]]).

Real CEOs are busy running banks. They don't moonlight as crypto influencers.

## How to spot it

- A **well-known person** "recommending" an investment in an **ad** or **news-style story**
- **Urgency** and pressure
- **Unexpected calls, texts or emails** after you register interest
- Requests to share **personal or banking details** via links
- **Fees** or "taxes" demanded before you can withdraw

## What to do

1. **Don't click** the ad or enter details.
2. **Check** any platform on the FMA's warnings list and the FSPR ([[nz-fma-fake-nz-registered-investment-firms|how]]).
3. **If you shared info or paid,** call your bank immediately (Kiwibank's 24/7 fraud line: 0800 113 355, or +64 4 473 1133 from overseas).
4. **Report** to Police (105) and Netsafe.

## Same scam, other faces

Deepfakes of **PM Christopher Luxon** cost one Taranaki pensioner **$224,000** ([[nz-deepfake-luxon-investment-scam-ads|story]]). ANZ has seen fake **stock education** ads ([[nz-anz-fake-stock-education-ads-investment-chat-groups|details]]).
