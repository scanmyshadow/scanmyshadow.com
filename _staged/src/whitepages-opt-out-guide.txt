=== BODY
## What Whitepages shows about you

Whitepages is one of the oldest US people-search sites. A free listing can show your name, age range, current and past addresses, relatives and phone numbers, and the paid “Premium” reports add more. For a stranger, that's often enough to find your front door. That's why it's usually first on any [[us-data-broker-sites-opt-out-guide|data broker opt-out list]].

## Step by step

These steps follow [DuckDuckGo's Whitepages opt-out guide](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/whitepages-opt-out).

1. **Find your listing.** Search whitepages.com for your name plus city, state or ZIP. Skip the “Whitepages Premium” results and open your free listing. Copy its URL: it should include your name, city, state abbreviation and a unique ID.
2. **Open the suppression page.** Go to [whitepages.com/suppression-requests](https://www.whitepages.com/suppression-requests), paste the URL and click “Next.”
3. **Confirm it's you.** Click “Remove Me”, check the details, choose a reason from the dropdown and click “Next.”
4. **Verify by phone.** Enter a phone number, tick the box and click “Call now to verify.” Answer the automated call and type the four-digit code shown on screen. Wait for the “thank you” message.

## The phone verification question

Yes, you have to give Whitepages a phone number to be removed from Whitepages. It's the privacy equivalent of being asked for ID to prove you don't want to be identified. If that bothers you, a virtual or secondary number works for receiving the call; see our guide to [[email-alias-temporary-phone-number-privacy|aliases and temporary numbers]].

## How long it takes

About 15 minutes of your time. Processing is typically within an hour, and the listing should vanish within a few hours of verifying. Google and Bing may show a cached copy for days or weeks; use [[remove-yourself-from-google-results-about-you|Google's Results About You tool]] to speed that up.

## Keep it off

Whitepages refreshes its database, so listings can return. Set a reminder to search yourself every three months. If you have several listings (old addresses, maiden name, middle initial), each one needs its own request.
