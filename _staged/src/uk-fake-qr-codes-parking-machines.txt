=== BODY
## What's happening

In March 2026 [Hartlepool Council](https://www.hartlepool.gov.uk/news/article/240/warning-issued-over-scam-qr-code-on-pay-and-display-parking-ticket-machine) warned that a scam QR code was found on a pay and display machine at Seaton Carew. Codes had been found on machines across the borough the year before, and similar scams have been reported across England.

The council's line is simple: **it never uses QR codes as a payment option**. So any QR code on its machines is fake.

## How the scam works

Someone sticks a printed QR code on a machine, often with “Scan to pay” or an app logo. Scanning it opens a convincing payment page. You enter your card details and registration. The “payment” goes nowhere except to criminals, and you may still get a parking fine. It's called quishing: [[what-is-quishing-qr-code-phishing|QR code phishing]].

## How to pay safely

- **Cash or card directly at the machine.**
- **The official app** you've downloaded from the app store (Hartlepool mentions RingGo).
- **Type the parking website address** from the signs yourself, if offered.

Never scan a sticker, even if it looks neat. Real QR codes are rare on council machines and are never stickers slapped on the side.

## Report it

- Tell the council (Hartlepool's Community Safety Team: 01429 523100).
- Report QR codes to RingGo through its QR code safety page, so it can work to shut them down.
- If you paid, call your bank or **159** and report to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/).
