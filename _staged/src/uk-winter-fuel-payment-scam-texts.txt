=== TLDR
Every autumn, fake DWP texts tell older people to “apply” for their Winter Fuel Payment through a link, sometimes asking for a small transfer to “check” their card. Most eligible people are paid automatically and don't need to do anything. For winter 2026 to 2027, payments are £100 to £300 for people born on or before 27 June 1960, with letters in October or November and most payments in November or December. Delete the text and forward it to 7726.

=== BODY
## The fake text

[Take Five](https://www.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/news/winter-fuel-payment-scam-texts/) has warned about criminals posing as the Department for Work and Pensions and texting people to “apply” for their Winter Fuel Payment. Some ask for a small payment to “check” your card. The link leads to a site that collects bank details.

## The real payment, 2026 to 2027

According to [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/winter-fuel-payment):

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | People born on or before 27 June 1960 (subject to eligibility rules) |
| Amount | £100 to £300 |
| Claim? | Most eligible people are paid automatically |
| Letter | In October or November, stating the amount |
| Payment | Most in November or December 2026 |
| Higher incomes | If your total income is over £35,000, HMRC takes it back |
| Scotland | Not available; check Pension Age Winter Heating Payment instead |

So the real system mostly runs itself. A text asking you to apply, click or pay is not how it works.

## The giveaways

- “Apply now” or “claim before the deadline.”
- A link, especially one not ending in gov.uk.
- A request for bank details or a small “verification” payment.
- Pressure: “payments close tonight.”

Take Five's line is the one to remember: “You do not need to do anything to receive the Winter Fuel Payment if you are eligible.”

## What to do

- Don't click. Mark the message as junk and delete it.
- Forward it to **7726**.
- Check facts only on gov.uk, typed in yourself.
- If you shared bank details, call your bank on the number on your card or dial **159**, and report it to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/).

=== FAQ
Q: Do I need to apply for the Winter Fuel Payment?
A: Most eligible people get it automatically and don't need to do anything, according to GOV.UK.
Q: How much is the Winter Fuel Payment for 2026 to 2027?
A: Between £100 and £300, for people born on or before 27 June 1960.
Q: When is it paid?
A: Most eligible people are paid in November or December 2026, after a letter in October or November.
Q: What should I do with a Winter Fuel Payment text?
A: Don't click. Delete it and forward it to 7726. If you shared bank details, call your bank or 159.
