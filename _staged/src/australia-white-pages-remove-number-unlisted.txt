=== TLDR
To get off the **White Pages**, make your landline **unlisted** (often called a silent number). With Telstra: **My Telstra → Services → your service → three dots → Manage directory listing**. It's **free**. Online directory changes take up to **3 business days**. Unlisting **doesn't stop telemarketers** (use the Do Not Call Register) and won't remove you from directories unrelated to Telstra. And yes, even silent numbers have leaked before.

=== BODY
## How to unlist with Telstra

From [Telstra's guide](https://telstra.com.au/support/internet-and-home-phone/change-or-unlist-number):

1. Sign in to **My Telstra**.
2. Go to **Services** and pick the service.
3. Tap the **three dots** in the corner.
4. Choose **Manage directory listing** and follow the prompts.

If you can't see the option, contact Telstra. If you have a paid **Thryv** listing, contact Thryv to change it.

## What it does

An **unlisted** number is left out of **printed, online and voice-assisted public directories**. There's **no extra charge**.

## How long it takes

Online directory changes can take up to **3 business days**. Need it gone from the online White Pages sooner? Contact Telstra.

## What it doesn't do

- **Doesn't stop telemarketing.** Register with the [[australia-do-not-call-register-guide|Do Not Call Register]].
- **Doesn't remove you** from directories **unrelated** to Telstra and the White Pages.
- **Doesn't hide your number** when you call out. For that, change your caller ID settings.

## Silent numbers have leaked before

Between 2021 and 2022, Telstra published more than **24,000** silent numbers with names and addresses in the White Pages, a failure the regulator called a privacy and safety risk. Read [[australia-telstra-silent-numbers-white-pages-breach|what happened]]. Unlisting is still worth doing; just don't treat it as invisibility.

## Other places your address shows up

- The **electoral roll**: apply as a [[australia-silent-elector-how-to-hide-address|silent elector]] if your safety is at risk.
- **Data breaches**: a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows where your email has leaked.
- People-search and old business listings: see [[can-someone-find-my-address-using-my|can someone find my address?]]

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove my number from White Pages Australia?
A: Make your number unlisted. With Telstra, go to My Telstra, Services, your service, then Manage directory listing.
Q: Does it cost money to have an unlisted number?
A: Telstra says there's no extra charge for the unlisted option.
Q: How long does it take to disappear from White Pages online?
A: Up to 3 business days, or contact Telstra if you need it sooner.
Q: Will an unlisted number stop telemarketing calls?
A: No. Register on the Do Not Call Register for that.
