=== TLDR
BNZ's **Scam Savvy Survey** (1,263 Kiwis, mid-2024) found **87%** were **targeted** by scams in the past year, **1 in 8** fell victim and **7% lost money**. **70%** now report scams, up from **46%** in 2022. **Government impersonation** hit **52%** of respondents. **Website scams** more than doubled to **22%** of victims. The **2025** survey found the share targeted dipped to **84%**, **social media** overtook email as the main scam channel, and most Kiwis now worry about **AI-generated** scams. BNZ says it will **never** ask for your PIN, password, account number or authentication codes.

=== BODY
## The numbers

From [BNZ's release](https://www.bnz.co.nz/about-us/news/scammers-undeterred-9-in-10-nzers-targeted-but-reporting-surges) (October 2024):

| Finding | Result |
|---|---|
| Targeted in past 12 months | **87%** (88% in 2023) |
| Fell victim | **1 in 8** |
| Lost money | **7%** |
| Reported scams | **70%** (62% in 2023; 46% in 2022) |
| Government impersonation experienced | **52%** (45% in 2023) |
| Email as the channel used on victims | 34% |
| Website scams (share of victims) | **22%** (9% in 2023) |
| Social media scams | 22% of respondents |

Survey of **1,263** New Zealanders, 30 July to 16 August 2024, weighted to be nationally representative.

## Three years of BNZ data

| | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Targeted in past 12 months | 88% | 87% | **84%** |
| Main scam channel | Email (40%) | Email (34% of victims) | **Social media (26%)**, ahead of email (22%) |
| Reported scams | 64% | 70% | Not reported |

Sources: [BNZ 2023](https://www.bnz.co.nz/about-us/news/nine-out-of-ten-targeted-by-scams-but-new-zealanders-getting-more-scam-savvy), BNZ 2024 (above) and [BNZ's November 2025 survey](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/BU2511/S00359.htm) of **1,070** New Zealanders, surveyed in September 2025.

## 2025: the AI worry

The 2025 survey asked about artificial intelligence for the first time:

- **64%** worry about **AI-generated phishing**;
- **58%** worry about **deepfakes**;
- **53%** worry about **AI voice cloning**.

People are changing behaviour in response: **62%** are more cautious checking messages and links, **55%** limit what they share online, **50%** fact-check news, and **7%** have set up a **family safe word** for emergencies. BNZ's head of fraud operations, Margaret Miller: "AI is making scams more sophisticated and harder to spot," and "Scammers are more successful when people are rushing."

A family safe word costs nothing and beats a cloned voice every time. If "your son" calls from a strange number needing money, ask for the word.

## Where money is hardest to recover

BNZ's 2023 survey found **56%** of **social media** scam victims and **22%** of fake **website** victims couldn't get their money back. With social media now the top channel, that's worth remembering before paying a seller you met on Facebook or Instagram.

## What it tells us

- **Nearly everyone is targeted.** If you haven't had a scam text this month, check your phone's working.
- **Reporting is way up**, from under half to 70% in two years. That's a real win: reports power blocking and takedowns.
- **Government impersonation** is the top disguise, think IRD, NZTA, Ministry of Justice.
- **Fake websites** are surging, often reached through ads.

BNZ's Ashley Kai Fong: "every report makes it harder for scammers to operate."

## What BNZ will never ask for

- Your **PIN** or **password**
- Your **account number**
- To **share authentication codes**

## BNZ's advice

- If you suspect a scam, **call your bank immediately**. Speed limits losses.
- Don't click links or attachments from **unknown senders**, or from known ones if the message seems off.
- Keep **security software updated**.
