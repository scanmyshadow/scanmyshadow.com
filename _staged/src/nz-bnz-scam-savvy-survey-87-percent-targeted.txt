=== TLDR
BNZ's **Scam Savvy Survey** (1,263 Kiwis, mid-2024) found **87%** were **targeted** by scams in the past year, **1 in 8** fell victim and **7% lost money**. **70%** now report scams, up from **46%** in 2022. **Government impersonation** hit **52%** of respondents. **Website scams** more than doubled to **22%** of victims. BNZ says it will **never** ask for your PIN, password, account number or authentication codes.

=== BODY
## The numbers

From [BNZ's release](https://www.bnz.co.nz/about-us/news/scammers-undeterred-9-in-10-nzers-targeted-but-reporting-surges) (October 2024):

| Finding | Result |
|---|---|
| Targeted in past 12 months | **87%** (88% in 2023) |
| Fell victim | **1 in 8** |
| Lost money | **7%** |
| Reported scams | **70%** (62% in 2023; 46% in 2022) |
| Government impersonation experienced | **52%** (45% in 2023) |
| Email as the channel used on victims | 34% |
| Website scams (share of victims) | **22%** (9% in 2023) |
| Social media scams | 22% of respondents |

Survey of **1,263** New Zealanders, 30 July to 16 August 2024, weighted to be nationally representative.

## What it tells us

- **Nearly everyone is targeted.** If you haven't had a scam text this month, check your phone's working.
- **Reporting is way up**, from under half to 70% in two years. That's a real win: reports power blocking and takedowns.
- **Government impersonation** is the top disguise, think IRD, NZTA, Ministry of Justice.
- **Fake websites** are surging, often reached through ads.

BNZ's Ashley Kai Fong: "every report makes it harder for scammers to operate."

## What BNZ will never ask for

- Your **PIN** or **password**
- Your **account number**
- To **share authentication codes**

## BNZ's advice

- If you suspect a scam, **call your bank immediately**. Speed limits losses.
- Don't click links or attachments from **unknown senders**, or from known ones if the message seems off.
- Keep **security software updated**.
