=== TLDR
A notice of correction is a free statement of up to 200 words you can add to your UK credit report to explain something accurate, like missed payments during illness or a fraud-related entry. Lenders read it when you apply. It doesn't change your score, may slow applications slightly, and has to be added separately at TransUnion, Experian and Equifax. If information is wrong, use a notice of dispute instead.

=== BODY
## What it is

From [TransUnion's guide](https://www.transunion.co.uk/consumer/credit-report-help/what-is-a-notice-of-correction-and-how-can-i-add-one-to-my-credit-report-if-needed): a short statement you add to your credit report for anyone who searches your file. It explains **accurate** information. For **inaccurate** information, you use a separate Notice of Dispute.

## The rules

- **No more than 200 words.**
- Relevant and accurate.
- Not trivial, rude or libellous.
- Must not blame or name another person or company.

So “My ex-partner ran up debts on our joint account” won't fly, but “Payments were missed in March to June 2025 while I was in hospital; the account has been up to date since” will.

## When it helps

- explaining missed payments during illness, bereavement or redundancy;
- explaining why there's no electoral register record (e.g., you've just moved to the UK);
- after identity fraud, explaining that accounts or searches weren't yours while they're being removed (check with the agency on wording).

## How to add one

At TransUnion: use its online form or write to its Consumer Services Team. **It doesn't carry over**: add it separately at Experian and Equifax too.

Lenders see it during applications. It's free, won't affect your score, but may make applications take a little longer while someone reads it.

## If you're a fraud victim

Notice of correction is only one tool. Also:

1. report to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/);
2. ask the lender to remove the fraudulent account;
3. dispute the entry with each agency;
4. consider [[uk-cifas-protective-registration-explained|Cifas Protective Registration]].

See our [[how-to-report-identity-theft-uk|UK identity theft guide]] and [[how-to-check-your-credit-report-for-fraud|how to check your report for fraud]].

=== FAQ
Q: What is a notice of correction?
A: A statement of up to 200 words added to your credit report to explain accurate information to lenders.
Q: Does a notice of correction affect my credit score?
A: No, but it may make applications take slightly longer to process.
Q: Do I need to add it at all three agencies?
A: Yes. A notice added at TransUnion doesn't carry over to Experian or Equifax.
Q: What if the information on my report is wrong?
A: Use a Notice of Dispute and ask the agency to investigate.
