=== TLDR
Ofcom published draft rules in July 2026 for a fraudulent advertising code under the Online Safety Act, with 40 proposed measures for platforms like Facebook, Google and YouTube: banning scam advertisers, verifying who sets up ad accounts, requiring banking and investment advertisers to be FCA-authorised, testing AI ad tools and fast-tracking police reports. Fines could reach £18m or 10% of global revenue. The consultation closed on October 2; final rules are expected next year, so protection won't arrive until 2027 at the earliest.

=== BODY
## Why this matters

UK Finance says **66%** of bank transfer fraud in 2025 started online, and investment fraud cases rose **26%**, as [Which? reported](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/big-tech-must-tackle-scam-ads-or-face-fines-anVqt0t5B3EL). Fake celebrity investment ads, copycat retailer ads and fake “apps” in sponsored posts are a big part of that.

## The proposed measures

Among Ofcom's 40 draft measures:

- **ban fraudsters** who post scam ads and stop them opening new accounts;
- **verify identity** of people setting up advertising accounts, to stop impersonation of real businesses;
- **require FCA authorisation** for anyone advertising banking or investment services;
- **strengthen security** to reduce account hijacking;
- **test AI ad-making tools** for misuse;
- create a **dedicated channel for law enforcement** to flag scam ads for swift removal.

## The teeth

Firms that fail to comply could face fines of **up to £18m or 10% of global revenue**, whichever is greater. Meta is challenging the proposed fines in court, Which? reports.

## Timeline

- Consultation: ran until **2 October 2026**.
- Final rules: expected next year.
- Which? says consumers would be unprotected until **2027 at the earliest**, and urged Ofcom to move fast. Ofcom says platforms don't have to wait.

Which?'s Rocio Concha called the proposals “a significant step” towards holding firms accountable.

## Until then: protect yourself

- Treat investment ads with celebrities as fake by default ([[deepfake-investment-scams-digital|deepfake investment scams]]).
- Check any finance firm on the FCA register ([[uk-fca-firm-checker-clone-firms-fca-impersonation|how]]).
- Don't install “apps” from ads ([[uk-fake-casino-apps-social-media-ads|fake app ads]]).
- Report scam ads using the three dots on the ad.
