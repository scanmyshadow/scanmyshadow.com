=== TLDR
**Caller Check** lets a real bank caller prove who they are by sending a notification **inside your banking app** (ASB Mobile Banking; ANZ goMoney). In **July 2026**, ANZ warned scammers are faking it, sending "**security verification**" by **email, SMS or WhatsApp**. Rule: **if the check doesn't appear in your bank's app, it's a scam.** Hang up and call the bank's published number.

=== BODY
## How real Caller Check works

- **ASB:** ask the caller for a **Caller Check** notification; legit ones show **in the ASB Mobile Banking app** ([ASB](https://www.asb.co.nz/banking-with-asb/security-alerts.html)).
- **ANZ:** Caller Check is a secure notification sent **only to the ANZ goMoney app**. It isn't available if you don't use goMoney ([ANZ](https://www.anz.co.nz/banking-with-anz/banking-safely/recognise-scams-fraud/latest-scams/july-2026-anz-impersonation-scams/)).

## How the scam copies it

ANZ's July 2026 warning:

1. A scammer **cold calls**, claiming to be ANZ.
2. They send a fake **"security verification"** by **email, SMS or WhatsApp**.
3. They insist verification has to happen through that channel.

ANZ: "If you receive one anywhere other than goMoney, it's a scam."

It's a bit like a burglar showing you a laminated card that says "Genuine Police Officer." The real badge is in your app, not in your inbox.

## What to do when a "bank" calls

1. **Ask for a Caller Check** in your app.
2. **If nothing appears in the app,** hang up.
3. **Call back** on the number on your card or the bank's website.
4. **Never share** login details, full card details or two-factor codes.
5. **Don't click links or call numbers** from unexpected messages.

## Banks' "never" lists

| Bank | Never asks for |
|---|---|
| ANZ | Internet Banking logins, full card details, 2FA codes |
| ASB | Passwords, PINs, text codes, token codes, Netcodes; reading out codes |

## If you've been caught

- **ANZ:** 0800 269 296 (overseas +64 4 470 3142), 24/7
- **ASB:** 0800 272 372 (overseas +64 9 303 0332), 24/7

Report within 3 months for possible [[nz-bank-scam-reimbursement-rules-explained|reimbursement]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your details are in breach data that make these calls convincing.

=== FAQ
Q: What is Caller Check?
A: A feature that lets a real bank caller send a verification notification to your banking app, so you know the call is genuine.
Q: Can Caller Check come by text or WhatsApp?
A: No. ANZ says Caller Check only appears in goMoney; ASB's appears in its mobile banking app. Any other channel is a scam.
Q: What should I do if a caller can't send a Caller Check?
A: Hang up and call your bank on the number on your card or its website.
