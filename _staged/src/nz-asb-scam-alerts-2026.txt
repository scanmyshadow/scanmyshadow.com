=== TLDR
In **October 2026**, ASB warned of a rise in callers posing as its **fraud team** who ask you to key in a **number sequence that secretly turns on call forwarding**, sending your calls (and bank verification calls) to the scammer. Earlier alerts cover cold calls that send you to a **fake ASB web address** and ask for **Netcodes** or **FastNet Business token codes**, plus fakes on **WhatsApp, Google Meet and iCloud** and calls to **schools**. ASB will **never** ask for codes, call forwarding, remote access or "safe account" transfers. Ask for **Caller Check** in the ASB app. Fraud line: **0800 272 372**.

=== BODY
## October 2026: the call-forwarding trick

According to [NZ Adviser](https://www.mpamag.com/nz/news/general/asb-warns-customers-as-bank-impersonation-scams-rise-in-new-zealand/594702) (23 October 2026):

- callers claim to be from **ASB's own fraud team**;
- they **spoof caller ID** so the number looks identical or very close to ASB's;
- they ask you to **key in a number sequence**, framed as a way to "stop scam calls," which actually **turns on call forwarding** to the scammer's phone;
- they also contact people through **WhatsApp, Google Meet and iCloud**, often using a company logo as the profile picture.

Once your calls are forwarded, the scammer can receive calls meant for you, including a genuine check from your bank. That's why this matters so much.

ASB head of fraud operations **Julie Wilton** said anyone asked to forward their calls should hang up: it's "a big red flag." She added: "We will never contact customers through an app, unless it's the ASB Mobile App," and "A real organisation won't mind you checking. A scammer will."

### The numbers behind the warning

- **Netsafe:** 34% of August 2026 reports involving someone posing as another person related to a **bank or financial institution**, up from **20%** between April and July 2026. (Netsafe notes these are reports made to it, not all scams.)
- **Westpac NZ:** bank impersonation made up **71%** of gross scam losses among its business customers in the first half of 2026.

## ASB's other alerts

From [ASB's security alerts page](https://www.asb.co.nz/banking-with-asb/security-alerts.html):

| Alert | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Bank impersonation (March 2026)** | Cold call about fraud on a personal or business account; you're told to type a fake ASB web address and read out Netcodes or FastNet Business token codes |
| **WhatsApp, Google Meet, iCloud** | Scammers pose as ASB Fraud, other banks or NZ brands; ask for information, remote access or phone setting changes |
| **Schools** | Callers claim a problem with the school's account, such as a payment to the wrong account; ask for usernames, passwords or codes |
| **Business customers** | Told the FastNet Business account is at risk or being hacked; some told to install remote-access software |

The Netcode request is the punchline every time. Everything else is costume.

## What ASB will never do

- Contact you via **WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud**
- Ask for **call forwarding**, **remote access** or **phone setting changes**
- Ask you to key in **long number sequences** to "secure" your account
- Ask for your **password, PIN, text codes, token codes or Netcodes**, or ask you to read them out
- Ask you to type any web address **other than asb.co.nz**
- Need your **full card number or CVV**
- Ask you to **download software**
- Ask you to **buy gift cards**, set up **crypto** or **money remittance** accounts, or **move money to keep it safe**

## Use Caller Check

If someone says they're ASB, ask them to send a **Caller Check** notification. Real ones appear **only in the ASB Mobile Banking app**. If they can't send one, or one arrives by text, email or WhatsApp, **hang up** and call ASB on its publicly listed number. Scammers have started faking caller checks by message: see [[nz-bank-caller-check-impersonation-scams|Caller Check scams]].

## Already keyed in a code?

If you entered a number sequence during a call, your phone may now be forwarding calls. Check your phone's **call forwarding** setting and turn it off, or ask your mobile provider to check. Then call ASB.

## How to report

- **Phone (24/7):** **0800 ASB FRAUD (0800 272 372)**; overseas **+64 9 303 0332**
- **Texts:** forward to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]])
- **Schools and businesses:** contact your Account Manager or call 0800 ASB FRAUD

## If you gave a code or sent money

1. Call ASB immediately and ask them to check for **new payees, devices or transfers**.
2. Change your banking password from a clean device.
3. Report to Police. The new [[nz-bank-scam-reimbursement-rules-explained|reimbursement rules]] may apply, but only if you report to your bank and Police within three months.

=== FAQ
Q: Will ASB ever ask me to set up call forwarding?
A: No. ASB says it will never ask for call forwarding or long number sequences to secure your account. Hang up.
Q: Will ASB ever ask for my Netcode?
A: No. ASB says it will never ask for Netcodes, text codes or token codes, or ask you to read them out.
Q: Does ASB contact customers on WhatsApp?
A: No. ASB says it never contacts you through WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud, and only uses the ASB Mobile App.
Q: What is ASB's fraud number?
A: 0800 ASB FRAUD (0800 272 372), or +64 9 303 0332 from overseas, 24/7.
Q: How do I check a caller is really from ASB?
A: Ask for a Caller Check notification in the ASB Mobile Banking app. If they can't send one, hang up.
