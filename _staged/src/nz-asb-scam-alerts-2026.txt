=== TLDR
In **March 2026**, ASB warned of scammers **cold calling** about "fraud" on personal or business accounts, sending people to a **fake ASB web address**, and asking for **Netcodes** or **FastNet Business token codes**. ASB also flags fakes on **WhatsApp, Google Meet and iCloud**, and calls to **schools**. ASB will **never** ask for codes, remote access, call forwarding or "safe account" transfers. Not sure? Ask for **Caller Check** in the ASB app. Fraud line: **0800 272 372**.

=== BODY
## ASB's current alerts

From [ASB's security alerts](https://www.asb.co.nz/banking-with-asb/security-alerts.html):

| Alert | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Bank impersonation (Mar 2026)** | Cold call about fraud; you're sent to a fake ASB web address and asked for Netcodes or FastNet Business token codes |
| **WhatsApp, Google Meet, iCloud** | Scammers pose as ASB Fraud, other banks or NZ brands; ask for info, remote access or phone setting changes |
| **Schools** | Callers claim problems with the school's account; ask for usernames, passwords or codes |
| **Business customers** | Told accounts are at risk; some told to install remote access software |

The Netcode request is the punchline every time. Everything else is costume.

## What ASB will never do

- Contact you via **WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud**
- Ask for **remote access**, **call forwarding** or **phone setting changes**
- Ask for your **password, PIN, text codes, token codes or Netcodes**
- Ask you to **read out codes** from your phone or FastNet token
- Ask you to type any web address **other than asb.co.nz**
- Need your **full card number or CVV**
- Ask you to **download software**
- Ask you to **buy gift cards**, set up **crypto**, or **move money to keep it safe**

## Caller Check

If someone says they're ASB, ask for a **Caller Check** notification. Real ones appear **in the ASB Mobile Banking app**. If they can't send one, **hang up** and call ASB on its published number. Scammers have started faking caller checks by text or WhatsApp; see [[nz-bank-caller-check-impersonation-scams|Caller Check scams]].

## How to report

- **Phone (24/7):** **0800 ASB FRAUD (0800 272 372)**; overseas **+64 9 303 0332**
- **Texts:** forward to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]])

## If you gave a code

Call ASB immediately. Ask them to check for new payees, devices or transfers. The new [[nz-bank-scam-reimbursement-rules-explained|reimbursement rules]] may apply, but only if you report within three months.

=== FAQ
Q: Will ASB ever ask for my Netcode?
A: No. ASB says it will never ask for Netcodes, text codes or token codes, or ask you to read them out.
Q: Does ASB contact customers on WhatsApp?
A: No. ASB says it never contacts you through WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud.
Q: What is ASB's fraud number?
A: 0800 ASB FRAUD (0800 272 372), or +64 9 303 0332 from overseas, 24/7.
Q: How do I check a caller is really from ASB?
A: Ask for a Caller Check notification in the ASB Mobile Banking app. If they can't, hang up.
