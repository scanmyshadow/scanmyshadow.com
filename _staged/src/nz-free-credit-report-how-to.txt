=== TLDR
You can get a **free credit report** from each of NZ's three agencies: **Centrix, Equifax and Experian**. Each has its own process; Equifax's free option takes about **10 business days** (paid is faster). Check for **accounts you didn't open** (loans, cards, power accounts, BNPL), **missed payments**, **defaults** and **enquiries you didn't approve**. Errors must be corrected by the provider or agency. If your info was stolen, **suppress** your file.

=== BODY
## Where to get it

From [govt.nz](https://www.govt.nz/browse/consumer-rights-and-complaints/debt-and-credit-records/check-your-own-credit-report/):

| Agency | Where to request |
|---|---|
| **Centrix** | Request section on its "my credit score" page |
| **Equifax** | Products section; free option "Get my report in 10 business days" |
| **Experian** | Ordered through CreditConnect |

Each holds slightly different data, so checking all three gives the full picture. Ignore the upsells unless you actually need a report tomorrow.

## What to check

- **Credit accounts you didn't apply for**: loans, credit cards, **power bill** accounts, **buy now pay later**.
- **Missed payments** you didn't know about.
- **Defaults**: accounts sent to debt collection after missed payments.
- **Credit enquiries** you didn't give permission for.

A power account you never opened might sound minor. It's often the first step in building a fake identity history.

## Fixing errors

- **Missed payments or defaults**: contact the **credit provider** or company named. If it's wrong, they **must correct it**.
- **Errors in the report itself** (like your address): contact **each agency**. Centrix takes direct contact; Equifax has a disputes process; Experian requires you to log in.
- Your rights are explained on the **Privacy Commissioner's** website.

## Signs of identity theft

If you see things you don't recognise:

1. Contact the lender and the agency.
2. Place a **credit suppression** ([[nz-credit-suppression-how-to|how]]).
3. Report to Police (105) and get help from **IDCARE** (0800 121 068).

## When to check

- once or twice a year as routine;
- after any breach you're caught in;
- before a home loan application;
- after losing your wallet, licence or passport.

A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows which breaches your email is in, which is a good prompt to pull your reports.

=== FAQ
Q: Can I get a free credit report in New Zealand?
A: Yes, from Centrix, Equifax and Experian. Each has its own process and timeframe.
Q: How long does a free Equifax report take in NZ?
A: Equifax's free option is labelled "Get my report in 10 business days."
Q: What should I look for on my NZ credit report?
A: Accounts you didn't open, missed payments, defaults and credit enquiries you didn't approve.
Q: How do I fix a mistake on my credit report?
A: Contact the credit provider for payment errors, or each agency for errors in the report itself.
