=== TLDR
Equifax Canada has two options: an Identity Alert anyone can add (a statement plus phone number, lasting six years) and a free Fraud Warning for confirmed fraud victims (also six years). In Ontario and Manitoba lenders must call you before extending credit when an alert is on file; elsewhere they're encouraged to. Alerts apply to one bureau only, so contact TransUnion Canada separately. Place Equifax alerts by phone (1-800-465-7166), online or by mail.

=== BODY
## Why alerts matter in Canada

Canadians can now freeze credit at the bureaus in some provinces (see [[how-to-freeze-your-credit-canada|credit freezes in Canada]]), but fraud alerts remain the most widely used protection. An alert tells lenders to check it's really you before opening credit.

## Equifax's two alerts

From [Equifax Canada](https://equifax.ca/personal/education/identity/how-can-i-place-a-fraud-alert-on-my-equifax-credit-report):

| | Identity Alert | Fraud Warning |
|---|---|---|
| Who can add it | Anyone | Confirmed fraud or ID theft victims |
| What it contains | Your statement plus a required phone number | A phone number for lenders |
| Duration | 6 years unless removed in writing | 6 years |
| Cost | Not stated on the page | Free |
| Lender contact | Required in Ontario and Manitoba; encouraged elsewhere | Encouraged |

## How to place an Equifax alert

- **Phone**: 1-800-465-7166 (automated).
- **Online**: Equifax's online form.
- **Mail**: Equifax Canada Co., Consumer Relations Department, Box 190, Montreal, Quebec H1S 2Z2, with photocopies of two IDs.

For a Fraud Warning, call Equifax or submit a dispute; Equifax may open a fraud investigation.

## Don't forget TransUnion

Each alert applies only to that bureau. Contact **TransUnion Canada** separately to add an alert there. Lenders may check either.

## When to add one

- after a breach that exposed your SIN, date of birth or ID ([[canada-sin-stolen-what-to-do|SIN stolen steps]]);
- after a lost wallet or stolen ID;
- after scam calls where you gave out personal details;
- if you see unknown inquiries on your report.

Keep the phone number on your alert current; a lender calling an old number is no protection at all. Updating it is free.

## Pair it with these

Report fraud to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/), and check your exposure with a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]].

=== FAQ
Q: How long does an Equifax fraud alert last in Canada?
A: Both the Identity Alert and the Fraud Warning last six years.
Q: Is the Equifax Fraud Warning free?
A: Yes, for confirmed fraud or identity theft victims.
Q: Do lenders have to call me if I have an alert?
A: In Ontario and Manitoba they must call before extending credit. Elsewhere they're encouraged to.
Q: Does an Equifax alert cover TransUnion?
A: No. Contact TransUnion Canada separately.
