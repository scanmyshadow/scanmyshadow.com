=== TLDR
**Doxxing** is sharing someone's **personal or identifying information without consent**: full name, **home address**, workplace, phone number or family members' details. In New Zealand, posting content that **intentionally causes severe emotional distress** can be illegal under the **Harmful Digital Communications Act (HDCA)**. If it happens: **report** to the platform, **lock down** your profiles, **warn family**, and contact **Netsafe** (free, confidential). If you're threatened or in danger, call **111**.

=== BODY
## What doxxing is

From [Netsafe](https://www.netsafe.org.nz/doxxing/), doxxing is "sharing another person's personal or identifying information without their consent." Doxxers often piece it together from **public online content**, but some **hack accounts** or **guess passwords**.

## The law

Under the **Harmful Digital Communications Act**, sending messages or posting content that **"intentionally causes severe emotional distress"** can be illegal. Netsafe can explain your options under the HDCA.

## What to do right now

1. **Report** the post and the account to the platform.
2. **Screenshot** everything (posts, URLs, dates).
3. **Lock down** privacy settings; set profiles to private; limit who can contact you; consider changing profile names if you're being tracked.
4. **Tell trusted friends and family.** They may be targeted too. Ask them to go private.
5. If it's **safe**, ask the person (or a mutual friend) to remove it.
6. **Contact Netsafe**: free helpline, online reporting, email and live chat.
7. If you're **threatened** or worried about anyone's safety, call **111**.

## Prevent the next one

From Netsafe:

- **Think before sharing** personal details online.
- **Turn off geotagging** on your phone and camera.
- **Search your own name** and details; remove what you can.
- Use **strong, unique passwords** and **two-factor authentication**.
- **Report and block** instead of arguing online.

Searching yourself feels a bit vain. It's actually the most useful ten minutes of privacy work you'll do this year.

## Other places your address leaks

- The **electoral roll**: apply for the [[nz-unpublished-electoral-roll-how-to|unpublished roll]] if you're at risk.
- **Data breaches**: a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows which ones hold your email.
- Organisations holding your data: ask them with an [[nz-privacy-act-access-request-how-to|access request]].

If an organisation leaked your information, you can also [[nz-privacy-commissioner-complaint-how-to|complain to the Privacy Commissioner]].
