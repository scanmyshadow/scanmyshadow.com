=== TLDR
Scammers send **texts and prerecorded calls** saying you have **unpaid fines** and need to pay now, through a **link** or by giving your **card details**. ID Support NSW is clear: "**NSW Government will never ask you to make a payment via a link in a SMS text message.**" Check any fine yourself through Service NSW, never through the message. Report it to Scamwatch and ReportCyber.

=== BODY
## What the scam looks like

According to [ID Support NSW](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/id-support-nsw/learn/scams/threats-and-extortion-fines-and-penalties):

- **SMS texts** or **prerecorded phone messages** claim you have **unpaid fines**.
- The goal is your **credit card details**, through a link, a cold call or a voicemail service.

The texts often use urgent wording: "final notice", "licence suspension", "enforcement action today."

## The rule that settles it

> "NSW Government will never ask you to make a payment via a link in a SMS text message."

So any fine text with a payment link is fake. You don't need to work out whether the fine sounds plausible. The link is the giveaway.

## How to check a fine safely

- Look it up **yourself** with **Service NSW**: type the address or open the official app you already use.
- Use contact details you find independently, never the ones in the message.

## What not to do

- Don't give card details to **cold callers** or **voicemail** services.
- Don't pay through **links in unexpected texts**.
- Don't call back numbers left in recorded messages.

## Where to report

- **ReportCyber** at cyber.gov.au
- **Police Assistance Line**: 131 444
- **Scamwatch**
- Forward the text to ACMA on **0429 999 888** ([[australia-report-spam-sms-acma-0429-999-888|how]])

Collect as much detail as you can (sender number, screenshots, link address) before reporting.

## If you paid

1. Call your bank and cancel the card.
2. Report to ReportCyber and police.
3. Contact IDCARE (1800 595 160) if you gave other personal details.
