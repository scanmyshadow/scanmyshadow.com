=== TLDR
A text says your **bank rewards points are about to expire** and gives a **"redeem" link**. The site looks like your bank and asks for your **login, card number or PIN**. **ANZ** (November 2025 and January 2026), **Westpac** (March 2026) and **NAB** (June 2026) have all warned about it. Banks say they don't send unexpected texts with login links. Check points only in your banking app.

When a scam gets warnings from three of the big four, it's not a one-off. It's a product line.

## Why it works

- **Loss aversion.** "Use it or lose it" hits harder than "you've won something."
- **Real programs.** People do have points, often more than they remember.
- **Small ask.** "Just log in to redeem" feels routine.
- **Not your money... yet.** It feels low-risk, so guards drop.

## What the fake site wants

- your **internet banking login**;
- your **card number** and sometimes **PIN**;
- a **one-time code** to "confirm" redemption.

With those, they can log in, add payees or set up a digital wallet.

## How to check your points safely

Open your **banking app** or type your bank's address yourself. Points that are really expiring will show there. If the app says nothing, the text was lying.

## What banks say they never do

- **ANZ:** never asks you to click a link to log in.
- **Westpac:** never asks you to verify your identity by clicking a link in an SMS.
- **NAB:** never sends unexpected texts with links.

## If you clicked and entered details

1. **Call your bank now** using the number on its website or card.
2. Change your banking password from another device.
3. Ask the bank to check for new payees, devices or wallets.
4. Report the text to your bank and ACMA (**0429 999 888**). See [[australia-report-spam-sms-acma-0429-999-888|reporting guide]].

## Same scam, other places

Airline and retail points get targeted too. See [[canada-loyalty-points-theft-pc-optimum-aeroplan|loyalty points theft]] and [[was-i-affected-by-the-qantas-data-breach|the Qantas breach]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your number is in breach data.

=== FAQ
Q: Is the "rewards points expiring" text from my bank real?
A: ANZ, Westpac and NAB have all warned about scam texts claiming rewards points are expiring. Check your points in your banking app instead.
Q: What happens if I click the rewards points link?
A: It leads to a fake site that asks for your login, card number or PIN.
Q: Do Australian banks send text messages with login links?
A: ANZ, Westpac and NAB all say they don't ask you to log in or verify identity via links in texts.
Q: Where do I report a rewards points scam text?
A: Forward it to your bank's reporting number or address and to ACMA on 0429 999 888.
