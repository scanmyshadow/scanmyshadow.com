=== TLDR
Fake ServiceOntario texts claim you owe outstanding fees and your driver's licence won't be renewed unless you pay now, with a link to a fake site. Police say ServiceOntario “does not send text messages requesting payment or financial details.” An earlier wave offered fake refunds for the scrapped licence plate sticker fees. Don't click; check through ontario.ca and forward the text to 7726.

=== BODY
## The text

According to [Brockville police](https://www.brockvilledaily.ca/news/the-brockville-top-3/scam-warning-issued-after-fake-serviceontario-texts-target-residents), the messages:

- claim you owe **outstanding fees**;
- warn your **driver's licence won't be renewed** unless you pay immediately;
- link to a fake website that collects personal and banking information.

Police said at least **one person** had already fallen victim when they issued the warning in April 2026, [local TV reported](https://hometowntv12.ca/2026/04/08/scam-alert-from-brockville-police-fraudulent-service-ontario-text-message/).

## The earlier refund version

In 2022, less than a week after Ontario announced it was scrapping licence plate sticker fees, scammers texted "refund" links. Ontario said the message was "not authorized by ServiceOntario and is a scam" ([CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/service-ontario-scam-text-refund-1.6367224); [CTV News](https://toronto.ctvnews.ca/it-s-a-scam-ontario-warns-residents-not-to-click-on-link-in-licence-plate-refund-text-1.5799250)).

How the real refunds worked is the tell: they were **$60 to $240**, depending on your last renewal, and went out **by cheque in the mail**. No link, no text, no form. Real policy news, fake messenger: the classic recipe.

## How to check a ServiceOntario message in 30 seconds

| Real ServiceOntario | Scam text |
|---|---|
| Renewals through **ontario.ca** or in person | A link in a text |
| Refunds by **cheque or official account** | "Claim your refund" link |
| No payment requests by text | "Pay now or your licence won't be renewed" |

## What ServiceOntario says

ServiceOntario **does not send text messages requesting payment or financial details**. Renewals and payments happen through ontario.ca or in person.

## What to do

- Don't click, reply or share anything.
- Delete the text and block the sender.
- Forward it to **7726**.
- Check your licence status by typing ontario.ca yourself.
- If you entered card details, call your bank; watch your accounts.
- Report losses to police and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/) (1-888-495-8501).
