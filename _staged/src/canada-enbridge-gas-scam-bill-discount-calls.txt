=== TLDR
Enbridge Gas says it **does not** offer discounts on gas bills, take **credit card payments over the phone**, need your **banking info** to verify your account, call to **demand immediate payment**, or sell gas, appliances or thermostats **door-to-door**. Scammers use fake Enbridge websites, ads for free thermostats and heat pumps, spoofed caller ID and texts. Verify by calling **1-877-362-7434** yourself.

=== BODY
## The scams Enbridge warns about

From [Enbridge Gas's fraud page](https://enbridgegas.com/ontario/contact-us/fraud-awareness):

- **Fake websites and phone numbers** where "agents" offer bill discounts and collect card or account details.
- **Social media ads** for free smart thermostats, energy kits or rebates.
- **Emails offering a free heat pump.**
- **Callers "checking your appliances"** in Ontario.
- **Spoofed caller ID, robocalls and texts** with links.

## What Enbridge never does

| Scam claim | Enbridge reality |
|---|---|
| "We can lower your bill with a discount" | Enbridge does not offer discounts on natural gas bills |
| "Pay by card now over the phone" | Enbridge doesn't take card payments over the phone |
| "Give your bank info to verify your account" | Not required |
| "Pay immediately or we disconnect" | Enbridge doesn't call to demand immediate payment |
| "We're here to sell or service your furnace" | Enbridge doesn't sell, rent or service appliances or thermostats door-to-door |
| "Show me your bill and sign here" | Representatives don't ask to see your bill or sign contracts |

The "discount" pitch is the sneakiest. Nobody gets suspicious when someone offers to *save* them money. That's the point.

## Who's really at your door

- Real employees and authorized contractors carry **visible photo ID**.
- Expect visits only for **booked appointments** or in areas getting **new gas service**.
- Not sure? Close the door and call **1-877-362-7434**.

## If you already paid or shared details

1. Call your **bank or card company** and watch statements.
2. Call **Enbridge Gas** at 1-877-362-7434.
3. Report to **local police** and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/).
4. If you ever feel physically threatened at the door, call **911**.
