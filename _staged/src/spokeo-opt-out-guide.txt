=== TLDR
Find your Spokeo profile URL, paste it with your email at spokeo.com/optout, complete the CAPTCHA, then click the link in the “Spokeo Directory Removal Confirmation” email. Spokeo says removal takes 2 to 3 days, but listings are often gone within an hour. Do one request per listing.

=== BODY
## What Spokeo collects

Spokeo pulls together names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, relatives and social profiles into one page. It's popular with people checking a date, a seller or an old classmate, and also with people you'd rather not hear from.

## Spokeo runs more sites than you think

California's data broker registry (an August 2026 snapshot compiled by [Offlist](https://www.offlist.me/ca-broker/spokeo-inc)) lists **Spokeo, Inc.** of Pasadena as doing business as:

| Site | Covered by spokeo.com/optout? |
|---|---|
| Spokeo.com | Yes |
| AnyWho.com | Yes |
| Family.me | Yes |
| Freepeopledirectory.com | Yes |
| Peoplewin.com | Yes |
| **ThatsThem.com** | **No**: it has its own form ([[thatsthem-opt-out-guide|ThatsThem guide]]) |

So one Spokeo opt-out does more than it looks, but ThatsThem still needs its own request.

In its 2024 registry filing, Spokeo reported receiving almost **3.8 million opt-out-of-sale requests**. You're far from the only person doing this.

## Step by step

These steps follow [DuckDuckGo's Spokeo opt-out guide](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/spokeo-opt-out).

1. **Find your profile URL quickly.** Spokeo's normal search can be slow. Instead, type a URL in this format: spokeo.com/firstname-lastname/full-state-name/full-city-name. On the results, don't click your listing; right-click your name and copy the link. It should end in a long ID string.
2. **Submit the request.** Go to [spokeo.com/optout](https://www.spokeo.com/optout), paste the profile URL, enter your email, complete the reCAPTCHA and click “Opt Out.”
3. **Confirm by email.** Open the email titled “Spokeo Directory Removal Confirmation” and click the verification link. A green notice confirms Spokeo received the request.

## How long it takes

Spokeo says 2 to 3 days. In practice, DuckDuckGo found listings are typically removed within about an hour. Search engines can keep showing the cached page for a few days to several weeks.

## Common snags

- **Multiple listings.** If you've moved or used a different name, you may have several profiles. Each needs its own request.
- **No email arrives.** Check spam and promotions folders. Use an email you can access but don't mind sharing; an [[email-alias-temporary-phone-number-privacy|email alias]] works well.
- **Spokeo doesn't cover other sites.** As ThatsThem's guidance notes, Spokeo's removal doesn't extend to other brokers. You still need to do the others.

## California residents: add DROP

California's **Delete Request and Opt-out Platform** (DROP) sends one deletion request to every registered broker, and Spokeo is registered. [Malwarebytes explains](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/08/californians-can-tell-data-brokers-to-drop-their-informatio) that from **1 August 2026** registered brokers must check DROP and have **90 days** to delete your records. Verify residency (manually or with Login.gov), submit once, and keep your **DROP ID**. Still do the Spokeo form above: it's faster. Our [[california-drop-data-broker-delete-request-guide|DROP guide]] has the details.

Outside California? Oregon, Texas and Vermont require broker registration but have no one-stop tool, so you still go site by site.

=== FAQ
Q: Does a Spokeo opt-out also remove me from AnyWho?
A: Spokeo's registry entry lists AnyWho, Family.me, Freepeopledirectory and Peoplewin as Spokeo sites covered by spokeo.com/optout. ThatsThem, also a Spokeo site, needs its own request.
Q: How do I opt out of Spokeo?
A: Copy your profile URL, submit it with your email at spokeo.com/optout, complete the CAPTCHA and click the link in the confirmation email.
Q: How long does Spokeo take to remove a listing?
A: Spokeo says 2 to 3 days, but listings are often removed within an hour.
Q: Does a Spokeo opt-out remove me from other sites?
A: No. Each people-search site needs its own opt-out request.
Q: Do I need to create a Spokeo account?
A: No. You only need the profile URL and an email address to confirm.
