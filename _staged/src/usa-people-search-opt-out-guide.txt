=== BODY
## What it is

USA People Search is one of the many free-to-search, pay-for-more people finders. Results typically show name, age, current and past addresses, phone numbers and relatives. It's part of the long tail of sites you'll find when you [[how-to-google-yourself-digital-footprint-audit|google yourself]] properly.

## Step by step

From [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually):

1. Go to **usa-people-search.com/manage**.
2. Enter your email address to start.
3. Open the confirmation email and click the link.
4. Enter the personal information you want removed and submit.

Timeline: **within 3 days** of submission.

## Tips

- Use an email you can access; an [[email-alias-temporary-phone-number-privacy|alias]] keeps your main inbox out of broker databases.
- Enter details that match the listing (name variants, old addresses) so the right record is removed.
- Search again in a private window after three days.
- Recheck every few months; data brokers refresh.

## A second walkthrough, and what the site shows

DeleteMe's [USA People Search guide](https://joindeleteme.com/opt-out-guides/how-to-remove-yourself-from-usa-people-search/) (updated September 2026) describes the route from the site footer:

1. Click **"Do Not Sell My Personal Information"** at the bottom of usa-people-search.com.
2. Enter your email, tick the two boxes, complete the CAPTCHA and click **"Begin Removal Process"**.
3. Search your **full name, city and state**, click **"View Details"** on your listing, then **"Remove Record"**.
4. Click the link in the confirmation email. It **expires after 24 hours**, so do it straight away.

The site says removals are processed within **72 hours**. A free listing typically shows your **name and aliases, age, current and past phone numbers and addresses**, and **relatives and associates**. It links to a paid site for full background reports.

DeleteMe also notes that if you're on USA People Search you're probably on **411.com** too, and that records can **reappear** as the site re-collects public data. Put a reminder in your calendar to search again in three months.
