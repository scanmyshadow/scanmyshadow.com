=== BODY
## What it is

USA People Search is one of the many free-to-search, pay-for-more people finders. Results typically show name, age, current and past addresses, phone numbers and relatives. It's part of the long tail of sites you'll find when you [[how-to-google-yourself-digital-footprint-audit|google yourself]] properly.

## Step by step

From [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually):

1. Go to **usa-people-search.com/manage**.
2. Enter your email address to start.
3. Open the confirmation email and click the link.
4. Enter the personal information you want removed and submit.

Timeline: **within 3 days** of submission.

## Tips

- Use an email you can access; an [[email-alias-temporary-phone-number-privacy|alias]] keeps your main inbox out of broker databases.
- Enter details that match the listing (name variants, old addresses) so the right record is removed.
- Search again in a private window after three days.
- Recheck every few months; data brokers refresh.
