=== TLDR
“Nottingham Knockers” are doorstep sellers, usually young men, who claim to be on a probation or rehabilitation scheme and sell cheap, poor-quality goods. Police forces across the UK warn they often target older or vulnerable people and may be scoping homes for burglary; some have become aggressive. Which? flagged them again in August 2026. Don't open the door, don't engage, note details and call 101 (or 999 if threatened).

=== BODY
## Who they are

[North Wales Police](https://wrexham.com/news/police-warn-of-door-to-door-nottingham-knockers-scam-targeting-residents-280182.html) describes young men going door to door claiming to be part of a probation or rehabilitation scheme, often showing fake ID, and selling cheap, poor-quality goods.

[Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/the-latest-scam-alerts-from-which-aBRLy2b02WkC) also warned in August 2026 that callers raising money, running rehabilitation schemes or selling cheap goods may be rogue traders or gathering information on older or vulnerable residents.

## The real risk

It isn't the overpriced household goods. Police say the visits may be used to work out who lives alone, who has cash or valuables, and which homes are easy targets. In some areas sellers have become violent or forced their way in.

## How to handle the doorstep

- **Don't open the door.** Speak through a closed door or window if needed.
- **Don't engage** or buy anything.
- **Note details**: description, direction they went, vehicle registration.
- **Report** to police on **101** or online. If you feel threatened, call **999**.
- **Tell neighbours**, especially older ones.

A door chain or video doorbell helps, though secure the doorbell's privacy settings ([[how-to-secure-a-smart-doorbell-camera|how]]).

## Related doorstep scams

Rogue roofers and “your tiles are loose” builders are a bigger money risk; see the [[uk-roofing-gang-jailed-63-years-1m-fraud-older-homeowners|roofing gang jailed for £1m fraud]]. Many councils run “no cold calling” zones and Trading Standards schemes you can join.

## The online equivalent
