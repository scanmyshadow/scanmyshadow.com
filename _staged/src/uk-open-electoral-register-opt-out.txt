=== TLDR
The UK has two electoral registers: the full register (used for elections and some checks) and the open register, which anyone can buy. People-finder sites like 192.com use the open register. Opting out is free: tick the box when you register to vote, or contact your council's electoral services by phone or email. You stay on the full register and keep your vote.

=== BODY
## Two registers, very different audiences

When you register to vote you go on the **full register**, which is used for elections, jury service and by credit reference agencies. Unless you opt out, you also go on the **open register** (sometimes called the edited register), which can be sold to any person, company or organisation. That's how your name and address end up in marketing lists and people-finder sites such as [[uk-192-com-remove-your-details|192.com]].

## How to opt out

Three ways, using [Waltham Forest Council's process](https://www.walthamforest.gov.uk/council-and-elections/elections-and-voting/registering-vote/how-opt-out-open-register) as a typical example:

1. **When you register to vote**, choose to opt out of the open register.
2. **By phone** to your council's electoral services team.
3. **By email** to electoral services with “Opt Out” in the subject, your full name and address, and a clear statement that you want to opt out of the open register.

Waltham Forest confirms by email within five working days. Your council's process will be similar; search “[your council] open register opt out”. In Northern Ireland, contact the Electoral Office for Northern Ireland.

## What changes, and what doesn't

**Changes:** future editions of the open register won't include you, so new buyers won't get your details.

**Doesn't change:**

- copies already sold or already published on people-finder sites;
- your right to vote;
- credit checks, which use the full register.

So pair the opt-out with removal requests on sites that already list you.

## Who should definitely do this

- anyone who has moved to get away from someone;
- people in public-facing jobs (teachers, nurses, police, landlords);
- anyone tired of junk mail.

For stronger protection, some people can register anonymously. Ask your council about anonymous registration if your safety is at risk.

=== FAQ
Q: What is the open register?
A: An edited version of the electoral register that can be bought by any person, company or organisation.
Q: How do I opt out of the open register?
A: Tick the opt-out when you register to vote, or contact your council's electoral services by phone or email.
Q: Will opting out affect my credit score or vote?
A: No. You stay on the full register, which is used for elections and credit checks.
Q: Will it remove me from 192.com?
A: Not on its own. It stops future open register copies; request removal from 192.com separately.
