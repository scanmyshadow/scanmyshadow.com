=== TLDR
If your **New Zealand passport** details were exposed in a **data breach or scam**, use form **PAS201** to choose: **(1)** add an **alert** and keep travelling on it until it expires, **(2)** add an alert **and cancel** it, or **(3)** cancel **without** an alert. The alert triggers **extra checks** if your passport details show up in a **passport, citizenship or RealMe Verified Identity** application. Want a new passport straight away? Use the normal application form.

=== BODY
## Your three options

From the [Passport Office's PAS201 form](https://www.passports.govt.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0027/54756/PAS201-Declare-your-New-Zealand-passport-or-travel-document-lost,-stolen,-damaged-or-involved-in-a-data-breach-or-scam-251128.pdf):

| Option | Can you still travel? | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Alert, keep passport** | Yes, until expiry | Extra security checks if your details appear in a passport, citizenship or **RealMe Verified Identity** application |
| **Alert and cancel** | No | Alert + passport cancelled |
| **Cancel, no alert** | No | Passport cancelled |

For most breach victims, **alert and keep** is the sweet spot: protection without wrecking your holiday plans.

## How to apply

- Fill in PAS201 online or by hand, then **print and sign** it.
- **Describe what happened** (where, what was exposed).
- **Adding an alert only?** Email it to **passportsnz@dia.govt.nz**.
- **Cancelling?** **Post** the passport with the form (addresses for NZ, Australia and the UK are on the form).
- A parent or guardian signs for anyone **15 or under**.

## Want a new passport now?

PAS201 doesn't issue one. Use the regular **adult (PAS310)** or **child (PAS305)** application.

=== FAQ
Q: What should I do if my NZ passport was in a data breach?
A: Use form PAS201 to add an alert, cancel the passport, or both.
Q: Can I still travel if I add an alert to my NZ passport?
A: Yes. You can add an alert and keep using the passport until it expires.
Q: What does a passport alert do?
A: It triggers extra security checks if your passport details appear in a passport, citizenship or RealMe Verified Identity application.
Q: Does PAS201 give me a new passport?
A: No. Use a separate adult or child passport application for a new one.
