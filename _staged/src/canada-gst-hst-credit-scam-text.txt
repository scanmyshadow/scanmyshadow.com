=== TLDR
The CRA says it does **not** send texts or emails about benefits or tax credits with links to click. It only texts for **multi-factor authentication** codes, and only if you chose that option. It doesn't use WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger. So any text about your **GST/HST credit**, Disability Tax Credit, Home Accessibility Tax Credit or **OAS** with a link, a deadline or a code is a scam. Check benefits by typing canada.ca yourself.

=== BODY
## The scam messages

The [CRA's scam page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/scams-fraud/recognize-scam.html) specifically warns about messages:

- about the **GST/HST credit**, with **urgent deadlines** and links to "application forms";
- about the **Home Accessibility Tax Credit**, **Disability Tax Credit** and **Multigenerational Home Renovation Tax Credit**;
- about **Old Age Security**, sometimes with a link **and a code**.

The CRA's rule: it does not send texts or emails about benefits or tax credits with links asking you to click them.

## How the CRA actually contacts you

| Channel | What the CRA does | What it never does |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | MFA codes only, if you chose phone | Refunds, payments, account fixes, links |
| **Email** | Notice of a new message in your CRA account, or a link you asked for on a call | Links asking for personal or financial info; emails from a named person asking you to reply |
| **Phone** | May ask you to verify identity | Demand e-Transfer, crypto, prepaid or gift cards; threaten arrest or deportation; charge a fee |
| **WhatsApp / Messenger** | Never | Never |

So the honest answer to "does the CRA send text messages?" is: only six-digit codes you asked for.

## Why benefit scams work

People expect GST/HST credit payments every quarter, so a text about "your credit" doesn't feel odd. Add a deadline ("apply by Friday or lose your payment") and a link to a perfect copy of the CRA sign-in page, and the scam writes itself. The fake page collects your CRA login, SIN and bank details, and then someone changes your direct deposit.

## How to check safely

- Sign in to **CRA My Account** by typing the address yourself.
- Real addresses start with **canada.ca** or end with **cra-arc.gc.ca**.
- For benefits, check official Government of Canada or provincial pages.
- For calls, hang up and call a CRA number you find yourself.

## If you clicked and entered details

1. Sign in to CRA My Account directly and check your **direct deposit** and **address**.
2. Call the CRA using an official number.
3. Read our guide to a [[canada-cra-my-account-compromised-what-to-do|compromised CRA My Account]].
4. If you gave your SIN, see [[canada-sin-stolen-what-to-do|SIN stolen: what to do]].

## Where to report

- The CRA's reporting page or **reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca**
- Local police and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/)
- Forward the text to **7726** ([[canada-7726-spam-text-reporting-guide|how]])
