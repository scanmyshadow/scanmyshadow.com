=== BODY
## Why Premium Bonds are perfect bait

Millions of Britons hold Premium Bonds, prizes are drawn monthly, and “you've won” is the most pleasant sentence in English. A fake prize message only needs to reach a holder at the right moment. Older savers are particularly targeted.

## What NS&I says about its texts

From [NS&I's text scam page](https://www.nsandi.com/get-to-know-us/security/text-message-scams):

- Genuine texts use the sender ID **“NSandI”** and sign off **“NS&I.”**
- NS&I **never sends a link in a text that goes directly to its log-in page**.
- It won't ask you to enter your online banking details through a text.

NS&I also has guidance on [phone call scams](https://www.nsandi.com/get-to-know-us/security/phone-call-scams).

## Common fakes

- “Congratulations, your Premium Bond has won £25,000. Claim here.”
- “Your NS&I account has been suspended. Verify your details.”
- Calls offering “guaranteed” NS&I-style bonds with better rates (often clone investment firms).
- Messages from a supposed family member asking you to cash in bonds and send money.

## How to check a prize

Use the prize checker on nsandi.com or in the NS&I app, typed or opened yourself. Real winnings are paid to your nominated account or reinvested; you don't pay a fee to receive them. Any request for a release fee or tax upfront is a scam ([[what-is-advance-fee-fraud|advance fee fraud]]).

## Report it

NS&I's steps:

1. **Stop**: don't reply, click or pay.
2. **Verify** the sender using a number you already know is correct.
3. **Forward** the text to **7726**.
4. **Report** to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/).
5. If you think you've been scammed, **call NS&I immediately**.

## Bond-style investment scams

Some scammers offer “government-backed” or “NS&I partner” bonds at high rates. Check any firm on the FCA Firm Checker first ([[uk-fca-firm-checker-clone-firms-fca-impersonation|how]]). A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows if your email is in breach lists sold to these callers.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I know a text from NS&I is real?
A: NS&I says genuine texts use the sender ID "NSandI," sign off "NS&I," and never link directly to its log-in page.
Q: Will NS&I text me about a Premium Bonds prize with a link?
A: Check prizes yourself on nsandi.com or the app. NS&I won't send a text link straight to its log-in page.
Q: Do I need to pay a fee to receive a Premium Bonds prize?
A: No. Any fee request is a scam.
Q: How do I report a fake NS&I text?
A: Forward it to 7726 and report it to Report Fraud.
