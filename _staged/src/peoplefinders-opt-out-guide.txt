=== BODY
## The catch

Most people-search sites remove anyone who asks. PeopleFinders, according to [Redact's guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/peoplefinders-com/remove), only honours opt-outs from residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws. The list it gives: California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia.

Live in Florida, New York or Illinois? You may get a polite no. Privacy in the US still depends on your postcode, sorry, ZIP code. See our [[us-state-privacy-laws-guide|state privacy law guide]].

## Step by step

1. Go to [peoplefinders.com/opt-out](https://www.peoplefinders.com/opt-out) (the “Do Not Sell My Personal Information” page). In the **Right to Opt-out** section click **Next** and fill in the basic fields.
2. Open the email from support@peoplefinders.com, “Complete your peoplefinders.com Privacy Request”, and click the link **within 24 hours**.
3. Fill in the **Record Suppression Form**: first name, last name and street address are required; email, phone, date of birth and middle name help matching. Avoid special characters.
4. Wait for an acceptance or rejection email.

Removal is claimed within three days if accepted. Check in a private window after 72 hours; if you're still listed at day seven despite an acceptance, start again.

## If you're rejected

- **Appeal** through the “Your Privacy Rights” section of PeopleFinders' privacy notice, naming the law you think applies, or call (800) 718-8997.
- **Try the FastPeopleSearch route.** DuckDuckGo's guide says removing a listing from FastPeopleSearch also removes it from related sites including PeopleFinders. Our [[fastpeoplesearch-opt-out-guide|FastPeopleSearch guide]] takes five minutes, then check PeopleFinders again.

A: Use the Right to Opt-out section at peoplefinders.com/opt-out, confirm the email within 24 hours and complete the Record Suppression Form.
Q: Why was my PeopleFinders opt-out rejected?
A: PeopleFinders only honours opt-outs from residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws.
Q: Which states qualify?
A: Redact lists California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia.
