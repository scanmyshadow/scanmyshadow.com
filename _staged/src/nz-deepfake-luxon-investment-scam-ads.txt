=== TLDR
Taranaki grandmother **Jill Creasy, 72**, lost **$224,000** of retirement savings after seeing a **deepfake video of PM Christopher Luxon** on **Facebook** recommending crypto. The FMA has warned about **fake news stories** using **Luxon, Winston Peters, Jacinda Ardern and Clarke Gayford**. The pattern: fake article → sign-up form → "broker" calls → small deposit (about **$420**) → bigger deposits → fees to withdraw → **recovery scam**.

=== BODY
## Jill's story

From [Newstalk ZB / NZ Herald](https://newstalkzb.co.nz/news/national/scammers-who-used-christopher-luxon-deepfake-video-to-steal-224-000-from-pensioner-caught-on-secret-recording) (November 2024):

- Jill saw an **AI-generated video** of Luxon apparently recommending Kiwis invest in **crypto**.
- She was persuaded to buy **Bitcoin** and lost **$224,000**.
- Months later, the "advisers" called back to **win her trust again**, asking for **remote access** to "return" nearly half a million dollars, and **coaching her** on what to tell **TSB** about a $10,000 payment.
- She **secretly recorded** them and gave the audio to the Herald, which passed it to authorities.

Meta said it's using facial recognition against "celeb-bait scams." Jill called scams a "crime of shame." She spoke up anyway. Respect.

## The FMA's warning

From the [FMA](https://fma.govt.nz/library/articles/deepfake-video-scam-warning-fake-news-stories-political-endorsements/) (August 2024):

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Paid **ads** link to **fake news articles** styled like NZ media |
| 2 | False quotes and endorsements from **Luxon, Peters, Ardern, Gayford** |
| 3 | Scary hooks like "**pension payments will be cancelled**" |
| 4 | Registration form → a "**broker**" calls |
| 5 | Small first deposit (about **NZ$420**), then bigger ones |
| 6 | Withdrawals blocked by "**fees**" or "**taxes**" |
| 7 | Later, **recovery scams** target the same victims |

## How to spot it

- News-style content with **politicians** or **deepfake videos** promoting investments
- Headlines that **scare** you about super or pensions
- Links to **unfamiliar trading platforms**
- **Pressure** to deposit quickly; **fees** before withdrawals
- Anyone coaching you on **what to tell your bank**

That last one is the loudest alarm there is.

## What to do if you've been scammed

From the FMA:

1. **Call your bank** immediately; ask about reversing transactions.
2. If you installed **remote access** software, get a professional to check your device.
3. Report repeated scam calls to your **telco**, and scam texts to **DIA** (7726).
4. **Tell someone you trust.**
5. **Victim Support**: **0800 842 846**.
