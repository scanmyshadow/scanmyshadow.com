=== TLDR
Just under 400,000 UK passports are reported lost or stolen each year, and people wait an average of 73 days to report it, according to Action Fraud. A passport holds everything a fraudster needs to open accounts in your name. Report it to HM Passport Office online straight away: the passport is cancelled, shared with the National Crime Agency within 24 hours and flagged on Interpol's lost and stolen document database. Then protect your credit file.

=== BODY
## The numbers

From an [Action Fraud campaign](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/news/a-lost-passport-could-lead-to-identity-crime):

- about **50 million** people hold a UK passport;
- **just under 400,000** are reported lost or stolen each year;
- people wait an average of **73 days** before reporting it.

Seventy-three days is a long time to leave a fraudster holding your photo, full name, date of birth and signature.

## What fraudsters can do with it

- open bank accounts or apply for credit in your name;
- pass ID checks for phone contracts or rentals;
- use it to try to cross borders.

We'd never wait 73 days to cancel a lost bank card. A passport deserves the same urgency.

## What to do, in order

1. **Report it to HM Passport Office online** (you can report all losses and thefts this way). The passport is cancelled.
2. Your details are **shared with the National Crime Agency within 24 hours** and recorded on **Interpol's Stolen and Lost Travel Document database**.
3. **If stolen**, consider reporting the theft to police too, especially if other ID went with it.
4. **Check your credit reports** at all three agencies ([[uk-free-statutory-credit-report-how-to-get|free statutory reports]]).
5. **Consider [[uk-cifas-protective-registration-explained|Cifas Protective Registration]]**, designed for exactly this kind of risk.
6. **Watch for scam calls** claiming to be HM Passport Office or police asking for fees or details.

A cancelled passport can't be used again, even if you find it in the sofa next week. Factor that into your travel plans.

## If it was a photo, not the passport

Sending a photo of your passport to a “landlord,” “employer” or “prize company” carries similar risk. See our [[how-to-report-identity-theft-uk|UK identity theft guide]] and [[uk-spareroom-flatshare-rental-scams|rental scam red flags]].

Passport scans also turn up in data breaches. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows if your email is in known breaches.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I report a lost UK passport?
A: Report it to HM Passport Office online. It will be cancelled.
Q: What happens after I report it?
A: It's cancelled, shared with the National Crime Agency within 24 hours and recorded on Interpol's lost and stolen documents database.
Q: Can I use my passport if I find it after reporting it lost?
A: No. Once reported, it's cancelled and can't be used.
Q: How can I protect myself from identity fraud after losing my passport?
A: Check your credit reports, consider Cifas Protective Registration and watch for scam calls.
