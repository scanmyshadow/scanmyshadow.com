=== TLDR
On **27 July 2026**, the NDIA said cyber criminals **used myGov to access some NDIS accounts**, through **phishing** and by persuading people to **hand over sign-in details**. The NDIS "**will never ask for your personal details by SMS**." Participants, nominees and carers should **check plan funds for payments you didn't request**, turn on **MFA**, and never share codes. NDIS: **1800 800 110**; IDCARE: **1800 595 160**.

=== BODY
## What happened

From the [NDIS news update](https://www.ndis.gov.au/news/11611-phishing-scams) (27 July 2026):

- Criminals accessed **some NDIS accounts through myGov**.
- They got in via **phishing**, and by **persuading people to voluntarily give up sign-in details**.
- The NDIA is working with **Services Australia**, which runs myGov.

## Why NDIS accounts are targeted

NDIS plans hold **funds for supports**. A scammer with access can try to claim for **services that were never provided**. Participants may also rely on carers or nominees to manage accounts, which gives scammers more people to trick.

## What the NDIS never does

> "The NDIS will never ask for your personal details by SMS."

Don't share passwords, **SMS authentication codes** or personal information with anyone you don't know or trust.

## What participants and nominees should check

- **Plan funds:** look for payments for services you didn't request or receive.
- **Bank accounts:** look for anything unusual.
- **myGov:** check sign-in history and remove unknown devices ([[australia-mygov-account-compromised-what-to-do|full myGov steps]]).

## Protect your account

1. Turn on **multi-factor authentication**; passkeys or Digital ID are best.
2. **Change passwords** and don't reuse them.
3. Be cautious with **unexpected emails, texts, messaging app chats and calls**.
4. **Don't click** suspicious links. Type my.gov.au yourself.

## Who to contact

- **NDIS:** 1800 800 110 (8am–8pm AEST business days)
- **IDCARE:** 1800 595 160 for personalised support
- **Scamwatch** and **ReportCyber** to report

## For carers and support workers

If you help someone manage their NDIS, talk with them about codes: **nobody from the NDIS or myGov will ask for one**. A simple fridge note ("Never share a code") can do more than a long briefing.
