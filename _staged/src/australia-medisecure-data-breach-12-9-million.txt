=== TLDR
The **MediSecure** breach (disclosed **May 2024**) affected about **12.9 million** people. Exposed: **personal and health information relating to prescriptions** distributed through MediSecure's systems up to **November 2023**, plus **Medicare card details** (numbers, individual identifiers, expiry dates). MediSecure later went into administration. Your Medicare account **can't be accessed with card details alone**, and you don't need to act on concession cards. Your biggest risk is health-themed scam calls.

=== BODY
## What happened

MediSecure, an electronic prescription delivery service, told the government about **12.9 million** people may be affected. It's one of the largest breaches in Australian history ([Reuters factbox](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/24/factbox-openai-data-breach-latest-in-long-list-of-hacks-in-australia)), and the company later went into administration.

## What was exposed

According to [ID Support NSW](https://www.nsw.gov.au/id-support-nsw/learn/data-breaches/data-breach-announcements/medisecure-data-breach):

- **Personal and health information relating to prescriptions** distributed by MediSecure up to **November 2023**;
- **Medicare card details**: numbers, individual identifiers and expiry dates;
- healthcare provider information.

The current national prescription delivery service **wasn't** affected. Paper and e-scripts work as normal.

## What you don't need to worry about

- Services Australia says you **don't need to act** on Pensioner Concession, Health Care or Commonwealth Seniors Health cards.
- Your **Medicare online account can't be accessed with card details alone**.

## What you should watch for

Scammers love health data because it sounds personal. Expect:

- calls "from your pharmacy" or "from Medicare" mentioning a medication;
- "compensation for the MediSecure breach" offers;
- fake insurer calls about a claim.

**Hang up and call back** on a number you find yourself.

## What to do

1. **Report misuse** to ReportCyber at cyber.gov.au.
2. Turn on **multi-factor authentication** for myGov and email.
3. Use **strong, unique passphrases**.
4. **NSW residents:** ID Support NSW offers free help on **1800 001 040**.
5. Elsewhere: **IDCARE** on **1800 595 160**.

Health data stays sensitive for life, so this is one to remember. See also [[was-i-affected-by-the-medibank-data-breach|Medibank]] and [[australia-genea-ivf-data-breach|Genea]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows the breaches tied to your email.

=== FAQ
Q: How many people were affected by the MediSecure breach?
A: About 12.9 million people, according to MediSecure.
Q: Was my Medicare card number exposed?
A: Medicare card details, including numbers, individual identifiers and expiry dates, were exposed for affected people.
Q: Can someone access my Medicare account with my card number?
A: No. Services Australia says the Medicare account can't be accessed with card details alone.
Q: Do I need to replace my concession card?
A: No. Services Australia says no action is needed on concession or Commonwealth Seniors cards.
