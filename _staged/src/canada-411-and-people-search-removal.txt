=== BODY
## Where Canada411 gets your listing

Directory listings in Canada flow from the phone company that provides your landline. So the fix starts there, not with the directory. According to [Cogeco's support page](https://support.cogeco.ca/hc/en-ca/articles/360059892993-How-do-I-remove-my-name-and-Home-Phone-number-from-the-directory), subscribing to its **Unlisted Name and Phone Number** feature removes your listing from:

- directory assistance;
- the printed White Pages;
- **canada411.ca**.

Other providers (Bell, Telus, Rogers, Vidéotron and others) offer the same kind of unlisted option. Ask yours; there may be a monthly fee.

## What going unlisted doesn't do

- **Call Display**: people you call still see your name and number. Dial **\*67** before a call, or ask for permanent Call Display blocking.
- **Other directories**: some online directories don't get their data from your phone company, so you'll have to contact them directly.
- **People-search sites**: listings already copied elsewhere stay until you remove them.

## Your rights under PIPEDA

Canadian organisations covered by PIPEDA must let you access your personal information and challenge its accuracy, and you can withdraw consent in many cases. If a Canadian company won't remove your data, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. See [[canada-pipeda-explained|PIPEDA explained]].

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove my number from Canada411?
A: Ask your phone company for an unlisted name and number. Cogeco says this removes you from canada411.ca, directory assistance and the White Pages.
Q: Is an unlisted number free in Canada?
A: It depends on the provider; some charge a monthly fee. Ask your phone company.
Q: Will going unlisted hide my number when I call people?
A: No. Use *67 per call or ask for permanent Call Display blocking.
Q: Does it remove me from people-search websites?
A: No. Contact those sites directly to remove your listing.
