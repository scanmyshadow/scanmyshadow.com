=== TLDR
In **November 2025**, a fake social media ad used **ANZ's name and logo** to offer a **stock market education programme**, then funnelled people into **"investment advice" chat groups** on another platform. **ANZ doesn't offer stock trading education**; its real ads come from **ANZ Aotearoa** with a **verified blue tick**. ANZ says investment scams were about **20%** of cases for scammed customers aged **66–74**. Once in the group, people are pressured to invest.

=== BODY
## What happened

From [ANZ's 20 November 2025 warning](https://www.anz.com.au/newsroom/new-zealand/2025/11/New-Zealanders-warned-to-be-wary-of-new-wave-of-investment-scams/):

- A fake ad used ANZ's **name and logo**.
- It offered a **stock market education programme**.
- It directed people to **investment advice chat groups** on another platform.
- ANZ **doesn't** offer stock trading education. Real ANZ ads come from **ANZ Aotearoa** with a **verified blue tick**.
- The ads were reported for removal; ANZ and the **FMA** are monitoring.

## How group-chat scams work

ANZ's Alan Thomsen: "Once people join these group chats, they are typically pressured to invest in what is a scam."

The playbook: a friendly "professor" or "mentor", a chat full of members posting big wins (all fake), a "trading platform" showing paper profits, then fees when you try to withdraw. It's a stage play where you're the only audience member who paid.

## Red flags

- Investment ads featuring **well-known business leaders** or commentators
- **High** or **low-risk** returns that sound too good to be true
- **Pressure** to invest fast
- **Unregistered** promoters
- Offers to **recover** money you've already lost

## What to do

1. Check the promoter is registered with the **Companies Office** or **FMA** ([[nz-fma-fake-nz-registered-investment-firms|how]]).
2. Contact providers using a number from their **official website**.
3. Get advice from a **licensed financial adviser**.
4. Already invested? Stop paying, call your bank (ANZ: 0800 269 296), report to Police (105).
