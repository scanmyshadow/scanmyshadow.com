=== BODY
## What USPhonebook is

A free reverse phone lookup site. Type a number, get a name, and often an address and relatives. That's handy if you're checking a missed call, and less handy if it's your number someone is looking up. It's run by the same company as FastPeopleSearch and PeopleSearchNow, per Redact.

## Step by step

From [Redact's USPhonebook guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/usphonebook-com/remove):

1. Go to **usphonebook.com/removal**. Leave “I am” set to “The subject of the request”, enter your name and email, tick the authorisation box, solve the reCAPTCHA and click “Begin Removal Process.”
2. A “You're not done” page appears. That's normal.
3. Find the email from support@usphonebook.com titled “Complete your usphonebook.com Privacy Request” (check spam). Click “Click here to fill out the form.” **The link expires after 24 hours.**
4. Fill in your date of birth, phone, street address, city, state and ZIP. Confirm your email, tick the review box, solve the second reCAPTCHA and click “Confirm Removal Process.” You can't edit after submitting.
5. Screenshot the confirmation, which says removal happens in “3 days or less.”

## Check it worked

After three days, use a private window and search by name and by phone number. If you're still listed, run the process again; if it persists after another week, Redact suggests calling (888) 747-4095.

## Don't assume the siblings are covered

The source doesn't confirm one removal covers FastPeopleSearch or PeopleSearchNow, so do them separately. Our [[fastpeoplesearch-opt-out-guide|FastPeopleSearch guide]] takes five minutes.
