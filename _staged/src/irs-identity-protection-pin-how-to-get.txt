=== TLDR
An IRS Identity Protection PIN is a six-digit number that blocks anyone else from filing a federal tax return with your Social Security number or ITIN. Anyone who can verify their identity can get one, fastest through an IRS online account. You get a new one every year (viewable online mid-January to mid-November). The IRS never asks for your IP PIN by phone, email or text.

=== BODY
## Why bother

Tax identity theft is simple: a criminal with your name, date of birth and SSN files a return before you and pockets the refund. You find out when your real return is rejected. If your SSN has been in a breach (and after the last few years, that's a lot of people), an IP PIN is the free fix. Nobody can e-file with your SSN without it.

## Who can get one

According to the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin), anyone with an SSN or ITIN who can verify their identity. Parents and guardians can request one for dependents.

## Three ways to apply

| Route | Who it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|
| IRS online account (IP PIN section of your profile) | Anyone 18+ who can verify identity online | Immediate |
| Form 15227 | If you can't verify online and your AGI is under $84,000 (single) or $168,000 (married filing jointly) | By mail, usually 4–6 weeks |
| In person at a Taxpayer Assistance Center | Anyone; bring a photo ID plus one other ID (two IDs for a dependent) | By mail, usually about 3 weeks |

Under-18s must use one of the alternative options.

## The yearly routine

- You get a **new IP PIN every calendar year**.
- If you enrolled online, the IRS **doesn't mail** it; log in each year to get it (generally available mid-January to mid-November).
- Give it to your tax software or preparer when prompted. It's needed on Forms 1040, 1040-NR, 1040-PR, 1040-SR and 1040-SS, including prior-year returns filed during the year.
- A wrong or missing IP PIN means an e-filed return is rejected or a paper return is delayed.

Lost it? View it online or request a reissue by mail; don't file a new Form 15227.

## The scam to watch for

The IRS says it will **never ask for your IP PIN**. Calls, emails or texts asking for it are scams. Our guide to [[irs-scam-what-to-know|IRS scams]] covers the usual tricks, and [[tax-season-scams-2026|tax season scams]] the seasonal ones.

=== FAQ
Q: What is an IRS IP PIN?
A: A six-digit number that prevents anyone else filing a federal tax return with your SSN or ITIN.
Q: Who can get an IP PIN?
A: Anyone with an SSN or ITIN who can verify their identity, including dependents through a parent or guardian.
Q: Do I get a new IP PIN every year?
A: Yes. It's valid for one calendar year, and online enrollees must retrieve the new one each year.
Q: Will the IRS ever ask for my IP PIN?
A: No. Any call, email or text asking for it is a scam.
