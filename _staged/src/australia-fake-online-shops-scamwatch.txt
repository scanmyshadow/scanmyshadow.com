=== TLDR
Fake online shops copy real brands' **URLs and designs**, use **fake reviews** and **celebrity endorsements**, and take your money and card details for goods that never arrive. Scamwatch said about **half** the malicious sites the National Anti-Scam Centre took down in one three-month period were **shopping scams**. Red flags: **prices way too low**, **only glowing reviews**, **no ABN, T&Cs or privacy policy**, and **odd payment requests** like multiple PayIDs or gift cards.

=== BODY
## How fake shops work

From [Scamwatch's December 2024 warning](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au/about-us/news-and-alerts/online-shopping-scams):

- Scammers build **websites and social media profiles** that **imitate well-known brands**, often copying URLs and page designs.
- They add **fake reviews** and **fake celebrity endorsements**.
- You enter personal and card details. **Nothing arrives**, and your details may be **misused**.

The National Anti-Scam Centre removed or restricted thousands of scam sites; about **half** of those taken down in the prior three months were **shopping scams**.

## Red flags

| Flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Prices much lower** than elsewhere | The oldest trick in retail crime |
| **Reviews all positive**, no detail | Real customers complain about something |
| **No ABN, T&Cs or privacy policy** | Legit Aussie businesses show these |
| **Odd payment methods**: money orders, gift cards, **multiple PayIDs or bank accounts** | No buyer protection; the "changing PayID" is a big tell |
| **New social media store** with huge discounts | Popped up last week, gone next week |

A 90% off designer handbag isn't a sale. It's a sign.

## Quick checks before you pay

1. **Search the company name** plus "scam" or "reviews" on independent sites.
2. **Compare prices** with other stores.
3. **Don't trust the top search result** blindly; ads can be fake. Go to the brand's site directly.
4. **Look for** contact details, dispute resolution, T&Cs and a privacy policy.
5. **Pay with a credit card or PayPal**, which give you dispute options.
6. Keep your **device and apps updated**.

## If you've paid

1. **Call your bank** and ask about a chargeback.
2. **Report the site** to Scamwatch.
3. If you shared personal info, contact **IDCARE** on **1800 595 160**.
4. Watch for follow-up "refund" scams.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I know if an online shop in Australia is fake?
A: Watch for very low prices, only positive reviews, no ABN or policies, and requests to pay by gift card, money order or multiple PayIDs.
Q: How common are fake online shops?
A: Scamwatch said about half the malicious sites taken down by the National Anti-Scam Centre in one three-month period were shopping scams.
Q: What payment method is safest online?
A: Scamwatch suggests secure options like PayPal or a credit card.
Q: What should I do if I bought from a fake shop?
A: Contact your bank, report the site to Scamwatch and contact IDCARE if you shared personal details.
