=== TLDR
Companies House's free “Find and update company information” service shows a company's directors, registered address, filing history and status. Before paying a builder, landlord's company, online shop or investment firm, check it exists, is active, has filed accounts, and that the names and address match what you were told. A company being registered doesn't make it honest, but a missing, dissolved or brand-new company is a clear warning.

=== BODY
## What the register shows

From [Companies House's own blog](https://companieshouse.blog.gov.uk/2019/01/29/checking-the-public-company-record/):

- directors' names and service addresses;
- filing history, including accounts, confirmation statements and charges;
- other business activity.

It also notes: “It's quite easy for someone to say ‘I'm a company director',” which is precisely why you check. The search now lives on GOV.UK as “Find and update company information.”

## Five-minute check

1. **Search the exact company name** and the company number if you have it.
2. **Status**: is it “Active”? Avoid “Dissolved,” “Liquidation” or “Proposal to strike off.”
3. **Age**: incorporated last month? Be careful with big payments.
4. **People**: do the directors match the person you're dealing with?
5. **Filing history**: are accounts and confirmation statements up to date? Lots of overdue filings is a red flag.
6. **Address**: does the registered office match the address on their website or invoice?

## What it doesn't prove

A registration isn't a seal of approval. Scammers register companies too, and some clone real ones. So also:

- check investment firms on the [[uk-fca-firm-checker-clone-firms-fca-impersonation|FCA Firm Checker]];
- check reviews beyond the first page of results;
- for property, verify ownership with HM Land Registry ([[uk-spareroom-flatshare-rental-scams|as SpareRoom advises]]);
- never pay by bank transfer to a personal account for a business invoice.

## Directors' own privacy

If you're a director, only your month and year of birth are public, and you can use a service address instead of your home. If your home address is on old filings, you may be able to apply to have it removed. See our [[uk-192-com-remove-your-details|192.com guide]], since people-finder sites copy Companies House data.

=== FAQ
Q: Is a Companies House search free?
A: Yes. The Find and update company information service on GOV.UK is free.
Q: Does being on Companies House mean a company is legitimate?
A: No. It shows the company is registered, not that it's honest. Use it alongside other checks.
Q: What are the red flags on Companies House?
A: Dissolved or strike-off status, very recent incorporation, overdue filings and directors who don't match who you're dealing with.
Q: Can company directors hide their home address?
A: Directors can use a service address, and only month and year of birth are public.
