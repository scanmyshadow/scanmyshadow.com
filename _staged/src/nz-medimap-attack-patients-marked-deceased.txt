=== TLDR
On **22 February 2026**, **MediMap**, a medication portal used by **aged-care homes, hospices and disability services**, was taken offline after attackers **tampered with patient records**: changing **names and birth dates** and marking **living patients as "deceased."** It came weeks after the **Manage My Health** breach. PM **Christopher Luxon** acknowledged the need for stronger cyber laws, and the government's new strategy now asks the Ministry of Justice to look at **civil penalties** for privacy failures.

=== BODY
## What happened

According to [Rankiteo's incident summary](https://blog.rankiteo.com/med1772728377-medimap-cyber-attack-february-2026/):

- MediMap was taken offline on **22 February 2026**.
- Attackers **altered** records rather than (as far as reported) stealing medical files.
- Changes included **names**, **birth dates**, and marking **living patients "deceased."**

The number of affected MediMap patients wasn't given.

## Why tampering is scarier than theft

A stolen record is a privacy problem. A **changed** medication record is a **safety** problem. Staff rely on these systems to give the right dose to the right person. Marking someone as dead in a care system is, to put it mildly, not a clerical inconvenience.

## The policy fallout

- **PM Christopher Luxon** acknowledged the need to strengthen cybersecurity laws.
- The **Cyber Security Strategy 2026–2030** was released on **27 February 2026**, with an Action Plan tasking the Ministry of Justice to explore **civil penalties** for privacy breaches and a possible **offence for handling illegally obtained data** ([[nz-cyber-security-action-plan-stolen-data-offence|details]]).
- Critics noted the **Privacy Act 2020** has no civil penalties for failing to secure data; the only fines (up to **$10,000**) are for failing to **notify** the Privacy Commissioner of a breach.
- The Privacy Commissioner reported a **43% rise** in serious breach notifications in 2024–25 ([[nz-privacy-breach-notifications-rise-43-percent|more]]).

## Context: a bad run for NZ health data

MediMap followed the **December 2025 Manage My Health** breach, where hundreds of thousands of documents were stolen and the Privacy Commissioner later issued compliance notices ([[nz-manage-my-health-data-breach-privacy-commissioner-findings|our guide]]).

## If you or a family member uses aged care, hospice or disability services

- Ask the provider whether **records were checked** after the incident.
- Double-check **medications and personal details** at the next review.
- Be wary of **calls or messages** referencing the incident; verify through the provider directly.
- You can request a copy of health records to check accuracy ([[nz-health-records-access-request|how]]).
