=== TLDR
Kijiji is safe enough if you deal locally, meet in public, inspect before paying and keep messages on the platform. Scams to watch: “free shipping” that turns into fees (Kijiji's example: a $200 fee before a puppy ships), untraceable payments like Western Union and MoneyGram, fake e-Transfer “deposit” notifications and overpayment refund tricks. Kijiji calls cash the most secure payment method for in-person deals.

=== BODY
## The scams Kijiji warns about

From [Kijiji's payment scam page](https://help.kijiji.ca/safety/avoiding-payment-scams) and its [e-Transfer deposit warning](https://help.kijiji.ca/safety/e-transfer-deposit-scammers):

- **Fake shipping**: “free shipping” that suddenly needs “shipping, insurance or processing fees.” Kijiji gives the example of a **$200 fee** before a puppy ships. (There is usually no puppy.)
- **Untraceable payments**: Western Union, MoneyGram and similar, especially before you've seen the item.
- **Fake e-Transfer deposits**: a notification that a “deposit” has been sent, sometimes for more than the price, followed by a request to refund the difference ([[canada-fake-interac-e-transfer-notification-scam|how to spot fake e-Transfers]]).
- **Moving off-platform**: scammers push to text or email so Kijiji can't detect suspicious activity.

## Safe buying

- Meet in a public place; many police stations have safe exchange zones.
- Inspect the item before paying.
- Pay cash, or e-Transfer only after you've seen the item.
- Be wary of prices far below market.
- Never pay shipping or “holding” fees to a stranger.

## Safe selling

- Confirm payment has actually landed in your bank account (in your banking app, not an email) before handing anything over.
- Don't accept overpayments or refund the difference.
- Don't share verification codes; the [[google-voice-verification-code-scam|code scam]] hits sellers everywhere.
- Don't send items before payment clears.

## Rentals on Kijiji

Rental listings attract fake landlords asking for deposits before viewings. See [[rental-listing-scams-fake-apartment-ads|fake rental ads]].

## If you're scammed

Report the user to Kijiji, contact your bank, and report to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/). === FAQ
Q: Is Kijiji safe to use?
A: Yes, if you deal locally, meet in public, inspect items before paying and keep messages on Kijiji.
Q: What is the Kijiji shipping scam?
A: A seller offers free shipping, then demands shipping, insurance or processing fees, such as $200 before a puppy ships.
Q: Is e-Transfer safe on Kijiji?
A: Only after you've seen the item, and beware of fake e-Transfer deposit notifications.
Q: What payment does Kijiji recommend?
A: Kijiji calls cash the most secure payment method for in-person deals.
