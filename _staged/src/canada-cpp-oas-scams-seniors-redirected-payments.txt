=== TLDR
Canadians aged 60 and over lost nearly $180 million to fraud in 2024, more than any other age group, and filed 12,801 reports. Scams include fake Service Canada callers and redirected pension payments: one Edmonton case took $179,689 from seniors over four years through 176 redirected CPP and OAS payments. If a payment is missing, call Service Canada at 1-800-277-9914, then your bank, police and the CAFC.

=== BODY
## The scale

From [Money.ca's report](https://money.ca/news/canada-seniors-cpp-oas-fraud-warning-signs), citing CAFC data:

| 2024 | Figure |
|---|---|
| Losses, Canadians aged 60+ | nearly $180 million (highest of any age group) |
| Fraud reports filed by seniors | 12,801 (most of any specified age group) |

The same report describes an Edmonton case where **176 CPP and OAS payments** were redirected to a personal account over four years, taking **$179,689** from seniors.

## How the scams work

- **Fake Service Canada calls**: someone says your benefits are suspended, your SIN is compromised, or you're owed an increase, and needs your details “to fix it.”
- **Redirected payments**: criminals change your direct deposit details, or have payments reissued to an account they control.
- **Relative-in-trouble calls** timed around payment days.

Toronto Police describe seniors as targeted because of “perceived accessibility, vulnerability and reluctance to report this crime.” The last part matters: reporting is how payments get traced.

## Warning signs

- pressure to act right away;
- requests to keep it secret from family, caregivers or the bank;
- callers you can't verify;
- payment by wire, gift card or crypto;
- unfamiliar withdrawals, e-Transfers or **changes to your direct deposit**.

## What to do

- **Missing CPP or OAS payment?** Call **Service Canada: 1-800-277-9914**.
- Report to your bank, local police and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/).
- Create your My Service Canada Account yourself, so nobody can set one up in your name, and check it regularly.
- If your SIN was exposed, follow [[canada-sin-stolen-what-to-do|Service Canada's steps]].

## For families

A monthly two-minute check-in (“did your pension arrive?”) catches redirection fast.
