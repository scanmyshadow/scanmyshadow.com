=== TLDR
To complain to New Zealand's **Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC)**: **(1)** complain **in writing to the organisation first** (the OPC won't accept your complaint otherwise); **(2)** check the OPC is the right body; **(3)** gather evidence; **(4)** fill in the **online self-assessment and complaint form**. Investigations can take **up to 12 months** to start. The OPC **can't fine** organisations or order them to pay you, but it can investigate, conciliate and issue directions. **Class complaints** are possible.

=== BODY
## Step 1: Complain to the organisation

The [OPC](https://privacy.org.nz/your-rights/how-to-complain/) says it "won't accept a complaint to the Privacy Commissioner if you haven't done step one." Write to the organisation, explain the issue, keep a copy.

## Step 2: Make sure the OPC is the right place

| Issue | Who handles it |
|---|---|
| Employment | Employment Relations Authority or Labour Inspectorate |
| Health care | Health and Disability Commissioner |
| Government agencies (some issues) | Ombudsman |
| Tenancy | Tenancy Disputes Tribunal |
| Harmful online content | Netsafe |
| Mishandling of your personal information | **Privacy Commissioner** |

## Step 3: Gather evidence

- Proof you tried to resolve it (your complaint and their response)
- All correspondence, with dates and sender/recipient (PDFs of emails recommended)
- **How you were affected**
- What you want them to do
- A signed **representative authority** form if someone else (not a lawyer) acts for you

The online form **can't be saved halfway**, so have everything ready. It's not a form you want to do twice.

## Step 4: Lodge online

Complete the **self-assessment and complaint form** and agree to the OPC's Service Charter and Unreasonable Conduct Policy.

## What happens next

The OPC may: make a **preliminary enquiry**, send a **Compliance Advice Letter**, ask for more information, **investigate**, **conciliate**, or explain why it won't act. Investigations can involve a wait of **up to 12 months** due to demand.

**Good to know:** most complaints need evidence of **harm**, but **access and correction** complaints **don't**.

## What the OPC can't do

- Issue **fines**
- **Order** the organisation to pay you money
- Force anyone to accept a settlement or its findings

That's why the Commissioner is asking Parliament for stronger powers ([[nz-privacy-breach-notifications-rise-43-percent|more]]).

## Class complaints

One person can complain **on behalf of a group** affected by the same breach. Useful after big incidents like [[nz-manage-my-health-data-breach-privacy-commissioner-findings|Manage My Health]].

## Before you complain

Make an [[nz-privacy-act-access-request-how-to|access request]] to see exactly what they hold. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows which breaches your email is in.

=== FAQ
Q: Do I have to complain to the organisation before the Privacy Commissioner?
A: Yes. The OPC won't accept a complaint unless you've first complained to the agency.
Q: How long does a privacy complaint take in NZ?
A: If an investigation is needed, the wait for an investigator can be up to 12 months.
Q: Can the Privacy Commissioner fine a company?
A: No. It can't issue fines or order payment, but it can investigate, conciliate and issue directions after an investigation.
Q: Can I make a complaint for a group of people?
A: Yes. One person can make a class complaint for a group affected by the same breach.
