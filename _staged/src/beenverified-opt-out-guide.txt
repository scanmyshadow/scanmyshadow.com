=== BODY
## What BeenVerified is

BeenVerified sells background-style reports that can include addresses, phone numbers, relatives, social profiles and some public records. It markets heavily, so if a friend has “looked you up”, there's a fair chance it was here.

## Step by step

These steps follow [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually):

1. **Open the opt-out search.** Go to BeenVerified's opt-out page at beenverified.com/svc/optout/search/optouts, or use the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link at the bottom of the site.
2. **Search for yourself** by name and state.
3. **Select your record** and submit the opt-out.
4. **Confirm by email.** Click the verification link BeenVerified sends you. The request doesn't go through until you do.

## Several records?

People who have moved or changed their name often have more than one. Submit a separate request for each. For bulk removals, Cloaked notes you can email BeenVerified with your full name, date of birth and the record URLs.

## Checking it worked

Wait a week, then search again in a private browser window. If your record is still there, resubmit. Re-check every three months, because data brokers refresh and listings can return.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I opt out of BeenVerified?
A: Use the opt-out search (linked from "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"), select your record, submit and confirm through the email link.
Q: Do I have to opt out of each record separately?
A: Yes. Each record needs its own request, or you can email BeenVerified with your details and record URLs for bulk removal.
Q: Is BeenVerified opt-out free?
A: Yes, opting out doesn't cost anything.
Q: Can my BeenVerified record come back?
A: It can, as data is refreshed. Check every few months.
