=== BODY
## What BeenVerified is, and who's behind it

BeenVerified sells background-style reports that can include addresses, phone numbers, relatives, social profiles and some public records. It advertises heavily, so if someone has "looked you up" lately, there's a fair chance it was here.

According to [Redact's broker profile](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/beenverified-com), BeenVerified, LLC is owned by **The Lifetime Value Co. (LTVCO)**, which runs nine consumer brands: BeenVerified, PeopleLooker, PeopleSmart, NeighborWho, Ownerly, NumberGuru, ReversePhone, Bumper and MoneyBot5000. Redact says the brands share one data pipeline, and that PeopleLooker and PeopleSmart "most aggressively duplicate BeenVerified's people-search listings under different branding."

In other words, removing yourself from BeenVerified alone is a bit like closing one door of a house with nine entrances.

## Step by step

These steps follow [DeleteMe's BeenVerified guide](https://joindeleteme.com/opt-out-guides/how-to-remove-yourself-from-beenverified/), last updated September 2026:

1. **Open the opt-out search** at **beenverified.com/app/optout/search**.
2. **Search** your name and pick your state from the dropdown.
3. **Find your listing** and click **Proceed to Opt Out**.
4. **Enter an email address**, complete the CAPTCHA and click **Send Verification Email**. DeleteMe suggests using a separate address rather than your main one.
5. **Click "Verify Opt-Out"** in the email BeenVerified sends. Nothing happens until you do.
6. **Wait for the confirmation email** saying the opt-out worked.

Opting out is free. You don't need to create an account or pay for a report.

## How long removal takes

DeleteMe's guide says the listing "should be removed within 24 hours" in one place and within **72 hours** in its FAQ. Plan on three days, then check.

## Several records?

People who have moved, married or changed their name often have more than one listing. Submit a separate request for each. For bulk removals, [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually) notes you can email BeenVerified with your full name, date of birth and the URLs of the records.

## The sister sites

Redact is blunt: "Each brand keeps its own opt-out, so removing your BeenVerified listing doesn't clear the sister sites."

| Brand | Separate opt-out needed? | Notes from Redact |
|---|---|---|
| **PeopleLooker** | Yes | Duplicates BeenVerified people-search listings; do this one next |
| **PeopleSmart** | Yes | Also duplicates listings; do it alongside PeopleLooker |
| **NeighborWho** | Yes | Worth doing if you own property |
| **Ownerly** | Yes | Worth doing if you own property |
| **NumberGuru** | Yes | Part of the same group |
| **ReversePhone** | Runs through BeenVerified's portal | The one exception Redact names |

So the realistic order is: BeenVerified, then PeopleLooker and PeopleSmart, then NeighborWho and Ownerly if you own a home.

## If you live in California: use DROP too

California's **Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP)** lets residents send one deletion request to every registered data broker. According to [CalPrivacy](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/):

- it's **free** ("we will never charge you to use DROP");
- it opened to the public on **January 1, 2026**;
- since **August 1, 2026**, brokers must check DROP at least **every 45 days** and delete matching data;
- there are **over 600** registered brokers;
- status updates can take **up to 90 days** to show.

DROP doesn't replace checking the big people-search sites yourself, but it covers brokers you've never heard of. Our [[california-drop-data-broker-delete-request-guide|DROP guide]] walks through it.

## Checking it worked

Wait three days, then search your name again in a private browser window. If the record is still there, resubmit. DeleteMe's FAQ says it's "More than likely" your data gets relisted, because "data brokers relist information about people on a regular basis." Put a reminder in your calendar to check every three months.
