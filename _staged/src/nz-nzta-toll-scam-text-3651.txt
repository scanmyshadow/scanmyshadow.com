=== TLDR
Scam texts say you owe **road toll late fees** and link to a fake payment page. NZTA says genuine texts **only come from 3651** and **never include a payment link**. In January 2025, a Tauranga driver got one days after actually using a toll road, which is exactly why it felt real. Don't click. Check tolls by typing the NZTA site yourself, and report the text.

=== BODY
## What the scam looks like

From [SunLive](https://www.sunlive.co.nz/news/358441-fake-nzta-toll-fee-text-circulates-tauranga.html) (10 January 2025):

- A text asks you to pay **unknown late fees** on an **outstanding road toll**.
- It includes a **link** to a bogus payment page.
- The Tauranga recipient had used a toll road just days earlier.

Scammers don't know you drove the toll road. They text thousands of people and wait for the ones who did.

## The two-second test

NZTA says:

- Official texts **only come from 3651**.
- They **never include a payment link**.

Any other number, or any link, means scam.

## Other red flags

- **Bad grammar**
- **Overseas phone numbers**
- **Urgency**: "pay today or face a penalty"

## The email version: fake rego renewals

Texts aren't the only route. [Netsafe's guide to NZTA scams](https://netsafe.org.nz/?p=8546) says the most common fake NZTA emails ask you to "**renew your rego**", and scammers keep putting up new fake sites as old ones are taken down. Netsafe's checks:

| Genuine NZTA email | Scam |
|---|---|
| Comes from **@nzta.govt.nz** or **@reply.nzta.govt.nz** | Any other address |
| Includes **your vehicle details** (plate, make, licence expiry) | Generic "your vehicle" |
| **Never asks for your driver licence details** to renew rego | Asks for licence number "to verify" |

That last one matters. A scam that gets your licence number and card has most of what it needs to open accounts in your name.

## How to check your tolls

Go to the NZTA website by **typing it yourself**, or use NZTA's app for licence and vehicle info. Don't use the text's link.

## How to report

- Report it to **NZTA** using its online phishing form. NZTA: "The sooner we know about it, the faster we can act."
- Forward it to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]]).
- Report it to **Netsafe** at netsafe.org.nz/report. Netsafe can't investigate individual scams, but uses reports to track how they change.
- If you entered **card or bank details**: call your bank to replace the card, then report to Police (105).
- If you entered **driver licence details**: arrange a **replacement licence** through NZTA online or an approved agent, and contact **IDCARE** for free help.
- If you clicked a link: run a **malware scan** on the device.
