=== TLDR
NAB's 2026 alerts: emails about a **new device login** or **suspicious activity** asking you to call a number (September); **"Automatic Payment Arrangement Now Active"** emails (September); **fake audit** emails asking businesses for invoices (September); **"Unfamiliar charge on your card"** notices (August); **Rewards points** texts (June); and fake **"NAB Security Team"** emails (April). NAB says it **never** texts links unexpectedly or asks for your PIN. Report emails to **phish@nab.com.au** and texts to **0476 220 003**.

## NAB's 2026 scam list

From [NAB's latest alerts](https://www.nab.com.au/about-us/security/latest-fraud-scam-alerts):

| When | Scam |
|---|---|
| Sep 2026 | Emails claiming your NAB profile was used on a **new device** or there was **suspicious activity**; asks you to call a number |
| Sep 2026 | **"Automatic Payment Arrangement Now Active"** emails saying a new payment was set up; call this number |
| Sep 2026 | **Fake audit emails** to businesses requesting invoices for a "credit review", sometimes posing as a forwarded email from a NAB banker |
| Aug 2026 | **"Account Payment Notice"** or **"Unfamiliar charge on your card"** emails with a call number or approval link |
| Jul 2026 | **Origin Energy breach** raises targeted phishing risk (NAB not affected) |
| Jun 2026 | **Fake online gambling sites** using changing PayIDs and referral bonuses |
| Jun 2026 | **Rewards Points** texts and emails linking to fake NAB sites |
| Apr 2026 | Fake **"NAB Security Team"** emails referencing a fake transaction |
| Jan 2026 | **Investment scams** impersonating nabtrade's Tom Piotrowski and others |

Notice how many say "call this number"? Scammers learned people won't click links anymore, so they make you dial instead. Same scam, new door. More in our guide to [[australia-bank-new-login-call-this-number-email-scam|"call this number" bank emails]].

## What NAB never does

- Send an **email or text asking for personal information**
- Ask for your **card PIN** or full card details, or ask you to **leave your card out** for collection
- Ask you to **withdraw cash** so it can be "checked"
- Ask you to **transfer money** to keep it safe
- Ask you to **download programs** from its site
- Send **unexpected texts containing links**

## How NAB is fighting back

NAB says it stopped or recovered more than **$48 million** heading to scammers between **October 2024 and March 2025**, almost **$2 million a week**. Customers **abandoned $195 million** in payments after real-time scam alerts. NAB has also **removed links from its text messages** and blocks payments to some high-risk crypto exchanges ([NAB](https://www.nab.com.au/news/technology-ai/nab-scam-stop-and-recover)).

## How to report

- **Emails:** forward to **phish@nab.com.au**, then delete.
- **Texts:** forward to **0476 220 003**.
- **Phone:** **13 22 65** (option 3), or +61 3 8641 9083 from overseas.
- **App:** search "Contact us" → **Fraud and Scams**.
- **Money sent?** Call 13 22 65 immediately or visit a branch.
