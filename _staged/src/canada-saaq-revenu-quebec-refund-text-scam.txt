=== TLDR
Two Quebec refund scams keep coming back. Fake **SAAQ** texts say you were **overcharged** and offer a refund through a link; the SAAQ says it will **never** contact you by text about this. Fake **Revenu Québec** texts and emails promise **$400 to $600** payments; Revenu Québec says "at no time" does it offer refunds or payments that way. Don't click. Real money from both arrives by direct deposit, cheque or your official account.

=== BODY
## The SAAQ text

The [SAAQ warned](https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/en/newsroom/article/alerte-a-lhameconnage-ne-tombez-pas-dans-le-piege-des-messages-frauduleux) about texts that appear to come from the Société de l'assurance automobile du Québec. They say:

- you were **overcharged**;
- click the **link** to get your refund.

The SAAQ's position is blunt: it **will never contact you by text message** for this kind of issue. Don't click and don't share personal information.

## The Revenu Québec text

When Quebec sent out cost-of-living cheques, fraudulent texts and emails followed, promising **$400 to $600**. [Revenu Québec told CTV](https://montreal.ctvnews.ca/mobile/inflation-fighting-cheques-are-coming-but-beware-of-fraudsters-revenu-quebec-1.6169877): "At no time does Revenu Québec offer refunds or payments in this manner." Real payments went by **direct deposit or cheque**.

Notice the pattern? Scammers watch the news. Whenever there's a real government payment, a fake version arrives in your texts within days, usually with worse spelling.

## Why refund scams work

A "you owe money" text makes people suspicious. A "we owe *you* money" text makes people curious. The link leads to a page that asks for your card number "to deposit the refund," plus your name, address and date of birth. That's a full identity kit, handed over with a smile.

## What to do

1. **Don't click** the link or reply.
2. **Forward the text to 7726** so your carrier can block it. See our [[canada-7726-spam-text-reporting-guide|7726 guide]].
3. **Check directly** by typing saaq.gouv.qc.ca or revenuquebec.ca yourself, or logging into your official online account.
4. **Entered card details?** Call your bank now and ask for a new card.
5. **Report losses** to police and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/).

## More Quebec scams to know

Fake [[canada-hydro-quebec-scam-calls-texts|Hydro-Québec]] and [[canada-desjardins-scam-text-call-phishing|Desjardins]] messages use the same playbook. Quebec residents also have strong privacy rights under [[quebec-law-25-privacy-rights-explained|Law 25]]. === FAQ
Q: Does the SAAQ send refund texts?
A: No. The SAAQ says it will never contact you by text message about this kind of issue.
Q: Does Revenu Québec send payments by text or email?
A: No. Revenu Québec says at no time does it offer refunds or payments that way.
Q: How does Revenu Québec actually pay refunds?
A: By direct deposit or cheque.
Q: Where do I report a SAAQ or Revenu Québec scam text?
A: Forward it to 7726 and report any losses to police and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.
