=== BODY
## What FastPeopleSearch shows

Like its cousin TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch is free and shows addresses, phone numbers, emails, relatives and associates without a paywall. DuckDuckGo notes its data can be outdated, which is why it recommends searching by name rather than phone or address.

## Step by step

From [DuckDuckGo's FastPeopleSearch guide](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/fastpeoplesearch-removal):

1. Open [fastpeoplesearch.com/removal](https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/removal). A Cloudflare check may appear first.
2. Enter your email, tick the box confirming you're the subject (or authorised), complete the reCAPTCHA and begin the removal.
3. Search by first and last name plus city, state or ZIP.
4. Open your listing with “View Free Details” and note the URL.
5. Click “Remove My Record.”
6. Open the email “Opt out request for FastPeopleSearch.com” and click the blue button.

You'll see a “Record Removal Complete” page. No follow-up email arrives later.

## Check it worked

Revisit the old listing URL. A removed listing shows a “Something went wrong” message, which, for once, is the message you want to see.

## The bonus

According to DuckDuckGo, removing your listing here also removes it from related sites such as PeopleFinders and SmartBackgroundChecks. That's a rare two-for-one. Still, verify on those sites yourself: PeopleFinders has its own [[peoplefinders-opt-out-guide|state-based opt-out rules]].

## Limits

- Up to five requests per email address.
- Repeat steps 3 to 6 for each listing.
- Data can reappear as the site updates; recheck every few months.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove myself from FastPeopleSearch?
A: Start at fastpeoplesearch.com/removal, find your listing, click Remove My Record and confirm via the emailed link.
Q: How long does FastPeopleSearch removal take?
A: Usually about an hour; the site says up to 72 hours.
Q: Does it remove me from other sites?
A: DuckDuckGo says it also removes your listing from related sites such as PeopleFinders and SmartBackgroundChecks. Check them to be sure.
