=== TLDR
Cifas recorded over 220,000 fraud-risk cases in January to June 2026, its highest first-half total. Identity fraud made up 59% (nearly 130,000 cases, up 9%), mostly bank accounts and cards. Account takeover rose 5% to nearly 40,000, with online retail takeovers up 84%. Unauthorised SIM swaps rose 402%. Most identity fraud victims are 61 or older, but the fastest growth was among 21 to 30-year-olds.

Cifas chief executive Mike Haley: “Identity fraud now accounts for nearly three in five fraud-risk cases recorded to the UK National Fraud Database.”

## What the SIM swap jump means

A 402% rise in unauthorised SIM swaps is the number that should make everyone sit up. A SIM swap moves your phone number to a criminal's SIM, so your bank's one-time passcodes go to them. Cifas links the trend to intercepting security codes and bypassing account protection. If your phone suddenly says “No service” or “SOS only” at home, act fast: see [[sim-swap-fraud-how-it-works-and-how-to|how SIM swap fraud works]] and [[how-to-block-a-swapped-sim-card|how to block a swapped SIM]].

## Who's being hit

- **Most identity fraud victims are 61 and over.**
- **The biggest increase is among 21 to 30-year-olds**, up about 32%.
- **Under-30s make up 57% of money mule cases**, and 17% involve people under 21. Many are recruited with “easy money” job ads ([[what-is-money-mule-scams|money mules explained]]).

## Why online retail takeovers jumped

An 84% rise in online retail account takeover fits what we see in breach data: reused passwords from old breaches unlock shopping accounts with saved cards. Which? reported Argos account hacks earlier in 2026 where criminals used breached details to order and collect goods ([[argos-uk-scam-what-to-know|Argos scams]]).

## What to do with this

1. **Use unique passwords** for shopping accounts and turn on two-factor authentication.
2. **Add a PIN or password to your mobile account** to make SIM swaps harder.
3. **Check your credit report** regularly, and add [[uk-cifas-protective-registration-explained|Cifas Protective Registration]] if your details have leaked.
4. **Talk to younger relatives** about mule recruitment.
5. **See what's leaked**: a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows which breaches your email is in.
