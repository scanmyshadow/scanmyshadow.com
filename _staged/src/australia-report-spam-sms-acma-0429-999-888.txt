=== TLDR
In Australia, forward spam and scam texts to **ACMA's Spam SMS line on 0429 999 888** (standard SMS rates apply). Report the scam itself to **Scamwatch**. If it impersonates a bank, also forward it to that bank: **CommBank** hoax@cba.com.au, **Westpac** 0497 132 032, **NAB** 0476 220 003, **ANZ** hoax@cybersecurity.anz.com (email). Lost money? Call your bank first, right now.

=== BODY
## Step 1: Forward to ACMA

[Scamwatch](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au/node/160) points to ACMA's dedicated **Spam SMS line: 0429 999 888**. Forward the text there. Your normal SMS rates apply. You can also register online with ACMA so its Anti-Spam Team can contact you if needed.

**iPhone:** press and hold the message → More → tap the forward arrow → enter 0429 999 888.
**Android:** press and hold → Forward → 0429 999 888.

Save it as a contact called "SPAM" and you'll never have to look it up again.

## Step 2: Report to Scamwatch

Use Scamwatch's **Report a scam** page. Reports feed the National Anti-Scam Centre's takedown and warning work.

## Step 3: Tell the brand

If the text pretends to be a bank, forward it to that bank too:

| Bank | Where to forward |
|---|---|
| CommBank | hoax@cba.com.au ([source](https://www.commbank.com.au/security-privacy/report-hoaxes.html)) |
| Westpac | Texts: **0497 132 032**; emails: hoax@westpac.com.au ([source](https://www.westpac.com.au/security/latest-scams/)) |
| NAB | Texts: **0476 220 003**; emails: phish@nab.com.au ([source](https://www.nab.com.au/about-us/security/latest-fraud-scam-alerts)) |
| ANZ | Emails: hoax@cybersecurity.anz.com ([source](https://www.anz.com.au/content/anzcomau/en/personal/security/latest-scams-australia.html)) |

## Don't reply

Replying (even "STOP") to a scam text confirms your number is live. Report and delete.

## Lost money or shared details?

Reporting spam doesn't get money back. Do this instead:

1. **Call your bank immediately.**
2. Report to **ReportCyber** at cyber.gov.au.
3. Get free help from **IDCARE** on 1800 595 160.

## Fewer scam texts in future

Unverified sender names now get flagged under the [[australia-acma-sms-sender-id-register-unverified-label-1-july|SMS Sender ID Register]]. For calls, see the [[australia-do-not-call-register-guide|Do Not Call Register]]. And if you're wondering how scammers got your number, a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows the breaches it may have come from.

=== FAQ
Q: What number do I forward scam texts to in Australia?
A: ACMA's Spam SMS line, 0429 999 888.
Q: Is it free to forward spam to 0429 999 888?
A: Your mobile provider's standard SMS rates apply.
Q: Where else should I report a scam text?
A: Report it to Scamwatch, and forward bank-branded texts to the bank's own reporting address or number.
Q: Should I reply STOP to a scam text?
A: No. Replying confirms your number is active.
