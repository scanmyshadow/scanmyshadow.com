=== TLDR
Netsafe's **August 2026** update: **bank impersonation** reports jumped from **21 in July to 46 in August**, making up **34%** of impersonation reports (up from 20% in April–July), the highest share all year. **Government impersonation** fell from about **37 a month to 11**. Netsafe's read: scammers are **changing the costume, not the trick**. Hang up on unexpected bank calls and call back on the number on your card.

=== BODY
## The numbers

From [Netsafe's August 2026 update](https://resource.netsafe.org.nz/Netsafe-scams-update-August-2026.pdf):

| Measure | Before | August 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Bank/financial impersonation reports | 21 (July) | **46** |
| Bank share of impersonation reports | 20% (Apr–Jul) | **34%** |
| Government impersonation reports | ~37/month (Apr–Jul) | **11** |

Netsafe called bank impersonation "the standout trend for August," and noted no other significant increases.

## Same scam, new outfit

Netsafe suggests scammers may be switching **who they pretend to be**, not **how** they operate. Last quarter it was IRD and NZTA; this month it's your bank. The script barely changes: urgency, a "problem", a request for a code or a transfer.

Netsafe also cautions that monthly numbers shift with publicity and only count what's reported, so treat them as indicative.

## What bank impersonation looks like right now

- **Fraud calls** asking for Netcodes or token codes ([[nz-asb-scam-alerts-2026|ASB]])
- **Fake Caller Check** messages by text or WhatsApp ([[nz-bank-caller-check-impersonation-scams|explained]])
- **WhatsApp** messages with bank logos ([[nz-banks-whatsapp-impersonation-scams|banks don't use WhatsApp]])
- "**Read me the code to block the payment**" ([[nz-kiwibank-latest-scams-2026|Kiwibank warns]])

## Netsafe's advice

- **Don't share** passwords, PINs or verification codes.
- **Hang up** on unexpected calls.
- **Don't click** links in unexpected texts or emails.
- **Call your bank** using the number on your card or its official website.
- **Report** to the organisation and to Netsafe: **netsafe.org.nz/report** or **0508 638 723**.

If you lost money, also check whether the new [[nz-bank-scam-reimbursement-rules-explained|bank reimbursement rules]] apply. Report to the bank and Police within three months.

A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your number or email is in breach data.
