=== TLDR
In **April 2026**, Netsafe warned that scammers are now copying the **systems** that make businesses trustworthy: **domain renewals, hosting invoices, account renewals and login pages**. **Unauthorised billing scams** took **$58,744** so far in 2026 (over **$26,500** in January alone); **fake website** scam reports jumped **35%** from February to March. If you get an urgent "renew your domain" email, **log in to your provider directly**.

## What Netsafe said

Alex Yi, digital harms services manager: "Scammers are no longer just pretending to be organisations." They're copying **domain names, websites, invoices and account renewals**: the things that normally make an organisation look legit.

Three types:

1. **Unauthorised billing**: fake domain or hosting renewals, invoices from "providers."
2. **Fake websites and login pages** that steal logins and payments.
3. **Multi-step** scams: a social ad leads to a realistic site, then a phone or email follow-up closes the deal.

Domain renewal emails are perfect bait. Most people renew once a year and can't remember who their registrar is. Scammers are counting on that fog.

## How to spot it

- **Urgent** "renew now or lose your domain/website/email" messages
- An **invoice** from a provider you **don't recognise**, or for a service you don't use
- **Links** to log in or pay
- Slightly **wrong web addresses**

## Netsafe's advice

- Be wary of **urgent** requests to renew domains, pay invoices or update details.
- **Don't click links** in unsolicited emails; go to the official site.
- **Check web addresses** carefully.
- Turn on **MFA** on important accounts.
- **Verify** payment requests independently.
- When something seems legit but **unexpected**, check through a trusted channel.

## For small businesses

- Keep a simple list: **registrar, host, renewal dates, how they bill you**.
- Route all invoices to **one person** who checks them against that list.
- Never pay from an email link.

=== FAQ
Q: What are unauthorised billing scams?
A: Scams where fraudsters pose as domain name providers, hosting services or trusted organisations to collect payments.
Q: How much have Kiwis lost to unauthorised billing scams in 2026?
A: Netsafe said $58,744 so far in 2026 at the start of April, including over $26,500 in January.
Q: How do I check if a domain renewal email is real?
A: Don't click the link. Log in to your domain provider directly and check your renewal date there.
