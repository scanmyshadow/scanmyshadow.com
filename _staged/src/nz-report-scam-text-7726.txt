=== TLDR
In New Zealand, **forward scam texts to 7726** (it spells SPAM). It's **free**. The **Department of Internal Affairs** investigates and sends **daily reports to mobile networks** so they can block scams. Don't tap links or reply. Already clicked or shared details? **Call your bank now** and report losses to **Police**.

=== BODY
## Why 7726

- It's **free** to forward, per [MFAT's guidance](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/en/media-and-resources/text-message-scam).
- **DIA (Te Tari Taiwhenua)** can investigate.
- DIA sends **daily reports** to mobile operators via the 7726 service, and investigates harmful texts from identifiable NZ senders ([TCF](https://www.tcf.org.nz/digital-living/online-safety/scam-prevention-measures)).

## How to forward

**iPhone:** press and hold the message → **More** → tap the forward arrow → **7726** → Send.
**Android:** press and hold → **Forward** → **7726** → Send.

Save 7726 as a contact called "SPAM." Reporting becomes a two-second job, and oddly satisfying.

## Who tells you to use 7726

Practically everyone: [Kiwibank](https://www.kiwibank.co.nz/help/fraud-scams/latest-scams), [ASB](https://www.asb.co.nz/banking-with-asb/security-alerts.html), [NZ Post](https://www.nzpost.co.nz/contact-support/scams-and-fraud), [NZ Customs](https://www.customs.govt.nz/about-us/news/important-notices/customs-duty-scam-returns) and NZ Police. Even MFAT, after scammers impersonated the ministry and asked people for a code.

## What 7726 doesn't do

It doesn't get your money back. If you've clicked or paid:

1. **Call your bank** using the number on your card.
2. **Report to Police** at 105.police.govt.nz or call 105.
3. Report to **Netsafe** (0508 638 723).
4. Change any passwords you entered.

=== FAQ
Q: Is it free to forward a scam text to 7726 in NZ?
A: Yes. Forwarding to 7726 is free.
Q: What happens when I forward a text to 7726?
A: The Department of Internal Affairs can investigate and shares daily reports with mobile operators so they can block scams.
Q: What should I do if I clicked a link in a scam text?
A: Call your bank immediately, change passwords you entered, and report to Police and Netsafe.
Q: Should I reply STOP to a scam text?
A: No. Don't reply or follow the message's instructions; forward it to 7726 and delete it.
