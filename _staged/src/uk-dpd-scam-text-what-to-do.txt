=== TLDR
Fake DPD texts and emails say a parcel couldn't be delivered and ask for a small fee to rearrange it. The fee is the hook; days later a “bank fraud team” calls about suspicious activity and talks you into moving money to a “safe account.” DPD says it never asks for money or bank details, and genuine emails come only from dpd.co.uk, dpdlocal.co.uk or dpdgroup.co.uk. === BODY
## The two-step scam

A [police fraud alert](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/alert/fake-dpd-messages-lead-to-over-200000-in-losses-since-june) laid out how it works:

1. A text or email says your parcel couldn't be delivered because you weren't in or there was no safe place.
2. A link leads to a lookalike site asking for a small payment to rearrange delivery.
3. **A few days later**, you get a call or text that appears to be from your bank, warning of suspicious transactions. The number is spoofed.
4. The “bank” persuades you to move your money to a “secure account” they control. Sometimes they collect enough details to take out loans in your name.

In one wave the alert reported **£242,000** lost, with suspicious DPD emails jumping **655%** in a single month. The £1.45 fee was never the point; it was a key to your bank card and your trust.

## How to tell if it's really DPD

- DPD **never asks for money or bank details** by text or email.
- Genuine DPD emails come only from **dpd.co.uk, dpdlocal.co.uk or dpdgroup.co.uk**.
- Real tracking happens in the DPD app or on dpd.co.uk with your parcel number.
- Lookalike addresses (dpd-redelivery.com, dpdparcels-uk.net) are fake.

Christmas is peak season for these. If you're expecting several parcels, it's easy to believe one went astray.

## What to do

- Don't click. Check deliveries in the DPD app or by typing dpd.co.uk.
- Forward texts to **7726** and emails to **report@phishing.gov.uk**.

## If you paid the fee

1. **Call your bank** on the number on your card, or dial **159**, and cancel the card.
2. **Be ready for the follow-up call.** Any call about “suspicious activity” in the next week: hang up and call 159 yourself ([[uk-159-scam-hotline-how-it-works|how 159 works]]).
3. **Report it** to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/) (it replaced Action Fraud in 2026).
