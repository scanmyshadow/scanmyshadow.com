=== TLDR
Autotrader warns sellers about companies that promise ready buyers while using its name, texts and emails asking for a deposit to “secure” a sale or appointment, fake Autotrader websites, and phishing emails that steal your login. Autotrader says it will never ask for your login details or personal information by email, or ask you to verify your account via a link. === BODY
## The scams sellers face

From [Autotrader's seller advice](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/sell-my-car/advice/why-you-should-know-who-your-buyer-is):

- **Buyer-promise scams**: a company contacts you saying it has buyers lined up or will buy your car, sometimes using the Autotrader name to look legitimate.
- **Deposit scams**: texts or emails asking you to pay a deposit to secure a sale or an inspection appointment.
- **Fake websites**: copies of Autotrader that collect your information.
- **Phishing emails**: messages that look like Autotrader and link to a copycat login page.

The deposit one is the strangest: you're the seller, and somehow you end up paying. That alone should set off alarm bells.

## What Autotrader never does

- ask for your login details or personal information by email;
- ask you to verify your account by clicking a link.

## Selling safely

- Ignore unsolicited “we have a buyer” companies.
- Never pay a deposit to sell.
- Log in only by typing the Autotrader address yourself.
- Use a strong, unique password ([[strong-password-examples-and-ideas|ideas]]).
- Take payment by bank transfer and **confirm it has actually cleared** in your banking app before handing over keys. Fake payment screenshots and emails are common across marketplaces ([[zelle-business-account-upgrade-scam-marketplace|the US version]]).
- Meet buyers in daylight, in a public place or with someone else present.

## If you've been caught

If you paid a deposit, contact your bank or **159** and report it to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/). Forward phishing emails to **customersecurity@autotrader.co.uk** or call Autotrader's Customer Security Team on **0330 303 9001**.

=== FAQ
Q: Will Autotrader ask me to pay a deposit to sell my car?
A: Treat deposit requests to secure a sale or appointment as scams, as Autotrader warns.
Q: Does Autotrader email asking for login details?
A: No. Autotrader says it will never ask for login details or personal information by email.
Q: How do I report a fake Autotrader email?
A: Forward it to customersecurity@autotrader.co.uk or call 0330 303 9001.
Q: How do I know a buyer has paid?
A: Check that the money has cleared in your own banking app before handing over the car.
