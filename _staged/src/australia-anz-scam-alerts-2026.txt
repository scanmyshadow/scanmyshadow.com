=== TLDR
ANZ's 2026 alerts include **fake ANZ recruiters** emailing from Gmail (September), **"Advisor Verification"** calls asking you to read back a code (February), **fake direct debit** emails telling you to call a mobile number (February), and **ANZ Rewards points expiring** texts (January). ANZ says it will **never** ask for passwords, PINs or one-time codes, ask you to **log in via a link**, request **remote access**, or ask you to **move money**. ## The ones to watch

**Fake recruiters.** A recruiter using a Gmail address for a big bank is like a pilot showing up in thongs. Possible, but check first. Real recruiters use company email and won't ask for bank details or ID scans up front.

**Advisor Verification.** The caller emails you a "verification code" and asks you to read it back. That code is usually for something **they** are doing in your account. See our [[australia-bank-code-read-back-scam|read-back code scam guide]].

**Fake verification pages.** "Copy and paste this to prove you're human" installs malware. Explained in our [[australia-clickfix-fake-verification-copy-paste-scam|ClickFix guide]].

**Rewards texts.** The same trick hits Westpac and NAB customers. See [[australia-bank-rewards-points-expiring-sms-scam|rewards points scams]].

## What ANZ will never do

- Ask you to share **passwords, PINs, ANZ Shield or token codes**, or **one-time passcodes** for payments
- Ask you to **click a link to log in**
- Ask for **remote access** to your device
- Ask you to **transfer money** to another account

## How to report

- **Emails:** forward to **hoax@cybersecurity.anz.com**
- **Money sent or details shared:** contact ANZ through its **Report scams & fraud** page or the ANZ App. The Customer Protection Team works 24/7. Use contact details from ANZ's website or app, never from the message.
- **Texts:** also forward to ACMA on 0429 999 888 ([[australia-report-spam-sms-acma-0429-999-888|guide]])
- **Government:** Scamwatch and ReportCyber
