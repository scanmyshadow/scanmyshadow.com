## What ThatsThem shows

ThatsThem is a free lookup site that lets anyone search by name, address, phone number or email address. That last one is notable: type an email, get a name and address. If you've ever wondered how a stranger turned your email into your street, sites like this are part of the answer.

## Step by step

From [Redact's ThatsThem removal guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/thatsthem-com/remove):

1. Open [thatsthem.com/optout](https://thatsthem.com/optout).
2. Enter your full name, street address, city, state, ZIP, phone and email.
3. Complete the Cloudflare Turnstile check.
4. Click “Submit opt-out request.” A green tick and “Request Submitted!” confirm it. Screenshot that panel.

No account and no email link are needed. It takes a minute or two per record.

## After 72 hours

ThatsThem says it processes requests within 72 hours and sends a confirmation email in that window (check spam). Then:

- search yourself in a private window on all four tabs: **Name, Address, Phone and Email**;
- submit another request for any listing still showing;
- if that fails, email optout@thatsthem.com with what you submitted and your screenshot.

## Spokeo won't do it for you

Redact notes that Spokeo's removal tool doesn't cover ThatsThem. Every site needs its own request, which is why our [[us-data-broker-sites-opt-out-guide|broker list]] exists.

## The email-to-address problem

Because ThatsThem searches by email, the email you use everywhere is effectively a key to your home address. === FAQ
Q: How do I opt out of ThatsThem?
A: Fill in the form at thatsthem.com/optout with your details, pass the Cloudflare check and submit.
Q: Do I need to confirm by email?
A: No. The on-screen confirmation is your receipt, though ThatsThem says it sends a confirmation email within 72 hours.
Q: How long does ThatsThem take?
A: ThatsThem says requests are processed within 72 hours.
Q: Why do I still appear after opting out?
A: Name, address, phone and email can each be separate listings. Search all four tabs and submit for each.
