=== TLDR
In **June 2025**, ANZ warned of emails impersonating **ANZ Premier Wealth**, offering **"treasury and corporate Kiwi bonds"** and claiming they're protected by the new **Depositor Compensation Scheme (DCS)**. That's false: the DCS (from **1 July 2025**) covers up to **$100,000** per eligible customer if a bank or deposit-taker fails, but **not bonds**, **KiwiSaver** or **foreign currency accounts**. The emails use ANZ, Reserve Bank and Treasury logos. Delete them.

=== BODY
## The scam

From [ANZ's warning](https://www.anz.com.au/newsroom/new-zealand/2025/06/anz-warns-of-investment-scam-emails-that-impersonate-staff/) (13–14 June 2025):

- Emails impersonate **ANZ Premier Wealth** staff.
- They offer **"treasury and corporate Kiwi bonds."**
- They claim **DCS protection**, which is false.
- They use **ANZ's name, address, logo and registration details**, plus **Reserve Bank** and **Treasury** logos.
- You're invited to **reply** to a fake ANZ email or **call a fake ANZ number** to start a bond application.

## The real Depositor Compensation Scheme

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Start date | **1 July 2025** |
| Coverage | Up to **$100,000** per eligible customer if a bank or deposit-taker fails |
| **Not covered** | **KiwiSaver**, **foreign currency accounts**, **bonds and other tradeable products** |

Scammers love a new government scheme. People have heard the name but not the fine print, so "government-protected" sounds plausible. Here, the fine print specifically excludes what they're selling.

## Red flags

- **Unexpected** investment offers
- **High, fast or low-risk** returns
- **Pressure** to act quickly
- **Unregistered** promoters
- Claims of **DCS protection** for bonds, KiwiSaver or foreign currency
- Offers to **recover** lost funds

## What to do

1. **Don't reply** or call the number. Delete it.
2. **Verify** any investment and promoter independently, using a number from the provider's **official website**.
3. Get advice from a **licensed financial adviser**.
4. Think you've been scammed? Call ANZ on **0800 269 296** (overseas +64 4 470 3142).

Check promoters on the FSPR ([[nz-fma-fake-nz-registered-investment-firms|how]]). Other ANZ NZ scams: [[nz-anz-scam-alerts-2026|2026 alerts]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your email is in breach data.

=== FAQ
Q: Does the Depositor Compensation Scheme cover bonds?
A: No. The DCS doesn't cover bonds and other tradeable products, KiwiSaver, or foreign currency accounts.
Q: Is ANZ Premier Wealth emailing bond offers?
A: ANZ warned that emails from "ANZ Premier Wealth" offering Kiwi bonds were a scam.
Q: How much does the Depositor Compensation Scheme cover?
A: Up to $100,000 per eligible customer if a bank or deposit-taker fails, from 1 July 2025.
