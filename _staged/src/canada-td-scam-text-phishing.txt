=== TLDR
Fake TD Canada Trust texts claim your account is locked, a payment needs approval or your card was suspended, with a link to a fake login. Don't click. Open the TD app or EasyWeb yourself, or call TD Canada Trust on 1-866-222-3456 (or the number on your card). In the TD app, go to More > Contact Us > Call Us and choose “Suspected Fraud.” Report scams to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and forward texts to 7726.

=== BODY
## What the texts say

- “TD: Your account has been locked due to suspicious activity. Restore access: [link]”
- “TD Alert: A payment of $1,280.00 is pending. Reply NO if not you.”
- “Your TD Access Card has been suspended.”

The link goes to a page that looks like EasyWeb. Enter your login and the criminals have it, often within seconds.

## How to check safely

Close the text. Then:

- open the **TD app** or type **EasyWeb**'s address yourself;
- or call **TD Canada Trust: 1-866-222-3456** (personal accounts and credit cards), or the number on the back of your card.

In the TD app: **More > Contact Us > Call Us**, then choose Accounts or Small Business and **“Suspected Fraud”** ([TD's report fraud page](https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/privacy-and-security/how-you-can-protect-yourself/protect-yourself/report-online-fraud)).

Other TD lines: Direct Investing 1-800-465-5463, Insurance 1-877-397-4187, Business Banking 1-800-668-7328.

## Disputing a transaction

Use “Dispute a Transaction” in EasyWeb or the TD app, or call the number on your card.

## Report it

- Report fraud or scams to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/) and local police, as TD suggests.
- Forward scam texts to **7726**.

## Why “reply NO” is risky

Replying confirms your number is live, and a “fraud agent” often calls minutes later, already sounding helpful. That agent will ask for a code or tell you to move money. Hang up and call TD yourself. Our guide on [[canada-bank-impersonation-scams-what-to-know|bank impersonation scams]] walks through the script.

## Why they texted you

Bank texts go out in bulk to numbers from breaches and brokers. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows whether your email is in breach data.
