=== TLDR
Octopus Energy says genuine emails come only from @octopus.energy, @octoenergy.com, @octoes.com, @octopusev.com or @octopusenergy.services, and it will never ask you to log in anywhere except octopus.energy/login or ask you to confirm your password. Fake “refund” or “compensation” emails asking for bank details are scams. Screenshot and send them to hello@octopus.energy.

=== BODY
## Why energy companies are favourite disguises

Energy bills are big, refunds and credits are real, and price changes come every quarter. So “You're owed £187.43 in credit” sounds plausible. Which? has reported big jumps in energy-related scams during cost-of-living pressure, and Octopus, as one of the UK's largest suppliers, gets copied a lot.

## Octopus's own checklist

From [Octopus Energy's help page](https://octopus.energy/help-and-faqs/articles/what-should-i-do-if-i-think-i-ve-received-a-scam-or-phishing-email/):

**Real sender domains**

- @octopus.energy
- @octoenergy.com
- @octoes.com
- @octopusev.com
- @octopusenergy.services

**Real login page**: only **https://octopus.energy/login/**.

**Never**: Octopus will never ask you to confirm your password.

**Warning signs**: spelling and grammar errors, and offers of payment or compensation in exchange for usernames, passwords or bank details.

## How to report

Record what you can (a screenshot, or the number that called you) and send it to **hello@octopus.energy**. Forward scam emails to **report@phishing.gov.uk** too. If you lost money, report to [Report Fraud](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/) and call your bank or **159**.

Octopus's advice is pleasingly blunt: “If in doubt: give us a shout!”

## Other energy scams

The same tricks hit British Gas, EDF and others: fake refunds, fake smart meter appointments, and fake “energy grant” texts. See [[british-gas-scam-what-to-know|British Gas scams]] and [[uk-council-tax-refund-band-reduction-scam|council tax refund scams]], which use the same “you're owed money” hook.

=== FAQ
Q: What email addresses does Octopus Energy use?
A: @octopus.energy, @octoenergy.com, @octoes.com, @octopusev.com and @octopusenergy.services.
Q: Will Octopus Energy ask for my password by email?
A: No. Octopus says it will never ask you to confirm your password.
Q: How do I report a fake Octopus email?
A: Screenshot it and send it to hello@octopus.energy, and forward it to report@phishing.gov.uk.
Q: Is an Octopus refund email real?
A: Check your account by going to octopus.energy/login yourself. Don't use links in the email.
