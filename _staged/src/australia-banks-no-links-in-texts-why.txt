=== TLDR
Australia's big banks have made text scams easier to spot. **NAB removed links from its text messages**. **CommBank** says it never includes a link in an SMS or email to log in. **Westpac** says it never asks you to verify identity via a link in an email or SMS. **ANZ** says it never asks you to click a link to log in. So the rule is simple: **a "bank" text with a link is a scam.** Open the app instead.

## Did it work?

NAB says it stopped or recovered more than **$48 million** heading to scammers between October 2024 and March 2025, and customers **abandoned $195 million** in payments after scam alerts. Removing links was one of several steps, alongside **real-time payment alerts** and **blocking some high-risk crypto exchanges**.

## The one rule

**If a text says it's from your bank and has a link, it's a scam.** You don't have to judge the wording, the logo or the sender name. Spoofed messages can even land in the same thread as real ones.

It's like a restaurant announcing it never asks for your PIN at the table. Easy test, very hard to fake around.

## But watch for the workaround

Scammers adapted. Instead of links, they now say **"call this number"**: see [[australia-bank-new-login-call-this-number-email-scam|the callback scam]]. And they'll ask you to **read out a code**: see [[australia-bank-code-read-back-scam|read-back code scams]].

So the full rule set:

1. **No links** from banks in texts. Ignore any that have them.
2. **No calling numbers** from unexpected messages. Use the number on your card or app.
3. **No reading out codes** to anyone.
4. **No "safe accounts."** Ever.

## What to do with a fake

Forward it to your bank and ACMA (**0429 999 888**). Full list in our [[australia-report-spam-sms-acma-0429-999-888|reporting guide]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your number is in breach data scammers use.

=== FAQ
Q: Do Australian banks send links in text messages?
A: NAB removed links from its texts, and CommBank, Westpac and ANZ say they don't send links to log in or verify identity.
Q: Is a text from my bank with a link a scam?
A: Based on the banks' own policies, treat any bank text with a login or verification link as a scam.
Q: What should I do instead of clicking?
A: Open your banking app or type the bank's website yourself.
Q: Has removing links reduced scams?
A: NAB says it stopped or recovered more than $48 million from October 2024 to March 2025 using measures including removing links and payment alerts.
