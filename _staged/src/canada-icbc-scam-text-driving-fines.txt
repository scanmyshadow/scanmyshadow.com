=== TLDR
In **September and December 2025**, ICBC warned about texts asking BC drivers to pay **driving fines or outstanding debt** through a link. ICBC says it **does not** text customers about infractions or debt, and **does not** ask you to confirm identity or pay through text links. Delete the text. If you paid, call your bank and the police. Earlier versions offered fake ICBC "rebates."

=== BODY
## What the texts say

ICBC issued warnings on [September 3, 2025](https://icbc.com/pa/about-icbc/newsroom/2025-09-03-scam) and again on [December 2, 2025](https://icbc.com/pa/about-icbc/newsroom/2025-12-02-icbc-warning-customers-about-text-message-scam). The texts:

- demand payment for **driving fines** or **outstanding debt**;
- include a link to a fake payment page.

ICBC is working with an outside cybersecurity company to report and take down the fake websites. Whack-a-mole, but with lawyers.

## What ICBC doesn't do

- **Doesn't** contact customers by text about driving infractions or outstanding debt.
- **Doesn't** ask you to confirm your identity or make payments through links in a text.

That's a clean rule. If ICBC is in your texts asking for money, it isn't ICBC.

## The older rebate version

When ICBC paid **$110 rebates**, scam texts offered the rebate through a link. ICBC said it would **not** issue rebates by text or email; real ones came by direct deposit, card credit or mailed cheque ([Tri-City News](https://tricitynews.com/local-news/dont-fall-for-icbc-rebate-text-scam-circulating-in-port-coquitlam-5465329)).

## ICBC's advice

- **Delete** any text asking for payment.
- **Don't click** links in unsolicited texts.
- **Check you're on the real ICBC site** before logging in. Type it yourself.
- **Don't share** personal or financial information by text or email.
- **Don't reply** to unknown senders or call numbers in suspicious texts.

## If you paid or entered details

1. Call your bank or card issuer and cancel the card.
2. Contact **police**, as ICBC advises.
3. Report to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/).
4. Forward future texts to **7726** ([[canada-7726-spam-text-reporting-guide|how]]).

## Same scam, other provinces

The same trick shows up as [[canada-serviceontario-scam-text-licence-renewal|ServiceOntario]], [[canada-407-etr-scam-text-unpaid-toll|407 ETR]] and [[canada-saaq-revenu-quebec-refund-text-scam|SAAQ]] texts. In the US it's [[dmv-text-scam-unpaid-ticket-suspension|DMV texts]]. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your number or email is in leaked data.
