=== TLDR
If your **IRD number** or **myIR** account may be compromised, **call Inland Revenue** (number on its contact page). IRD will **verify your identity**, set up **voice ID** to protect your account, check **what your IRD number was used for**, and help fix **false returns or debt**. Then talk to **IDCARE** (free) on **0800 121 068** and report the scam. Didn't click or share anything? Just report the message to IRD.

=== BODY
## If you think you've been compromised

From [Inland Revenue](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/scams/take-action-if-youve-been-scammed):

1. **Call IRD.** They will:
   - verify your identity;
   - set up **voice ID** to access and protect your account;
   - check what your **IRD number** may have been used for;
   - help fix **incorrect returns or debt**.
2. **Speak with IDCARE.** IRD may transfer you, or call **0800 121 068** (10am–7pm, Mon–Fri). Free help to respond and protect your information.
3. **Report the scam to the Commerce Commission** (instructions on IRD's page).

## Signs your myIR is being misused

IRD's [signs of a scam page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/scams/signs-of-a-scam) lists what to look for in your account:

- a **bank account or address** in myIR that isn't yours;
- a **notice of assessment** for a return you **didn't file**;
- an unfiled return showing as **filed**.

If you use a **tax agent**, ask them first whether they changed anything. If they didn't, follow the steps above.

## How to tell a real IRD message from a fake

| Real IRD | Scam |
|---|---|
| Texts come from **5678 or 4478** | Any other number |
| **No refund links or amounts** in emails or texts; log in to myIR yourself | "You're owed $412.80, claim here" |
| Sites: **ird.govt.nz**, **myir.govt.nz** | Lookalike addresses |
| "We will **never ask for your password**" | Asks for your myIR password |
| Payment only through official methods | Gift cards, Bitcoin, money transfers |
| Social media: **Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube** only | DMs asking for details; IRD has no X (Twitter) account |

IRD also says it never uses phrases like "**dear citizen**" or "**NZ Govt Tax Refund**". If you see those, you can stop reading.

## If you got a suspicious message but didn't click

Report it via IRD's **"Let us know about a scam"** page. Done.

## How IRD numbers leak

The usual suspects: **fake refund emails** linking to a copy of myIR (Kiwibank flagged one in September 2026), **fake job onboarding forms**, **phishing texts**, and **data breaches**. Our [[nz-ird-tax-refund-scam-surge|IRD refund scam guide]] has examples.

Tax refunds are the scammer's favourite bait because nobody ever thinks "free money from IRD" sounds suspicious. It should.

## Extra protection steps

- **Change your myIR password** and any reused passwords.
- **Check bank details** on your IRD account haven't been changed.
- **Suppress your credit file** if ID documents also leaked ([[nz-credit-suppression-how-to|how]]).
- **Check your credit report** for new accounts ([[nz-free-credit-report-how-to|how]]).
- Report losses to **Police** (105) and **Netsafe**.

=== FAQ
Q: What number do real IRD texts come from?
A: IRD says its texts to New Zealand mobiles come from 5678 or 4478. Texts from other numbers are likely scams.
Q: How can I tell if someone is using my myIR account?
A: Look for a bank account or address that isn't yours, a notice of assessment for a return you didn't file, or a return marked filed that you didn't submit.
Q: What should I do if my IRD number is stolen?
A: Call Inland Revenue so it can verify your identity, set up voice ID, check how your number was used and fix any false returns. Then contact IDCARE.
Q: What is IDCARE's NZ phone number?
A: 0800 121 068, 10am to 7pm, Monday to Friday.
Q: What if I received an IRD scam message but didn't click?
A: Report it through IRD's "Let us know about a scam" page.
Q: Can IRD protect my account after a scam?
A: IRD can set up voice ID to access and protect your account.
