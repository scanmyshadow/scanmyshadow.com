=== TLDR
A WhatsApp message with your bank's logo says there's **suspicious activity** and you need to act **now**. It's a scam. **ASB** says it **never** contacts you via **WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud**. **ANZ** warned of **WhatsApp impersonation** in March 2026. **Kiwibank** says WhatsApp scams are **increasing**. **NZ Post** says it **doesn't use WhatsApp** at all. Don't reply. Call your bank on the number on your card.

=== BODY
## What the banks say

| Organisation | What it says |
|---|---|
| **ASB** | Will never contact you through **WhatsApp, Google Meet or iCloud** ([ASB](https://www.asb.co.nz/banking-with-asb/security-alerts.html)) |
| **ANZ NZ** | Warned of **ANZ impersonation WhatsApp scams** (March 2026) ([ANZ](https://www.anz.co.nz/banking-with-anz/banking-safely/recognise-scams-fraud/latest-scams/)) |
| **Kiwibank** | WhatsApp and messaging app scams are **increasing**, using the logo and urgent "compromised account" claims ([Kiwibank](https://www.kiwibank.co.nz/help/fraud-scams/latest-scams)) |
| **NZ Post** | "NZ Post does not use WhatsApp as a communication channel" ([NZ Post](https://www.nzpost.co.nz/contact-support/scams-and-fraud)) |

## What the scam looks like

- A **bank logo** as the profile picture
- "**Suspicious activity**" or "**your account is compromised**"
- **Urgency**: act in the next few minutes
- A request for **login details, codes**, a **call** or a **video meeting** (Google Meet) so they can "help"
- Sometimes a request to change **phone settings** or install **remote access** (ASB warns about this)

A logo on WhatsApp is just a picture. Anyone can set one. It proves about as much as a fake moustache.

## Why WhatsApp?

It's encrypted, free, international, and feels personal. Scammers can message from overseas while looking official, and the chat feels more private than a text.

## What to do

1. **Don't reply** or click anything.
2. **Block and report** the account in WhatsApp.
3. **Call your bank** on the number on your card or its official site:
   - ANZ **0800 269 296**
   - ASB **0800 272 372**
   - Kiwibank **0800 113 355**
4. If you shared anything, tell the bank immediately and report to Police (105) and Netsafe.

Also watch for fake **Caller Check** messages on WhatsApp ([[nz-bank-caller-check-impersonation-scams|explained]]). A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your number is in breach data.

=== FAQ
Q: Do NZ banks contact customers on WhatsApp?
A: ASB says it never contacts you via WhatsApp. ANZ and Kiwibank have warned about WhatsApp impersonation scams.
Q: Does NZ Post use WhatsApp?
A: No. NZ Post says it doesn't use WhatsApp as a communication channel.
Q: What should I do with a bank WhatsApp message?
A: Don't reply, block and report it, and call your bank on the number on your card.
