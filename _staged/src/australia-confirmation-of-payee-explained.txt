=== TLDR
**Confirmation of Payee (CoP)** checks the **name** you type against the **BSB and account number** before a transfer. You'll see **Match**, **Close match** or **No match** (plus "account closed" or "not found"). It started in **July 2025**, now covers **100+ institutions**, and was used **150 million times** in its first year. It's a **warning, not a block**. And CoP **never contacts you**, so any message claiming to be from it is a scam.

=== BODY
## How it works

From the [Australian Banking Association](https://www.ausbanking.org.au/scam-safe-accord/confirmation-of-payee/):

| Result | What it means | Shows name? |
|---|---|---|
| **Match** | Name and account details agree | Yes |
| **Close match** | Similar but not exact ("John Smyth" vs "John Smith") | Yes |
| **No match** | Name doesn't fit the account | No (for personal accounts) |
| **Other** | Account closed, not found, or check couldn't run | — |

## Rollout

- Started **July 2025**; now live nationally at participating banks.
- **100+** banks, credit unions and mutuals.
- **150 million+** uses in year one; **8 in 10** Australians have seen or used it.

## CoP vs PayID

- **PayID** lets you pay to a phone number or email.
- **CoP** protects payments to a **BSB and account number**, like paying a tradie from an invoice.

## What to do with a "No match"

**Stop.** Verify the details with the payee using contact details **you already trust**, not ones from the email, text or invoice. That's exactly the gap payment redirection scams exploit: fake invoices with the right business name and the scammer's account number. See [[australia-property-settlement-payment-redirection-scams|property settlement scams]].

A "No match" on a $3,000 invoice is your bank tapping you on the shoulder. Listen to it.

## Innocent close or no matches

They can happen when:

- a business **trades under a different name**;
- someone uses a **nickname**;
- special characters differ ("O'Brien" can be typed "O Brien").

## The limits

- It's a **warning**, not a block. You can still send.
- A scammer can open an account **in a name that matches** what they told you. A "Match" proves the name, not the honesty.
- Money sent to the wrong account can be **hard to recover**.

## A scam to watch

CoP **will never contact you**, ask you to send money or ask for account details. "Confirmation of Payee team" calls or texts are fake.

If you think you've been scammed, call your bank immediately and report to Scamwatch. Banks' new legal duties are explained in our [[australia-scams-prevention-framework-explained|Scams Prevention Framework guide]].

=== FAQ
Q: What is Confirmation of Payee in Australia?
A: A check that compares the account name you enter with the BSB and account number before a transfer goes through.
Q: What should I do if I get a No Match?
A: Verify the payment details with the payee using contact details you already trust before sending.
Q: Does Confirmation of Payee block payments?
A: No. It's a warning; you can still choose to continue.
Q: Will Confirmation of Payee ever contact me?
A: No. Messages claiming to be from Confirmation of Payee are scams.
