=== TLDR
Desjardins says it **never** asks you, by email or text, to reply with your login, password, SIN or other confidential details, and **never** sends you to AccèsD or another site to log in or enter information. Any message that does is fake. Forward phishing emails to **protection@desjardins.com**, forward scam texts through your carrier (7726), and if you gave away information, call Desjardins security at **1-866-335-0338**.

=== BODY
## What Desjardins never does

From Desjardins' own [phishing page](https://www.desjardins.com/en/security/phishing.html), Desjardins never asks you to:

- reply with **login credentials or passwords**;
- reply with your **social insurance number** or other confidential info;
- **go to AccèsD** or another site through a link to log in or enter information.

That last one is the big one. The whole scam depends on a link to a fake AccèsD page. If the message has a login link, you already have your answer.

## What the fake messages look like

Desjardins lists the hooks scammers use:

- **Urgency:** update your account now, pay a tax agency, donate after a tragedy.
- **Gain:** you've won a prize, or money was sent to you.
- **Problem:** something's wrong with your account, your computer or a delivery.

Fake sites often have sloppy design, broken links, typos and web addresses that are *almost* right. "desjardlns" is not a typo you want to discover after typing your password.

## The phone version

Desjardins also warns about callers pretending to be from your financial institution to get you to [give up information or send money](https://www.desjardins.com/en/tips/email-text-phone-fraud-can-tell-fake-message-from-real-one.html). Hang up and call the number you look up yourself, not the one the caller gives you.

## How to report

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Phishing email | Forward to **protection@desjardins.com**, then delete |
| Scam text | Forward to **7726** (your carrier's process) |
| Gave information or money | Desjardins security: **1-866-335-0338** (Canada/US), **514-397-8649** (Montreal) |
| Calling from outside Canada/US | **514-397-4610** (collect) |
| Any fraud | [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/) |

## If you clicked

- **Didn't enter anything?** Report the message, scan your device, change your passwords, watch your accounts.
- **Entered details?** Report it, then **call Desjardins security right away** and contact the authorities.
