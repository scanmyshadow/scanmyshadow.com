=== TLDR
Going ex-directory is free. With BT you can choose “Ex-directory – No calls” (left out of the Phone Book, BT 118500 directory enquiries and BT's online directory) or “Directory Enquiries Only” (only available via directory enquiries). Other providers offer the same; just ask. It stops new directory listings, but you'll still want to remove old copies from people-finder sites like 192.com.

=== BODY
## Why it still matters

Landlines feel old-fashioned, but they're a goldmine for scammers: a name, a number and often an address, all pointing at a household that probably includes an older person. Directory listings feed people-finder sites, and people-finder sites feed scam call lists.

## BT's two options

According to [BT](https://www.bt.com/help/landline/manage-service/how-do-i-sign-up-to-ex-directory-services-), both are free:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| Ex-directory – No calls | Left out of the Phone Book, BT 118500 Directory Enquiries and BT's online directory |
| Directory Enquiries Only | Shared only through directory enquiries, not printed or listed online |

Request it by calling BT through its account and billing contact page. If you're with TalkTalk, Sky, Virgin Media or another provider, ask them for the same; it's a standard request.

## What it doesn't do

- remove your number from people-finder sites that already copied it ([[uk-192-com-remove-your-details|remove 192.com listings]]);
- stop cold calls by itself (register with the [[uk-telephone-preference-service-mailing-preference-service|Telephone Preference Service]] too);
- hide your address if it's on the open electoral register ([[uk-open-electoral-register-opt-out|opt out here]]).

## A tidy-up plan for your landline

1. Go ex-directory with your provider.
2. Register with the TPS.
3. Remove old listings from 192.com and similar sites.
4. Opt out of the open register.
5. Use a call-blocking phone if a relative gets lots of scam calls.

Then check the online side: a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows whether your email has leaked in breaches.

=== FAQ
Q: Does it cost money to go ex-directory?
A: No. BT says both its ex-directory options are free.
Q: What is the difference between ex-directory and Directory Enquiries Only?
A: Ex-directory keeps you out of the Phone Book, directory enquiries and online directory. Directory Enquiries Only shares your number only through directory enquiries.
Q: Will going ex-directory stop scam calls?
A: It reduces new listings but won't stop all calls. Also register with the TPS and remove old listings.
Q: Can I go ex-directory with providers other than BT?
A: Yes. Ask your provider; ex-directory is a standard option.
