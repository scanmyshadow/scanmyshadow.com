=== TLDR
Going ex-directory is free. With BT you can choose “Ex-directory – No calls” (left out of the Phone Book, BT 118500 directory enquiries and BT's online directory) or “Directory Enquiries Only” (only available via directory enquiries). Other providers offer the same; just ask. BT stopped printing the Phone Book in **March 2024** and closed its free online directory on **30 April 2024**, but directory enquiries still exist, so ex-directory still matters. It stops new directory listings; you'll still need to remove old copies from people-finder sites like 192.com.

=== BODY
## Why it still matters

Landlines feel old-fashioned, but they're a goldmine for scammers: a name, a number and often an address, all pointing at a household that probably includes an older person. Directory listings feed people-finder sites, and people-finder sites feed scam call lists.

## Wait, is there still a phone book?

Not really. BT said the **final print run** of the Phone Book ended in **March 2024**, and its free online directory, thephonebook.bt.com, was no longer available after **30 April 2024**, [Yahoo News reported](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/bt-axes-second-free-ditching-224834843.html). BT cited low demand and paper savings. **118 500 directory enquiries** remained for people who can't find a number online; at the time it cost **£1.55 a minute plus a 77p call charge**. [Planet Telecom noted](https://planet.uk/bt-phone-books-have-bit-the-dust-whats-next/) that BT still offers free PDF local number lists through its A-Z directory finder.

So why bother? Because your number and name can still be given out by directory enquiries, and old listings live on in people-finder databases. Going ex-directory closes the official tap.

## BT's two options

According to [BT](https://www.bt.com/help/landline/manage-service/how-do-i-sign-up-to-ex-directory-services-), both are free:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| Ex-directory – No calls | Left out of the Phone Book, BT 118500 Directory Enquiries and BT's online directory |
| Directory Enquiries Only | Shared only through directory enquiries, not printed or listed online |

Request it by calling BT on **0330 123 4150** (8:30am to 5pm, Monday to Friday), per BT's help page. If you're with TalkTalk, Sky, Virgin Media or another provider, ask them for the same; it's a standard request. [UK Phone Book](https://www.ukphonebook.com/news/how-to-go-ex-directory) says many providers remove you for free and it can take **a few days** to process.

## What it doesn't do

- remove your number from people-finder sites that already copied it ([[uk-192-com-remove-your-details|remove 192.com listings]]);
- stop cold calls by itself (register with the [[uk-telephone-preference-service-mailing-preference-service|Telephone Preference Service]] too);
- hide your address if it's on the open electoral register ([[uk-open-electoral-register-opt-out|opt out here]]).

## A tidy-up plan for your landline

1. Go ex-directory with your provider.
2. Register with the TPS.
3. Remove old listings from 192.com and similar sites.
4. Opt out of the open register.
5. Use a call-blocking phone if a relative gets lots of scam calls.

Then check the online side: a free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows whether your email has leaked in breaches.

=== FAQ
Q: Does BT still print the Phone Book?
A: No. BT said its final print run ended in March 2024 and its free online directory closed after 30 April 2024. 118 500 directory enquiries continued.
Q: Does it cost money to go ex-directory?
A: No. BT says both its ex-directory options are free.
Q: What is the difference between ex-directory and Directory Enquiries Only?
A: Ex-directory keeps you out of the Phone Book, directory enquiries and online directory. Directory Enquiries Only shares your number only through directory enquiries.
Q: Will going ex-directory stop scam calls?
A: It reduces new listings but won't stop all calls. Also register with the TPS and remove old listings.
Q: Can I go ex-directory with providers other than BT?
A: Yes. Ask your provider; ex-directory is a standard option.
