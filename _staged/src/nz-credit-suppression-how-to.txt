=== TLDR
New Zealand's version of a credit freeze is a **credit file suppression**. While it's active, credit reporting agencies **can't release your file to lenders without your written consent**, so a fraudster can't easily get credit in your name. Initial periods: **Experian 21 days**, **Centrix 20 days**, **Equifax 10 days**. You can apply to **one agency and ask it to pass the request to the others**, and extend it (even indefinitely) if you're at risk of fraud.

=== BODY
## What a suppression does

From [IDCARE](https://idcare.org/fact-sheets/suppressions-new-zealand), a suppression **"freezes access to your credit file."** Agencies can't disclose your information to credit providers without your written consent, unless the law requires it.

Your **existing** cards and loans aren't affected, unless something is up for renewal (like an expiring card).

## How long it lasts

| Agency | Initial period |
|---|---|
| **Experian** | 21 days |
| **Centrix** | 20 days |
| **Equifax** | 10 days |

Different lengths at each agency. Yes, it's a bit like three clocks in one kitchen all set to different times.

## How to apply

1. Pick one agency and apply online through its **suppression page**, according to [IDCARE](https://idcare.org/fact-sheets/suppressions-new-zealand): **Centrix** (centrix.co.nz), **Equifax** (equifax.co.nz/credit-file-suppression) or **Experian** (experian.co.nz/consumer/credit-file-suppression). Each agency **emails you** once it's in place.
2. **Ask that agency to pass the request** to the other two. [govt.nz](https://www.govt.nz/browse/consumer-rights-and-complaints/debt-and-credit-records/check-your-own-credit-report/) confirms you can apply to one and have it notify the others.
3. Keep the confirmation and note each end date.

## How to extend

- Apply **before** it expires.
- Give a reason, like **identity information stolen** or **compromised in a breach**.
- You'll usually need a **police report number**, a **statutory declaration**, or confirmation from a lender that you're at risk.
- Extensions can be granted **indefinitely** if the agency approves.
- **Centrix:** email or call **0800 236 874**. **Equifax:** same online form. **Experian:** its suppression extension page.

## Releasing it

To let a lender see your file, submit a **written request** naming the organisation.

## A timeline that works

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Apply at one agency and ask it to pass the request on; get your free credit reports so you know your starting point ([[nz-free-credit-report-how-to|how]]) |
| Day 1-2 | Report the incident to **Police** (105) so you have a report number |
| Before day 10 | **Extend at Equifax**, the shortest initial period |
| Before day 20-21 | Extend at **Centrix** and **Experian** |
| Then | Replace compromised ID (driver licence, passport) and diarise a review date |

Because the three agencies run different clocks, the Equifax deadline comes first. Miss it and your file at one agency quietly reopens while the others stay locked.

## Suppression vs Australia's credit ban

New Zealand's suppression works like Australia's **credit ban** (21 days, extendable to 12 months), but NZ extensions **can be indefinite** if the agency approves. See [[australia-credit-ban-how-to-request|how Australia's works]].

## When to suppress

- Your **driver licence or passport** was in a breach (the [[was-i-affected-by-the-latitude-financial-data-breach|Latitude breach]] exposed over a million NZ licence numbers).
- Your **wallet or ID** was stolen.
- You **gave ID to a scammer**.
- Your [[nz-free-credit-report-how-to|credit report]] shows enquiries you don't recognise.

## Get help

**IDCARE** (free) on **0800 121 068**. If an agency mishandles your request, you can raise it with the [Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/). A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows whether your email appears in breach data.

=== FAQ
Q: Can I freeze my credit in New Zealand?
A: Yes. It's called a credit file suppression. Agencies can't release your file to lenders without your consent while it's active.
Q: How long does a credit suppression last in NZ?
A: Initially 21 days with Experian, 20 days with Centrix and 10 days with Equifax. You can apply to extend it.
Q: Do I need to contact all three credit agencies?
A: You can apply to one and ask it to pass the request to the others.
Q: Which agency's suppression runs out first?
A: Equifax's, after 10 days. Extend there first, then Centrix (20 days) and Experian (21 days).
Q: What evidence do I need to extend a suppression?
A: Usually a police report number, a statutory declaration, or confirmation from a lender that you're at risk of fraud.
Q: Does a suppression affect my existing credit cards?
A: No, unless credit is due for renewal.
