=== BODY
## What MyLife is

MyLife builds **"reputation profiles"** with names, ages, addresses, relatives and a so-called reputation score. People usually find theirs by googling their own name and getting an unpleasant surprise.

## What the FTC case found

In December 2021 the [FTC and Department of Justice](https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2021/12/ftc-doj-obtain-ban-negative-option-marketing-21-million-consumers-deceived-background-report) settled *US v. MyLife.com, Inc. and Jeffrey Tinsley*. The complaint alleged that MyLife:

- sold **"teaser" background reports** suggesting people might have **arrest, criminal or sex offender records**, even when no such records were included;
- led some people to believe they or others had criminal records when they had none, or only **minor traffic citations**;
- used those fears to enrol people in **auto-renewing subscriptions**, without clearly disclosing charges, and misrepresented its refund and cancellation policies.

The order **permanently bans** MyLife and Tinsley from offering products with a "negative option" (auto-renewal) feature and from implying a traffic ticket means a criminal record. Tinsley was ordered to pay **$5 million**; MyLife's **$16 million** judgment was partially suspended because it couldn't pay in full. The FTC's Samuel Levine said MyLife "lured consumers into hard-to-cancel negative option subscriptions."

So if a MyLife profile hints at "records" about you, treat it with healthy suspicion.

## Step by step

From [Redact's MyLife guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/mylife-com/remove):

1. On mylife.com, type your name, switch on the **"This is Me"** toggle and search.
2. Click **"View Reputation Profile"** on your listing.
3. Tick some data boxes and the **"This is me – Needed to Edit Public Profile"** box, then click **Continue to Details**.
4. Let the "gathering info" animation finish. Don't close the tab.
5. Copy the **profile URL** from the address bar.
6. Go to **mylife.com/privacyrequest** and enter your state, name and city or ZIP.
7. Enter your email and click **Verify Email**. MyLife sends a **numeric code**, not a link. Enter it.
8. Paste the profile URL, complete the reCAPTCHA and submit.
9. Save the confirmation page and email.

Active work: about 10 to 15 minutes, mostly that animation.

## If nothing happens

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| No "deletion complete" email after several days | Email **privacy@mylife.com** or **membersupport@mylife.com** with your name, the listing URL and the date you submitted |
| Still no response | Redact suggests mentioning the 2021 FTC consent decree |
| California resident | Complain to the [California Privacy Protection Agency](https://cppa.ca.gov/), or use the state's free DROP platform ([[fastpeoplesearch-opt-out-guide|how DROP works]]) |
| Anywhere in the US | Report to the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/) |

## Don't pay to "fix" your score

You don't need to buy anything to remove or correct a profile. If a site suggests otherwise, that's a sales pitch, not a privacy process. And if you were ever charged for a MyLife subscription you didn't knowingly sign up for, contact your card provider.

## Clean up Google afterwards

Old MyLife pages can linger in search results after removal. Google's **Results about you** tool lets you request removal of results showing your phone number, home address or email; see [[remove-yourself-from-google-results-about-you|how to use it]].

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove my MyLife profile?
A: Copy your Reputation Profile URL, then submit it at mylife.com/privacyrequest with your name and location, verifying your email with the numeric code MyLife sends.
Q: How long does MyLife removal take?
A: The request takes about 10 to 15 minutes to submit. Wait several days for a deletion confirmation email.
Q: Do I have to pay MyLife to remove my profile?
A: No. Removal is free.
Q: What was the FTC case against MyLife?
A: In 2021 the FTC and DOJ settled with MyLife and its CEO over teaser reports that implied criminal records and hard-to-cancel subscriptions. The order bans auto-renewal products.
Q: What if MyLife doesn't respond?
A: Email privacy@mylife.com with your details and submission date, and report to the FTC or, in California, the CPPA.
