=== BODY
## What MyLife is

MyLife builds “reputation profiles” with names, ages, addresses, relatives and a so-called reputation score. People find them by googling their own name and getting an unpleasant surprise. In December 2021 the FTC and Department of Justice settled a case with MyLife and its CEO, *US v. MyLife.com, Inc. and Jeffrey Tinsley*, over its marketing practices.

## Step by step

From [Redact's MyLife guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/mylife-com/remove):

1. On mylife.com, type your name, switch on the **“This is Me”** toggle and search.
2. Click **“View Reputation Profile”** on your listing.
3. Tick some data boxes and the **“This is me – Needed to Edit Public Profile”** box, then click **Continue to Details**.
4. Let the “gathering info” animation finish. Don't close the tab.
5. Copy the profile URL from the address bar.
6. Go to **mylife.com/privacyrequest** and enter your state, name and city or ZIP.
7. Enter your email and click **Verify Email**. MyLife sends a **numeric code**, not a link. Enter it.
8. Paste the profile URL, complete the reCAPTCHA and submit.
9. Save the confirmation page and email.

Active work: about 10 to 15 minutes, mostly that animation.

## If nothing happens

Wait several days for a “deletion complete” email. No luck? Email membersupport@mylife.com or privacy@mylife.com with your name, the listing URL and the date you submitted. Redact suggests mentioning the 2021 FTC consent decree. California residents can complain to the [California Privacy Protection Agency](https://cppa.ca.gov/), and anyone can report to the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).

## Don't pay to “fix” your score

You don't need to buy anything to remove or correct a profile. If a site suggests otherwise, that's a sales pitch, not a privacy process.

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove my MyLife profile?
A: Copy your profile URL, then submit a request at mylife.com/privacyrequest and verify your email with the numeric code.
Q: How long does MyLife take?
A: About 10 to 15 minutes to submit; processing can take days. Watch for a deletion confirmation email.
Q: Do I have to pay MyLife to remove my information?
A: No. Opting out is free.
Q: What if MyLife doesn't remove my profile?
A: Email privacy@mylife.com with your details and submission date. California residents can complain to the CPPA; others can report to the FTC.
