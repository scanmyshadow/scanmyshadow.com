=== TLDR
Go to infotracer.com/optout, enter your name, city and state, select the matching record and submit the removal request. You'll get an email confirmation message. InfoTracer sells public-records style reports, so it's worth checking alongside the bigger people-search sites.

=== BODY
## What InfoTracer sells

InfoTracer markets public-records searches: background-style reports that can include addresses, phone numbers, relatives and court or property records. Like most people-search sites, it pulls from public records and other data sources, then charges for the full report.

## Step by step

From [Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually):

1. Go to **infotracer.com/optout/**.
2. Enter your **name, city and state**.
3. **Select the correct record.**
4. **Submit** the removal request and watch for the email confirmation message.

Cloaked doesn't give a timeline, so search again after a week.

## A note on public records

Opting out removes your listing from InfoTracer's site. It doesn't erase the underlying public records (court filings, property deeds) held by government offices. Those are covered in our guides to [[court-records-public-case-filings-search-results|court records in search results]] and [[are-divorce-death-arrest-records-public|which records are public]].

=== FAQ
Q: How do I remove myself from InfoTracer?
A: Go to infotracer.com/optout, enter your name, city and state, select your record and submit.
Q: Does InfoTracer opt-out delete public records?
A: No. It removes the listing from InfoTracer, not the original government records.
Q: Is there a fee?
A: No, opting out is free.
Q: How do I know it worked?
A: Watch for the confirmation email and search for yourself again after about a week.
