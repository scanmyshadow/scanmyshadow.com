=== BODY
## What InfoTracer sells

InfoTracer markets **public-records searches**: background-style reports that can include addresses, phone numbers, relatives and court or property records. According to [Redact's InfoTracer guide](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/infotracer-com/remove), its records are "assembled out of public records" and refresh from public-records feeds, which also means they can match the wrong person.

## Before you start

- Note every **state** you've lived in. Each search is by state, so an old address in another state can mean a separate record.
- Have an **email address** ready. It's optional on the form, but it's your only written record of the request.
- Leave the **city blank** unless your name is common.

## Step by step

From [Redact's August 2026 walkthrough](https://redact.dev/data-brokers/infotracer-com/remove):

1. Go to **infotracer.com/optout** ("Your Privacy Choices") and open the **"Personal Information Opt-Out Requests"** panel.
2. Enter your **first name, last name and state**, then click **Submit**. Nothing is deleted at this stage.
3. Check the results table (**Report ID, Name, Age, Addresses**). Compare age and address history before claiming a record.
4. Tick **"Select Record,"** then click **"Remove Data."**
5. Enter your **email** and submit. Screenshot the next screen: the confirmation disappears once you navigate away.
6. You'll see: "Your request has been received and will be processed within 24-48 hours."

A **slide-to-verify** box sometimes appears after searching. Drag the handle to the end and click Continue.

[Cloaked's manual opt-out guide](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually) describes the same basic route: name, city and state, select the record, submit, then watch for the email.

## If it doesn't work

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Record still there after a week | Email **support@infotracer.com** or **privacy@infopay.com** with your name, the Report ID and the date you submitted |
| Form not loading | Redact found the form broken from May 2026 until its 11 August 2026 walkthrough. Try again or use the email route |
| Record came back | Redact notes BBB complaints about records returning; resubmit and recheck quarterly |
| Several records | Repeat steps 3 to 5 for each one |

Phone fallback: (800) 791-1427.

## The sister sites

Redact describes InfoTracer as part of an **InfoPay/Accucom** group of sites. Each runs its own opt-out, so removing InfoTracer doesn't remove these:

- CourtCaseFinder
- StateCourts.org
- ThePublicIndex
- CourtRecords.us
- StateRecords
- RecordsFinder

If you found yourself on InfoTracer, search these too.

## Use your state privacy rights if needed

If you live in a state with a comprehensive privacy law, you can also send a formal deletion request. Redact notes these run on a **45-day** clock. Californians can use the state's free **DROP** platform to send one request to every registered data broker ([[fastpeoplesearch-opt-out-guide|how DROP works]]), and see our [[us-state-privacy-laws-guide|state privacy laws guide]].

## A note on public records

Opting out removes your listing from InfoTracer's site. It doesn't erase the underlying public records (court filings, property deeds) held by government offices. See our guides to [[court-records-public-case-filings-search-results|court records in search results]] and [[are-divorce-death-arrest-records-public|which records are public]]. === FAQ
Q: How do I opt out of InfoTracer?
A: Go to infotracer.com/optout, open Personal Information Opt-Out Requests, search your name and state, select your record, click Remove Data and add your email for confirmation.
Q: How long does InfoTracer removal take?
A: InfoTracer's confirmation screen says requests are processed within 24 to 48 hours.
Q: Is the InfoTracer opt-out free?
A: Yes. It's free and doesn't require an account.
Q: Does removing InfoTracer remove me from CourtCaseFinder or StateRecords?
A: No. Redact says sister sites such as CourtCaseFinder, StateRecords and RecordsFinder each run their own opt-out.
Q: Who do I contact if my InfoTracer record is still there?
A: Email support@infotracer.com or privacy@infopay.com with your name, the Report ID and your submission date.
Q: Does opting out delete my public records?
A: No. It removes the InfoTracer listing, not the original court or property records held by government offices.
