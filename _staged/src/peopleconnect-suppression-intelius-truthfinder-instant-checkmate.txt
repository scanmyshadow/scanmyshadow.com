=== TLDR
Intelius, TruthFinder, Instant Checkmate and US Search are all owned by PeopleConnect. Its suppression centre at suppression.peopleconnect.us lets you verify your email, enter your date of birth and legal name, pick your record, confirm a code and switch it from “Displayed” to “Suppressed.” Double-check your date of birth before submitting: it locks.

=== BODY
## Why one company matters

Four brands, one owner. According to [DuckDuckGo's TruthFinder guide](https://duckduckgo.com/duckduckgo-help-pages/truthfinder-opt-out), PeopleConnect owns TruthFinder, Intelius, US Search and Instant Checkmate, and a suppression request through its tool can reduce republishing across those sites. It's the closest thing the people-search world has to a bulk discount.

## Step by step

1. **Optional: find your listing first.** TruthFinder's search is slow; DuckDuckGo suggests a direct URL like truthfinder.com/results?firstName=jane&lastName=doe&state=CA.
2. **Go to [suppression.peopleconnect.us/login](https://suppression.peopleconnect.us/login).** Enter your email, accept the terms and continue. Click the verification link in the email.
3. **Enter your date of birth and legal name.** First, middle and last. Check the date carefully: [Cloaked notes](https://help.cloaked.com/en/articles/15197669-how-to-opt-out-of-peopleconnect-radaris-beenverified-spokeo-and-other-brokers-manually) it locks after submission.
4. **Select your record.**
5. **Verify identity** with a code sent to a phone number or email you can access.
6. **Switch the record to “Suppressed”** under Desired Behavior and save. “Toggling to Suppressed will hide that personal information from public view.”

## Time and results

About 15 to 30 minutes of work, and processing usually finishes within an hour. Because these sites refresh often, check back every few months.

## What it doesn't cover

PeopleConnect's tool covers its own brands only. You'll still need separate requests for Spokeo, Whitepages, BeenVerified, Radaris and the free sites. Our [[us-data-broker-sites-opt-out-guide|US broker list]] runs through them in order of impact.

## A note on handing over your birth date

Yes, you give a data broker your date of birth to stop it showing your data. That's the system we have. It's used to match records, and suppression hides rather than deletes. If you live in a state with a privacy law, you may also be able to request deletion; see our [[us-state-privacy-laws-guide|state privacy laws guide]].
