=== TLDR
On **October 14, 2026**, Saskatchewan RCMP warned of an email that claims you are **"the subject of a cybercrime investigation"** and threatens arrest unless you contact "investigators" at a **Gmail address** (carcmpgrca@gmail.com). The RCMP does not run investigations out of Gmail. Don't reply, don't click, don't send money or gift cards. If you're unsure, call your local detachment through 310-RCMP.

=== BODY
## The email

According to [Saskatchewan RCMP](https://rcmp.ca/en/saskatchewan/news/2026/10/4357840), the message:

- says you're **under a cybercrime investigation**;
- **threatens arrest** if you don't respond;
- tells you to contact "investigators" at **carcmpgrca@gmail.com**.

It's one of several police-themed scams the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre tracks.

## The giveaway

A national police force with a full IT department would not hand out a free Gmail address. That alone settles it. If a "federal investigator" emails from the same kind of account your nephew uses for Fortnite, it's not federal.

Other red flags common to these emails:

- vague accusations with no case details you can check;
- urgency ("respond within 24 hours or a warrant will be issued");
- a push toward payment, often gift cards or crypto, to "close the file."

## What the RCMP says to do

- **Don't reply** to the email.
- **Don't click** any links.
- **Don't send** personal information.
- **Don't send money or gift cards.**
- If you're unsure, call your local RCMP detachment through **310-RCMP**.
- Report it to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/) online or at **1-888-495-8501**.

## If you already replied

- Stop all contact.
- If you shared ID or banking details, call your bank and consider a [[canada-equifax-transunion-fraud-alert-guide|fraud alert]].
- If you sent your SIN, read [[canada-sin-stolen-what-to-do|what to do if your SIN is stolen]].
- Change your email password and turn on two-factor authentication.

## How did they get my email?

Mass scam emails go to addresses pulled from data breaches. A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|email exposure check]] shows which breaches your address is in, which explains a lot about your inbox.
