=== TLDR
In **July 2026**, fake **QR code stickers** appeared on parking meters across **Christchurch**, with meter screens **scribbled over** so drivers couldn't pay normally. By **September**, they'd spread to **Auckland** and **Wellington**. The codes led to **fake payment sites** run by offshore scammers to steal card details. **Christchurch City Council** says it "would never prompt payment through QR codes," and **Auckland Transport** doesn't use QR codes on any meters. Pay at the machine or in the official app.

=== BODY
## What happened

**Christchurch, July 2026.** [1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/07/14/fake-qr-code-payment-stickers-found-on-christchurch-parking-meters/) reported stickers on council and private meters. Screens were **vandalised with permanent marker** to stop people paying at the machine and push them to scan. Head of transport Stephen Wright said the council "would never prompt payment through QR codes."

**Auckland, September 2026.** [Auckland Transport](https://at.govt.nz/about-us/news-events/media-centre/2026-media-releases/aucklanders-warned-about-scam-parking-meter-qr-codes) found stickers on city-centre meters, with similar finds in Wellington and Christchurch. AT "does not use QR codes on any of our parking meters or machines." Police said the sites are run by **offshore scam operations**.

The **NCSC** flagged it in its [quarterly report](https://www.ncsc.govt.nz/insights-and-research/insights-reports/quarter-two-cyber-security-insights-2026/qr-codes-think-before-you-scan).

The marker-pen detail is the clever bit. Break the honest option, then offer the dishonest one as the "fix."

## How to pay for parking safely

- **Auckland:** the **AT Park app**, or card at the machine.
- **Christchurch:** the payment method **shown on the meter**.
- Anywhere: the council's official app or the machine itself.

## NCSC's QR tips

- **Check the web address** after scanning. Unusual? Don't enter anything.
- Be wary of codes that look **stuck on** or **over** an existing code.
- Be careful with codes tied to **urgent** requests.
- Tell friends and family.

## If you scanned and paid

1. **Call your bank** and cancel the card.
2. Report to **Police** (105.police.govt.nz) and your **council** or AT.
3. Report to the **NCSC**.
