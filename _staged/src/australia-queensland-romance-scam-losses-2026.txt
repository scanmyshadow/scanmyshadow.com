=== TLDR
The Queensland Government said in **February 2026** that more than **850 Queenslanders** reported losing close to **$6 million** to dating and romance scams over a year, with about **a quarter in February** alone. In one February, **61 Queenslanders lost $1.4 million+**, about **four times NSW** ($296,000) and over **three times Victoria** ($380,000). People aged **55–64** were most likely to be scammed, with a **median loss of $7,000**.

=== BODY
## The numbers

Top contact methods: **websites, emails and text messages**. And the government says victims likely **under-report** out of embarrassment.

Why February? Valentine's Day. Scammers time the love-bombing to land when people are most hopeful. It's cynical, and it works.

## Warning signs

- Someone you've **never met in person** asks for **money**
- The relationship **moves fast**
- Pressure to **move to another messaging app**
- **Investment** suggestions, often **cryptocurrency**
- **Urgent** money requests
- **Secrecy** and **isolation** from friends and family
- Endless excuses for **not meeting or video chatting**

If three or more of those sound familiar, step back and talk to someone you trust.

## What to do

1. **Stop sending money.** Don't send "one last payment" to unlock earlier ones.
2. **Contact your bank** immediately.
3. **Report** to police and **Scamwatch**.
4. Get support from **IDCARE** (1800 595 160) if you shared personal info.
