=== BODY
## What Which? found

[Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/the-latest-scam-alerts-from-which-aBRLy2b02WkC) reported on July 29, 2026 that ads on Facebook and Instagram promote fake apps dressed up as Google Authenticator, Disney+, Duolingo and Airbnb. Tap through and you end up at unlicensed online casinos asking for deposits. You came for language lessons and left with a slot machine.

## How the trick works

[Malwarebytes' March 2026 report](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/scams/2026/03/friendlydealer-mimics-official-app-stores-to-push-unvetted-gambling-apps) on a kit it calls **FriendlyDealer** explains the mechanics:

- Ads lead to **fake Google Play or App Store pages**, complete with fake reviews.
- The “Install” button creates a **web app** (PWA) on your home screen rather than a real app from the store.
- The “app” redirects to casino offers, and operators earn affiliate commission when you sign up or deposit.
- At least **1,500 domains** and **20+ casino brands** were generated from one configuration file.
- The kit collects browser data and can send **push notifications** after you close it.

Malwarebytes says this campaign doesn't steal passwords or install traditional malware, but it pushes people into **unregulated gambling** with no age checks, deposit limits or consumer protection.

## Why “unlicensed” matters

UK-licensed gambling sites must verify age, offer safer-gambling tools and follow complaint rules. An unlicensed site can refuse withdrawals, change terms or vanish. Before depositing anywhere, check the operator on the Gambling Commission's public register.

## How to remove a fake app

**Android**: uninstall the app, clear the site's data in Chrome (and any other browser), and remove notification permissions.

**iPhone**: delete the home-screen icon, clear the site's website data in Safari settings and deny notifications.

If you deposited money, contact your bank or card provider promptly. Report the ad to Facebook or Instagram using the three dots on the ad.

## Spot the fake next time

- Real app downloads open the actual Play Store or App Store app, not a web page.
- Big brands advertise their own apps from their own pages.
- If an “authenticator” app starts talking about spins, you have your answer.
