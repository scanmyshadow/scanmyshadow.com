=== TLDR
Fake Scotiabank emails and texts say your account is locked or needs verifying and send you to a lookalike login page. Scotiabank says it won't send unsolicited emails asking for passwords, PINs, access codes or card numbers, and won't ask you to restore account access by email or text. Forward emails (unchanged subject line) or text screenshots to phishing@scotiabank.com, and call 1-866-625-0561 if you think you've been defrauded.

=== BODY
## What the fakes look like

They follow a few patterns:

- **Account locked**: restore access through a link.
- **Unusual sign-in**: verify your identity now.
- **Rewards expiring**: points or a reward about to lapse.

They're built to get your login onto a fake page, or to start a phone call with a fake fraud agent.

## How big the problem is in Canada

The [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/features-vedette/2026/02/top-fraud-2025-fraudes-plus-courantes-eng.htm) received over **112,000 fraud reports** in 2025, with more than **$704 million** in reported losses. Its top 10 included:

| Fraud type (2025) | Reports | Victims | Losses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing | 2,869 | 467 | not counted |
| **Spear phishing** (targeted) | 813 | 571 | **$67.9M** |
| **Fake "fraud investigator"** calls | 2,167 | 1,137 | **$28.3M** |
| Identity fraud | 8,403 | 8,403 | not counted |

The fake investigator line is the one to remember. Scam texts and calls often work as a pair: the message primes you, then a caller posing as "bank security" talks you into acting. If a call follows a suspicious text, hang up and dial the number on the back of your card. See how one [[canada-barrie-fake-scotiabank-investigator-link-56000-lost|fake Scotiabank investigator took $56,000]].

## What Scotiabank says it won't do

From [Scotiabank's reporting page](https://www.scotiabank.com/ca/en/about/contact-us/security/report-an-incident-or-suspicious-activity.html):

- send unsolicited emails asking for your **password, PIN, access code, or card or account numbers**;
- ask you to **validate or restore account access** through email or text.

## How to report

**Emails**: forward to **phishing@scotiabank.com**. Scotiabank asks you not to change or retype the subject line, because that makes investigation harder. Then delete it. Don't forward it to friends.

**Texts**: take a **screenshot** and send it to **phishing@scotiabank.com**, then delete the text. You can also forward the text to 7726.

**Calls**: hang up and dial the number on the back of your card.

## If you think you've been defrauded

Call Scotiabank immediately on **1-866-625-0561**. Change your password, check recent transactions and e-Transfer recipients, and report to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/).
