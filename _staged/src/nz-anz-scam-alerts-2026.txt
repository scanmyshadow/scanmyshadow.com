## The Caller Check fake (July 2026)

From [ANZ](https://www.anz.co.nz/banking-with-anz/banking-safely/recognise-scams-fraud/latest-scams/july-2026-anz-impersonation-scams/): scammers cold call pretending to be ANZ and send fake **"security verification"** messages by **email, SMS or WhatsApp**, claiming that's how Caller Check works.

**Real Caller Check** is a secure notification **only in the ANZ goMoney app**. ANZ never sends it by email, SMS, WhatsApp or other messaging. "If you receive one anywhere other than goMoney, it's a scam."

The security feature became the costume. Clever, but beatable once you know where the real one lives.

## What ANZ will never do

- Ask for your **banking PINs, passwords or security codes**
- Send you a **link to log in** to Internet Banking (type anz.co.nz)
- Ask you to **download software**
- Ask for **remote access**

=== FAQ
Q: Does ANZ NZ send Caller Check requests by text or WhatsApp?
A: No. Caller Check is only sent to the ANZ goMoney app. Anywhere else, it's a scam.
Q: Will ANZ send me a link to log in?
A: No. ANZ says it never sends a link to log on to Internet Banking.
Q: What is ANZ NZ's fraud number?
A: 0800 269 296, or +64 4 470 3142 from overseas.
Q: Does ANZ contact customers on WhatsApp?
A: ANZ warned in March 2026 about scammers impersonating ANZ on WhatsApp.
