=== TLDR
ANZ NZ has posted eight scam alerts in 2026. The newest (September) describe **fake direct-debit emails** that give you a "support" number, **texts from numbers ANZ has used before** with links to fake login pages, and callers posing as the **ANZ Fraud team** who ask for codes, remote access, "safe account" transfers or even your **card and PIN in an envelope** for a courier. Real **Caller Check** only appears in the **goMoney** app. ANZ will never ask for your **OnlineCode, Visa Secure code, PIN or password**, and never asks for **remote access**. Call **0800 269 296** (overseas **+64 4 470 3142**).

## September 2026: staff impersonation, three ways

According to [ANZ's September alert](https://www.anz.co.nz/banking-with-anz/banking-safely/recognise-scams-fraud/latest-scams/september-2026-anz-impersonation-scam/), scammers pose as ANZ staff by email, text and phone:

| Channel | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Says a **direct debit** has been set up on your account and gives a phone number. The number plays an **automated recording**, then connects you to a scammer posing as ANZ. Other emails copy real ANZ emails with the links swapped for fake ones. |
| **Text** | Often arrives from a **number ANZ has used before**, urging you to click a link to "verify" your account or respond to suspected fraud. The link goes to a fake login page. |
| **Phone** | Callers claim to be the **ANZ Fraud team** and may already know some of your details. |

### What they ask for

- money transfers, including to a **"safe" account**;
- your Internet Banking login, **OnlineCode** or **Visa Secure** code;
- card details, PIN or passwords;
- your **cards and PIN in an envelope** for a courier to collect;
- **remote access** to your device;
- **gift cards** or new **crypto accounts**;
- a click on a fake **live chat** link.

The recorded message is a clever touch: it makes a scam line sound like a real call centre. A real bank number on a real ANZ statement doesn't need one.

## July 2026: the fake Caller Check

From [ANZ](https://www.anz.co.nz/banking-with-anz/banking-safely/recognise-scams-fraud/latest-scams/july-2026-anz-impersonation-scams/): scammers cold call pretending to be ANZ and send fake **"security verification"** messages by **email, SMS or WhatsApp**, claiming that's how Caller Check works.

**Real Caller Check** is a secure notification **only in the ANZ goMoney app**. ANZ never sends it by email, SMS, WhatsApp or other messaging. "If you receive one anywhere other than goMoney, it's a scam." The security feature became the costume. Clever, but beatable once you know where the real one lives. More in our guide to [[nz-bank-caller-check-impersonation-scams|Caller Check scams]].

## What ANZ will never do

- Ask for your **OnlineCode, Visa Secure code, PIN, password or card details** by email, text or phone
- Send you a **link to log in** to Internet Banking (type anz.co.nz yourself)
- Ask you to **download software** or give **remote access**
- Ask you to move money to a **"safe account"**

ANZ says its fraud team may call to check unusual transactions, "however we will never ask you for remote access."

## The bigger picture

Bank impersonation is growing across NZ. In October 2026, [ASB cited Netsafe data](https://www.mpamag.com/nz/news/general/asb-warns-customers-as-bank-impersonation-scams-rise-in-new-zealand/594702) showing **34%** of August 2026 impersonation reports involved someone posing as a bank or financial institution, up from **20%** between April and July. ASB also warned about scammers asking people to key in a code that turns on **call forwarding**; see our [[nz-asb-scam-alerts-2026|ASB alerts]].

2. Change your Internet Banking password from a clean device.

=== FAQ
Q: Does ANZ NZ send Caller Check requests by text or WhatsApp?
A: No. Caller Check is only sent to the ANZ goMoney app. Anywhere else, it's a scam.
Q: I got an email saying a direct debit was set up on my ANZ account. Is it real?
A: ANZ warned in September 2026 about fake direct-debit emails that give a phone number leading to scammers. Don't call it; check your account in goMoney or call 0800 269 296.
Q: Will ANZ send me a link to log in?
A: No. ANZ says it never sends a link to log on to Internet Banking.
Q: Will ANZ ever send a courier for my card?
A: ANZ lists requests for your cards and PIN in an envelope for courier pickup as a scam tactic.
Q: What is ANZ NZ's fraud number?
A: 0800 269 296, or +64 4 470 3142 from overseas.
