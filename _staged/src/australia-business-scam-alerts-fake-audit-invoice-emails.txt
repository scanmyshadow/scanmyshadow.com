=== TLDR
In **September 2026**, NAB warned of **fake audit emails** that ask businesses for **invoice details** for a "**credit review**" or "**internal audit**", sometimes styled as a forwarded email from a **NAB banker**. In **July 2026**, Westpac warned that a business called "**Westpac-Group Australia**" has **no link** to Westpac. Both feed **invoice and payment redirection fraud**, which cost Australians **$166.8 million** in 2025. Verify any request by phone with a number you already have.

=== BODY
## The fake audit email

From [NAB's alerts](https://www.nab.com.au/about-us/security/latest-fraud-scam-alerts) (September 2026):

- Emails ask businesses for **invoice details** as part of a "**credit review**" or "**internal audit**".
- Some pose as a **forwarded message from a NAB banker**.
- **Don't respond.** Verify with your banker first.

Why invoices? Because with real invoices, a scammer learns **who you pay, how much and when**. Next comes a perfectly timed "**our bank details have changed**" email from your "supplier."

## The look-alike company

[Westpac](https://www.westpac.com.au/security/latest-scams/) (July 2026): "**Westpac-Group Australia**" is **not affiliated** with Westpac and **isn't authorised** to use its name. **Verify any payment requests independently.**

A hyphen and the word "Group" are all it takes to look official. It's the corporate version of a knock-off handbag.

## The bigger picture

Payment redirection scams cost Australians **$166.8 million in 2025**, per National Anti-Scam Centre data cited by [CommBank](https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2026/09/property-settlement-scam-risk-rising.html). Businesses are prime targets because they pay large invoices routinely.

## Controls that work

1. **Never send invoices or customer lists** in response to unexpected "audit" requests. Call your banker on a number you already have.
2. **Verify every bank detail change by phone**, using a saved number, not one from the email.
3. **Use Confirmation of Payee**: a "No match" on a supplier payment is a red flag ([[australia-confirmation-of-payee-explained|how it works]]).
4. **Two-person approval** for new payees or changed details.
5. **Turn on MFA** for all business email accounts. A hacked inbox is how most of this starts.
6. **Train staff** to pause on urgency.

## If you've paid a fake invoice

- Call your bank **immediately** to try a recall.
- Report to **ReportCyber** and Scamwatch.
- Check your email for **forwarding rules** you didn't create.
