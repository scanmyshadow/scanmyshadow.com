=== TLDR
From **1 April to 30 June 2026**, New Zealand's **NCSC** recorded **1,129** incidents (vs 1,164 in Q1). Reported **direct financial loss** was **$2.7 million**, **down 52%** from $5.6M. **Scams and fraud** was the biggest category (**348 incidents**, ~$860K), but **unauthorised access** caused about **$1.3M**, half the total. **49 big incidents** ($10K+) made up **91%** of losses. Trends: **info-stealing malware scams** and **QR code phishing** on parking meters.

=== BODY
## The headline numbers

From the [NCSC's Q2 2026 insights](https://www.ncsc.govt.nz/insights-and-research/insights-reports/quarter-two-cyber-security-insights-2026):

| Metric | Q2 2026 |
|---|---|
| Total incidents | **1,129** (Q1: 1,164) |
| Triaged for specialist support | **92** (up ~19–20% from 77) |
| Direct financial loss | **$2.7M** (down 52% from $5.6M) |
| Incidents with a loss | 303 |
| Incidents ≥ $10,000 | **49**, accounting for **$2.4M (91%)** |

## What kind of incidents

| Category | Incidents | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| **Scams and fraud** | **348** | ~$860K |
| Phishing and credential harvesting | Second most common | — |
| **Unauthorised access** | — | ~**$1.3M** (about half) |

So scams are the most **common**; account break-ins are the most **costly**.

## Who's behind the serious ones

Of the 92 specialist-support incidents: **23%** likely state-sponsored, **37%** likely cybercrime, **40%** couldn't be attributed.

## Two trends to know

**1. Malware scams.** More Kiwis reported scams that trick them into downloading **information-stealing malware**. Example: fake job ads that make you install an app ([[nz-fake-job-ads-app-malware-scam|how it works]]).

**2. QR code phishing.** Fake QR codes on **Christchurch parking meters** sent people to payment-stealing sites ([[nz-parking-meter-qr-code-scam|full story]]).

## A note on "losses down 52%"

Reported losses swing a lot quarter to quarter because a handful of big cases dominate (91% from 49 incidents). One bad week for one business can double the number. It's not a sign scammers took a holiday.

## What to do

- Turn on **MFA** everywhere. Unauthorised access caused the most loss.
- Only install apps from **official app stores**.
- Check the web address after **scanning any QR code**.
- Report incidents to the NCSC and scams to **Netsafe** ([[nz-how-to-report-a-scam|reporting guide]]).
