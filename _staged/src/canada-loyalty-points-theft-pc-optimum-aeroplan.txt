=== TLDR
Loyalty accounts are easy money for thieves: often weak passwords, no 2FA, and owners who don't check for months. Two Ontario Aeroplan members lost about **$13,000** in points and neither got them back, partly because they reported too late. An Ottawa man lost **200,000** points overnight. PC Optimum members were hit after the 2018 program merger. Fix: unique password, 2FA, a secure email account, and **check your balance monthly**.

=== BODY
## Real Canadian cases

**Aeroplan, Ontario.** [CP24/CTV reported](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2025/06/12/two-aeroplan-members-lose-13000-in-points-after-getting-hacked) that a Toronto member lost **417,500 points (about $8,000)** and a Markham member lost **245,000 points (about $5,000)**. Thieves used the points to buy **gift cards**. Air Canada said the delay made investigation impossible, and Aeroplan's terms say members must report concerns immediately and monitor their own activity. Neither member got the points back.

**Aeroplan, Ottawa.** An Ottawa man told [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/ottawa/article/ottawa-mans-200000-aeroplan-points-stolen-as-he-slept/) hackers drained about **200,000 points** in minutes while he slept.

**PC Optimum.** After Loblaw merged PC Plus and Shoppers Optimum in 2018, members reported their points stolen. [Maclean's](https://macleans.ca/economy/business/how-to-protect-your-pc-optimum-points-from-theft) reported Loblaw locked some accounts, asked members to reset passwords and committed to reinstating points.

## Why points are a target

- **Points are cash** that's easy to turn into gift cards.
- **Passwords are reused.** Loyalty accounts often share a password with something that's already been breached.
- **Nobody checks.** The Toronto member hadn't looked in over a year. Thieves love a customer who's busy living their life.
- **Fewer protections** than a bank: weaker fraud monitoring and, as shown above, tough refund rules.

## How to protect your points

From Rewards Canada (via CP24) and Maclean's:

1. **Use a strong, unique password** for every loyalty account. A [[password-manager-mfa-security-key-guide|password manager]] makes this painless.
2. **Turn on two-factor authentication** wherever offered.
3. **Secure your email.** Whoever controls your email can reset everything else.
4. **Ignore "bonus points" ads and emails** that ask you to log in through a link.
5. **Check your balance at least monthly.** Rewards Canada's founder recommends this. Set a calendar reminder.
6. **Keep the card** with you and shield your PIN at checkout.

## If your points disappear

- **Report it immediately** to the program. Speed is everything under these terms.
- **Change your password** and email password.
- **Keep screenshots** of your balance history.
- **Report to the** [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/) if gift cards or money were involved.

Bonus Aeroplan tip from the same report: points can **expire** if you have no activity for **18 months**. Thieves aren't the only way to lose them.

## Where your password leaked
