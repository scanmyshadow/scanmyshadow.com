=== TLDR
NZ Post says it **never** asks for personal or financial details by **text or email**, never emails from anything but **nzpost.co.nz**, never texts from **overseas numbers** (watch for **+61**), and **doesn't use WhatsApp**. Genuine links go to **nzpost.co.nz** or **nzp.st**. Recent scams: high-volume SMS phishing (**July 2026**), "**select a new delivery date**" texts (**December 2025**), and **WhatsApp** "verify your address" messages (**September 2025**). Forward fakes to **7726**.

=== BODY
## What NZ Post never does

From [NZ Post's scams page](https://www.nzpost.co.nz/contact-support/scams-and-fraud):

- Ask for **personal or financial details** by email or text (passwords, card details, account info)
- Send email from a domain **other than nzpost.co.nz**
- Send texts from a phone number **outside New Zealand**
- Contact you on **WhatsApp**: "NZ Post does not use WhatsApp as a communication channel."

## Real vs fake at a glance

| Real | Fake |
|---|---|
| Emails end in **@nzpost.co.nz** | Odd domains, often ".icu" |
| Links go to **nzpost.co.nz** or **nzp.st** | Look-alike links |
| NZ numbers | **+61** or other overseas numbers |
| No WhatsApp | WhatsApp "verify your address" |

Kiwibank also flagged an NZ Post text scam in August 2026 ([[nz-kiwibank-latest-scams-2026|Kiwibank's list]]).

A small redelivery fee doesn't sound like much. That's the trick: the real prize is your card number.

## How to report

- **Texts:** forward to **7726** ([[nz-report-scam-text-7726|how]]), don't click.
- **Emails:** forward to NZ Post's reporting address (on its scams page), then delete.
- **QR code scams:** Police at **105**.
- **Facebook posts:** report to Facebook.

## If you paid or entered details

Call your bank immediately and report to Police (105) and Netsafe.
