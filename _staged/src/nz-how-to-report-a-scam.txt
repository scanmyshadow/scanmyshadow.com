=== TLDR
**Lost money?** Call your **bank** immediately (number on your card), then **Police** at **105.police.govt.nz** or **105**. **Scam text?** Forward free to **7726**. **Not sure or need support?** **Netsafe** at netsafe.org.nz/report or **0508 638 723**. **Cyber incident or hacked account?** The **NCSC**. **ID stolen?** **IDCARE** on **0800 121 068**. **Danger?** **111**.

=== BODY
## The order matters

If money has left your account, **every minute counts**. Banks can sometimes freeze funds before they're moved on.

| Priority | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Money lost** | **Your bank** | Number on your card (24/7 lines: ANZ 0800 269 296, ASB 0800 272 372, Kiwibank 0800 113 355) |
| **2. Crime report** | **NZ Police** | **105.police.govt.nz** or call **105** |
| **3. Advice and reporting** | **Netsafe** | netsafe.org.nz/report or **0508 638 723** |
| **Scam texts** | **DIA** | Forward to **7726** (free) |
| **Cyber incidents** | **NCSC** | Online reporting form |
| **Identity misuse** | **IDCARE** | **0800 121 068** |
| **Immediate danger** | Police | **111** |

Sources: [Police](https://www.police.govt.nz/news/release/two-charged-following-alleged%C2%A0scam%C2%A0targeting-vulnerable-victim), [Netsafe](https://resource.netsafe.org.nz/Netsafe-scams-update-August-2026.pdf), [MFAT/DIA](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/en/media-and-resources/text-message-scam), [IRD](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/scams/take-action-if-youve-been-scammed).

## Report to both bank and Police

Under the new bank Code, to be considered for **reimbursement** you must report to **your bank and Police within 3 months** of discovering the scam ([[nz-bank-scam-reimbursement-rules-explained|details]]). So don't skip the Police report, even if it feels pointless.

## Specific organisations

- **IRD scams:** IRD's "Let us know about a scam" page ([[nz-ird-number-myir-compromised-what-to-do|IRD guide]]).
- **NZTA texts:** NZTA's phishing form ([[nz-nzta-toll-scam-text-3651|NZTA guide]]).
- **Trade Me:** Community Watch ([[nz-trade-me-phishing-emails-after-sale|Trade Me guide]]).
- **Investment scams:** the **FMA** ([[nz-fma-fake-nz-registered-investment-firms|FMA guide]]).
- **Parking QR stickers:** Police 105 and your council ([[nz-parking-meter-qr-code-scam|QR guide]]).

## Don't be embarrassed

Police note scams aren't always obvious and work by building **urgency or trust**. One recent Bay of Plenty case: a vulnerable person was manipulated into withdrawing cash and handing it over; **two men were charged**. Reports are how cases like that get solved.

## After reporting

- Change passwords you may have exposed.
- Watch for **recovery scams** promising your money back for a fee.
- Consider a [[nz-credit-suppression-how-to|credit suppression]] if ID was shared.

A free [[/digital-footprint-checker/|exposure check]] shows if your details are in breach data.

=== FAQ
Q: Who do I contact first if I've been scammed in NZ?
A: Your bank, immediately, using the number on your card. Then report to Police on 105.
Q: How do I report a scam to NZ Police?
A: Online at 105.police.govt.nz or by calling 105.
Q: What is Netsafe's phone number?
A: 0508 638 723, or report online at netsafe.org.nz/report.
Q: Where do I report scam texts in NZ?
A: Forward them free to 7726.
