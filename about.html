About Scan My Shadow

Built to help you see what's already out there

A digital privacy service operated by Jaishree Exports — a registered Indian business — for everyday Indians who deserve to know what's exposed about them online.

01

Who We Are

Scan My Shadow is a digital privacy service operated by Jaishree Exports, a sole proprietorship registered in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, India.

We help individuals find out if their personal information — phone number and email address — has been exposed in data leaks, breaches, or across the open web. Our reports are written in plain English, delivered in minutes, and built for people who don't have a technical background.

02

What We Do

We scan your phone number and email address against breach databases and open-web sources, and deliver a plain-language Privacy Scan Report to your email address.

No technical knowledge required. No app to download. No login or passwords needed. Submit your details on our secure website form after payment and receive your report by email — usually within about 5 minutes.

Results are clear, practical, and written for real people — not security professionals.

03

Our Commitment

  • Your scan data is deleted immediately after the report is delivered.
  • We do not sell your data, ever.
  • We do not share your information with third parties except as required to perform the scan and deliver your report.
  • Our service surfaces data that already exists on the internet or in breach databases — we do not create, alter, or distribute personal data.
  • Every scan is consent-first. This service is built for scanning your own information, or for someone who has explicitly asked for your help.
